# ICES Northeast Atlantic smooth-hounds, 2025 review

ICES advice unit `sdv.27.nea` is smooth-hounds (*Mustelus* spp.) in the
Northeast Atlantic. The 2025 WGEF report is the latest detailed source located
for the current advice update. It notes an important species boundary: the
fishery-independent survey signal is primarily starry smooth-hound
(*Mustelus asterias*), while commercial data are reported for the genus and
may include other species.

The 2025 assessment applies ICES category-3 `rfb` advice. Its stock-size
indicator combines four standardized fishery-independent survey biomass
series, using fish at least 50 cm total length. The North Sea Q1 and Q3 series
are averaged together; the other series are EVHOE-WIBTS-Q4, FR-CGFS, and
IE-IGFS-WIBTS-Q4. The fishing-pressure proxy is calculated from recent
commercial length data. The assessment does not use catch-at-age, annual age
compositions, or survey numbers-at-age. Although the report summarizes
age-and-growth and maturity-at-length studies, these are not age-structured
inputs to the current rule.

The 2025 report gives the updated survey-based stock-size indicator and
fishing-proxy inputs and advises that landings should be no more than 6,395 t
in each of 2026 and 2027. Those aggregate indicators and advice do not provide
N-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, SSB, or recruitment. The unit is **ineligible
for the age-disaggregated inventory** on the current-assessment evidence
reviewed. The record may still be relevant to a future non-age-structured
database collection. No canonical numerical records or tinyAM translation
were added.

The review uses the 2025 update as the current source and does not treat the
earlier 2023 run as a separate stock unit.
