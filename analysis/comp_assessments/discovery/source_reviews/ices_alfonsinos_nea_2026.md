# ICES alfonsinos (alf.27.nea), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

This is the ICES assessment unit for alfonsinos (*Beryx* spp.) in subareas
1–10, 12 and 14. The latest advice located is ICES record 6154, based on the
2026 assessment and valid for 2027–2028. Stock Assessment Graphs identifies the
assessment as key 22402 and classifies it as Category 3.2.

## Assessment evidence

The 2026 public graph identifies the model as DLS and reports annual landings
in tonnes for 1990–2025, a biomass index in kg per day for 2003–2016 and
2019–2025, and a length-based fishing-pressure proxy. The graph's XML labels
stock size as `B_index`, reports no recruitment age or `Fage` field, and does
not provide age-specific population trajectories. Although the rendered graph
table uses an “SSB” heading, its metadata identifies this series as the
`B_index`; it is not treated here as spawning-stock biomass.

ICES advises limiting landings to 194 tonnes in each of 2027 and 2028 and says
that it cannot quantify the corresponding catches. No catch-at-age, age-specific
survey index, age-specific biology, estimated F-at-age or M-at-age outputs were
recovered for the current method. The available abundance and pressure series
are aggregate/length-based, so they do not meet the age-disaggregated screening
criteria.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. Its
current advice is based on a length-based indicator and aggregate landings and
biomass index, without verified age-structured population inputs or outputs.
No canonical stock, assessment, input or output rows were added.
