# NOAA Main Hawaiian Islands reef-fish assessment (2016)

NOAA Stock SMART lists eight species-specific Main Hawaiian Islands rows in this screen as fully accepted assessments from the 2016 PIFSC reef-fish assessment (published in 2017, data through 2016). The rows are Giant trevally, Twosaddle goatfish, Kumu, Spectacled parrotfish, Bullethead parrotfish, Regal parrotfish, Palenose parrotfish, and Redlip parrotfish. Each is a separate species management unit; the assessment report covers 27 species overall, and this screen does not create new inventory rows for species absent from the starting register.

The official report describes fishery-dependent catch estimates and fishery-independent diver-survey abundance and size-composition data. It estimates mortality and spawning-potential metrics from length-based methods, then uses a relatively simple age-structured population calculation to derive stock-status measures. This distinction matters: the report's age-structured calculation does not establish that catch-at-age, age-composition likelihoods, or age-specific survey indices were observed inputs.

The recoverable evidence supports aggregate catch, diver abundance, size composition, life-history inputs, and species-level mortality/SPR/catch-limit outputs. The public assessment report does not provide the age-specific observation series and comparable annual N-at-age/F-at-age surfaces needed for the current age-disaggregated candidate set. Life-history or age information used to derive growth assumptions is not treated as fishery or survey age composition. The eight records are therefore screened ineligible for the current set, while remaining distinct provisional stock units. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added.
