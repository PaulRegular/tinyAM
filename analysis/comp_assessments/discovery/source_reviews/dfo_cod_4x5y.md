# DFO Atlantic cod in 4X5Y: discovery review

The DFO Sustainability Survey lists Atlantic cod in NAFO 4X5Y. [DFO Science Advisory Report 2026/016](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2026/mpo-dfo/fs70-6/Fs70-6-2026-016-eng.pdf) describes this assessment unit in the Bay of Fundy and Scotian Shelf. No cod record for 4X5Y was found in the canonical stock table. The Canadian Georges Bank cod row covers 5Z, a different unit; the biological components within 4X5Y are assessed together and must not be counted as separate stocks.

The latest detailed numerical source for the production run remains to be established. The 2026 advisory report describes the assessment using data through 2024 and a switch from VPA to the Woods Hole Assessment Model (WHAM), including lobster-fishery bycatch. Its cited WHAM framework is marked in preparation. It identifies commercial catch and the Maritimes summer ecosystem research-vessel survey over 1983-2024. Figure 2 depicts commercial catch and survey abundance at age and estimated natural mortality, but does not provide their numerical tables. Figure 1 reports historical spawning biomass and age-1 abundance with 95% intervals; it states 2025 spawning biomass of 2,666 tonnes and 2024 age-1 abundance of 0.94 million. Verify the full historical numerical outputs, weights, maturity, survey timing and run-specific bycatch before determining eligibility.

The [2018 framework input report, Research Document 2022/006](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/Library/41058203.pdf), is useful for historical context, but its older VPA inputs cannot be assumed to be the 2024-data WHAM run inputs. The 2026/016 advisory report identifies the 2025 assessment framework as still in preparation and says further publications will follow; the current public sources reviewed here do not expose the run-matched age matrices, biology, timing, bycatch setup or full numerical outputs. Screening is complete, but eligibility remains unresolved until a detailed production-run source is available.
