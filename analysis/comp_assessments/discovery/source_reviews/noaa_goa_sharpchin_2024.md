# NOAA Gulf of Alaska Sharpchin Rockfish — 2024 update / 2025 rollover

Reviewed 2026-10-30. NOAA Stock SMART ID 10434 is Sharpchin rockfish (*Sebastes zacentrus*) in the Gulf of Alaska. It has no matching canonical tinyAM stock record. Stock SMART's latest stock-specific metadata row is the fully accepted 2023 Index Method assessment (assessment 13120; REMA Model 15.2; data through 2023). The 2024 GOA SAFE is an operational update to the broader complex; it carries forward the Tier 4 REMA estimates because no new survey biomass was available to refit. Sharpchin is the Tier 4 component of the GOA Other Rockfish complex, not a standalone age-structured population model.

The official 2023 GOA SAFE chapter describes a full operational assessment using aggregate bottom-trawl survey biomass and REMA Model 15.2. Its survey biomass series spans 1990–2023; the report gives Tier 4 reference calculations with M = 0.06 and biomass of 7,008 tonnes for the 2024–2025 specifications. It reports aggregate catch by year and area, but does not use catch numbers-at-age, age-specific survey indices, or age-specific population states in the fitted model. The life-history section discusses sharpchin age and maturity information as biological background and for Tier assignment; those data do not create an age-composition likelihood in the assessment.

The 2024 official GOA SAFE update changes the species composition of the Other Rockfish complex and updates catch through 2024. It says no new GOA survey biomass was available to rerun the Tier 4 REMA model; the sharpchin biomass and reference outputs therefore remain carried forward from the 2023 fit. The accepted method remains aggregate-index REMA Model 15.2. The 2025 final GOA SAFE chapter is a catch report: it explicitly says no assessment was conducted that year because of the federal shutdown, and that the previous assessment values were rolled over. It updates catch through November 8, 2025, not the population assessment.

No annual catch-at-age, age-specific abundance index, weight-at-age, maturity-at-age likelihood input, or estimated age-specific N/F/M output is available for this production assessment. M = 0.06 is a fixed Tier 4 reference-point assumption, not an estimated age-specific mortality process. The 2025 catch report retains aggregate OFL/ABC/TAC and catch only. The unit is screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated source set; no canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added. NOAA Stock SMART ID 10435 is a separate Pacific Coast entry and is not merged with this GOA candidate.
