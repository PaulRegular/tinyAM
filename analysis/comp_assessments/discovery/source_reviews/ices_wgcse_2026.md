# ICES WGCSE 2026 regional screening

The full [2026 Working Group for the Celtic Seas Ecoregion report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32625759.v1) was retrieved from the ICES Figshare record on 2026-10-29 and cached as `source_cache/discovery_ices_wgbie_2026/wgcse_2026_full_report.pdf`. The local `sources.csv` records the direct download URL and SHA-256. The report’s section 1.3 classifies the WGCSE stock methods; the individual stock sections provide the assessment summaries. This review applies only to the current methods described in that report and does not create canonical records.

The report identifies 14 category-1 age-based assessments. These remain subject to stock-specific input, timing, biological and output screening before eligibility can be confirmed. The report classifies 11 other stocks outside the age-structured assessment scope: two megrim stocks use SPiCT (category 2); plaice in 7.e is a category-3 data-limited update and plaice in 7.f-g and 7.h-k use survey trends and length indicators; haddock in 7.a and sole in 7.h-k receive category-5 advice; and seabass 6.a/7.b/7.j, cod 6.b, plaice 7.b-c and sole 7.b-c receive category-6 advice. Those 11 entries are screened ineligible for an age-structured candidate set under the current advice methods. This does not assert that age observations do not exist or that earlier assessments were not age structured.

The reviewed non-age-structured candidates are `lez.27.4a6a`, `lez.27.6b`, `ple.27.7e`, `ple.27.7fg`, `ple.27.7h-k`, `had.27.7a`, `sol.27.7h-k`, `bss.27.6a7bj`, `cod.27.6b`, `ple.27.7bc` and `sol.27.7bc`. Their individual decisions and category evidence are recorded in `candidates.csv`.

Irish Sea plaice (ple.27.7a) has now been screened against its existing 2026 canonical assessment and is eligible as a matched case; see ices_plaice_irish_sea_7a_2026.md. Irish Sea sole (sol.27.7a) has a separate stock-level review in ices_sol_27_7a_2026.md and remains unresolved. The other 12 age-based candidates still need stock-specific screening.
