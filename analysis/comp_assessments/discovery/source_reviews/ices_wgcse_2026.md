# ICES WGCSE 2026 regional screening

The full [2026 Working Group for the Celtic Seas Ecoregion report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32625759.v1) was retrieved from the ICES Figshare record on 2026-10-29 and cached as `source_cache/discovery_ices_wgbie_2026/wgcse_2026_full_report.pdf`. The local `sources.csv` records the direct download URL and SHA-256. The report’s section 1.3 classifies the WGCSE stock methods; the individual stock sections provide the assessment summaries. This review applies only to the current methods described in that report and does not create canonical records.

The report identifies 14 category-1 age-based assessments. Eight now have stock-specific eligible screens against canonical 2025/2026 runs; Irish Sea sole remains unresolved, and five still need stock-specific input, timing, biological and output screening. The report-level category alone does not establish source readiness.
The report classifies 11 other stocks outside the age-structured assessment scope: two megrim stocks use SPiCT (category 2); plaice in 7.e is a category-3 data-limited update and plaice in 7.f-g and 7.h-k use survey trends and length indicators; haddock in 7.a and sole in 7.h-k receive category-5 advice; and seabass 6.a/7.b/7.j, cod 6.b, plaice 7.b-c and sole 7.b-c receive category-6 advice. Those 11 entries are screened ineligible for an age-structured candidate set under the current advice methods. This does not assert that age observations do not exist or that earlier assessments were not age structured.

The reviewed non-age-structured candidates are `lez.27.4a6a`, `lez.27.6b`, `ple.27.7e`, `ple.27.7fg`, `ple.27.7h-k`, `had.27.7a`, `sol.27.7h-k`, `bss.27.6a7bj`, `cod.27.6b`, `ple.27.7bc` and `sol.27.7bc`. Their individual decisions and category evidence are recorded in `candidates.csv`.

Eight of the 14 category-1 assessments are screened eligible as matches to existing canonical stocks: Irish Sea plaice, Irish Sea cod, Celtic Sea cod, Celtic Sea haddock, Rockall haddock, Western Channel sole, Celtic Sea common sole, and West of Scotland whiting. Their source reviews record detailed report evidence and any approximations. Irish Sea sole remains unresolved in ices_sol_27_7a_2026.md. The other five age-based entries (anglerfish, seabass, pollack, and two whiting units) still need stock-specific identity and source screening.
