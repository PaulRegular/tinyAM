# Interior Fraser Coho assessment for 2022

DFO's inventory row maps to the Interior Fraser Coho Stock Management Unit
(SMU), covering the Fraser River watershed upstream of Hells Gate. The SMU
contains five Conservation Units and eleven subpopulations and is genetically
distinct from Lower Fraser Coho. No exact canonical database record was found.

The latest detailed assessment located is for return year 2022. It was reviewed
in 2024 and published in 2025 as DFO Science Response 2025/027. The assessment
uses DFO's Wild Salmon Policy rapid-status method, CU-level Ricker
spawner-recruit analyses, and simulation of abundance under different
smolt-to-adult survival and exploitation rates. It is not an age-structured
population assessment.

## Evidence for age-disaggregated inputs and outputs

| Component | Evidence and limitation |
|---|---|
| Catch at age | Natural-origin catch and exploitation rates are reported at aggregate scale. The Ricker model uses the proportion of recruits returning at ages 3 and 4, but the report does not include a catch-at-age likelihood. |
| Abundance index and age data | Spawner abundance is compiled by CU and aggregate natural pre-fishery abundance is reported. Age proportions support the stock-recruit calculation; no age-specific survey index or annual age-composition likelihood is described. |
| Weights and maturity | No weight-at-age surface is reported. Scale samples support generation-time and age-at-maturity information, but a model maturity-at-age surface is not supplied. |
| Mortality | Aggregate exploitation rate is estimated using several methods, including FRAM in recent years. No age-specific F or M surface is reported. |
| Timing | The assessment reports return-year and spawning-abundance series. It does not define a within-year timing fraction for an age-specific survey index. |
| Outputs | Outputs include CU-level spawner and recruit abundance, aggregate pre-fishery abundance, smolt-to-adult survival, exploitation, reference points, and forward simulations. It does not report population N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age. |

The report notes that scale-age samples are limited, at approximately 100
samples per CU per year, and that spawner abundance has unrepresented
observation error and changing survey quality. These issues affect the source
assessment's uncertainty but do not provide the age-specific population inputs
required by tinyAM.

## Screening decision

Record this as a distinct new DFO unit and screen it **ineligible** for the
age-disaggregated assessment set. Age proportions are used in the stock-recruit
analysis, but the assessment is based on CU-level spawner/recruit metrics and
aggregate exploitation; it does not fit or report age-specific population
surfaces. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records are added.
