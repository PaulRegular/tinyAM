# DFO/NAFO redfish in Divisions 3LN, 2024

## Unit and latest assessment

The 3LN management unit combines deepwater redfish (*Sebastes mentella*) and
Acadian redfish (*S. fasciatus*). It is distinct from the adjacent 3O unit and
from redfish management units elsewhere in the Northwest Atlantic. NAFO SCR
Doc. 24/048 is the latest detailed official assessment located. The 2025 SCR
Doc. 25/002 updates operating models for the management-strategy evaluation;
it is not a newer fitted production assessment.

## Evidence and screening decision

The 2024 assessment builds on an index-based status approach. The earlier
ASPIC production-model assessment had been rejected because of diagnostic
problems. The 2024 document reports standardized survey-index information and
stock-status evidence. It does not document a current age-structured model or
run-specific catch-at-age, age-specific survey likelihood, age-specific stock
weights, maturity, or mortality surfaces. It also notes that the report did
not update commercial catch-at-length. The 2025 operating-model work is
supporting MSE material, not a new fitted assessment.

This stock is screened out of the age-structured candidate pool because the
latest production assessment is index based and does not report the age-specific
population inputs and outputs needed for that comparison. This is a finding
about the current assessment materials, not a claim that no age samples exist
in any repository. No canonical numerical records were imported.
