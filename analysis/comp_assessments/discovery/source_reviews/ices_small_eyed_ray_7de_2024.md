# ICES English Channel small-eyed ray (rje.27.7de), 2024

## Stock and assessment identity

This is the ICES stock unit for small-eyed ray (*Raja microocellata*) in
divisions 7.d and 7.e. It is separate from the small-eyed ray unit in divisions
7.f and 7.g. The latest advice found is the 2024 ICES record 3699, with Stock
Assessment Graphs key 19097. The advice is valid for 2025–2028.

## Assessment evidence

ICES classifies the 2024 advice as Category 5. The graph source provides total
landings in tonnes for 2009–2023; it contains no age-specific catch or survey
series. The 2024 advice sets a landings limit of 32 tonnes per year and says
ICES cannot quantify the corresponding catches. The historical 2022 advice
described the method as no quantitative assessment, using landings data only;
it is cited only as method context and its values are not used in place of the
2024 series.

No age-specific biology, survey abundance index, natural-mortality surface or
population-at-age outputs are reported for the current advice. A sentinel
fishery in Division 7.e collects data to support possible future assessment
development; it is not evidence that those observations were used in the 2024
advice.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. The
current advice is landings-based Category 5, without age-structured population
inputs or outputs. No earlier run was substituted and no canonical stock,
assessment, input or output rows were added.
