# ICES whiting in Division 3.a, 2026

## Unit and current assessment

`whg.27.3a` is whiting in Skagerrak and Kattegat. It is a distinct assessment
unit from the North Sea (`whg.27.47d`), Irish Sea (`whg.27.7a`), and West of
Scotland (`whg.27.6a`) records in the canonical database. The 2026 ICES advice
identifies it as a Category 3 stock. The matching Stock Assessment Graphs
(SAG) source describes stock size as `B_index`, reports the unit as tonnes,
and does not provide age-specific population surfaces.

## Evidence and screening decision

The 2026 SAG data show an aggregate biomass-index series from 1975 through
2025. Low and high bounds are reported from 1983 onward. Landings and discards
are also shown, with the source series in tonnes; a relative fishing-pressure
measure is reported from 2022 through 2025. The SAG age-and-length section has
no age-specific outputs. The reviewed official advice and SAG pages do not
document catch-at-age, an age-specific survey likelihood, age-specific stock
weights or maturity, or an age-structured mortality input.

This is sufficient to screen the current assessment out of the age-structured
candidate pool: its latest official method is a Category 3 aggregate index
assessment, not an age-structured model with comparable N-at-age and F-at-age
outputs. This conclusion concerns the current official assessment and the
reviewed pages only. It does not claim that raw age samples do not exist
elsewhere. No canonical numerical records were imported.
