# DFO 4RST Capelin

## Current unit and assessment

DFO manages Capelin in NAFO Divisions 4RST as one Estuary and Gulf of
St. Lawrence unit. The 2026 Science Advisory Report assesses the 2025 stock and
provides advice for the 2026 and 2027 fisheries. DFO notes that the unit's
internal stock structure is not clearly defined. It is distinct from the
2J+3KL Capelin complex and should not be merged with it.

The current approach is explicitly index-based. It evaluates mature-biomass
indices for the northern and southern Gulf and compares a fishing-mortality
index with levels considered sustainable for a short-lived forage fish. The
report gives annual landings for 1960–2025, bottom-trawl survey catch weight
and numbers for 1990–2025, mature-biomass indices with 95% confidence intervals,
and the fishing-mortality index. A recruitment index is currently unavailable.
The report describes the last full assessment as 2024 while also documenting
the 2025 assessment reviewed in 2026; retain this source-reported date mismatch.

## Age-data screen

The latest assessment explicitly says age data are unavailable because true
growth annuli cannot be distinguished reliably from false annuli. This prevents
cohort analysis. Survey catches are reported by weight and number, but not by
age. No catch-at-age, age-specific survey index, weight-at-age, maturity-at-age,
or age-specific natural mortality is available from the reviewed source.

The fishing-mortality index is calculated from annual landings divided by the
previous year's northern-Gulf mature-biomass index, assuming survey catchability
`q = 0.01`. It is not a fitted F-at-age surface. The mature-biomass indices are
August–September survey quantities, whereas the fishery begins at least six
months later. The 2025 assessment does not report population N-at-age, F-at-age,
M-at-age, or SSB trajectories.

Research Document 2026/007 provides detailed information for the earlier
2022–2023 assessment. It supports understanding earlier methods and data, but
cannot replace the latest 2025 assessment record. The detailed report is cached
under `source_cache/discovery_dfo_capelin_4rst/`. The 2026/014 advisory report
was reviewed through the official publication viewer; local direct-download
attempts returned HTML rather than the PDF.

This distinct stock is ineligible for the age-structured tinyAM comparison set
because the latest assessment has no age data and is index-based. This does not
mean its reported aggregate indices are unavailable for future source
curation.
