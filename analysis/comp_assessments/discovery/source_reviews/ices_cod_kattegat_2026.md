# ICES cod in Kattegat (cod.27.21), 2026 screen

Reviewed 2026-10-30. The current ICES assessment unit is cod in Subdivision 21
(Kattegat), stock code `cod.27.21`. It is distinct from the Northern Shelf,
western Baltic and eastern Baltic cod units already recorded in the database.
No canonical record or assessment for this ICES stock code was found.

The 2026 WGBFAS audit appendix describes an accepted age-based SAM update as
indicative of trends and unable to mimic catches. It reports no forecast. The
full WGBFAS report's section 2.2 is a placeholder stating that the section
would be published with the stock advice. The appendix's summary audit is
therefore the available assessment-method evidence; it does not supply the
input tables needed to reconstruct the assessment.

The 2026 ICES advice record classifies the stock as Category 3 and advises zero
catch for 2027 and 2028. ICES Stock Assessment Graphs key 22381 reports relative
recruitment at age 1, relative SSB and relative mortality for ages 3–5 with
uncertainty, along with aggregate landings, catches and discards. The source
tables do not provide age-specific N, F or M surfaces. Age 1 for recruitment
and ages 3–5 for mortality identify the reported summary metrics; they do not
establish that age-specific input data are publicly available.

Catch-at-age, survey age compositions or abundance-at-age, run-matched
weights-at-age, maturity-at-age, M assumptions and survey timing could not be
verified from the current report chapter and official ICES source-data tables.
The assessment's age-based model label is not enough to conclude that the
assessment inputs satisfy the age-disaggregated screen. Eligibility is therefore
**unresolved**, pending access to run-matched inputs. No canonical assessment,
input or output values were added.
