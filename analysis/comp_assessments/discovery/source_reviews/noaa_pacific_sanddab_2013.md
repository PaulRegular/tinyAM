# Pacific Sanddab, U.S. Pacific Coast

Reviewed 2026-10-29. NOAA Stock SMART 10190 and the Pacific Fishery Management Council's 2026 stock definitions identify the U.S. Pacific Coast unit as coastwide. No corresponding canonical tinyAM stock record was found.

## Assessment and current advice

The latest detailed age-structured model located is the 2013 Stock Synthesis assessment, with data through 2012. It fits commercial and recreational catches, survey biomass indices, length compositions, and conditional age-at-length compositions. The report includes sex-specific retained-fish age compositions for the California trawl fleet in 2003 and 2007-2008 and for the Oregon/Washington trawl fleet in 1995-2012 with gaps; ages are grouped 0-11. The NWFSC survey age data are conditional age-at-length, not an age-specific abundance index. The report also describes growth, length-weight, maturity, and mortality inputs and provides annual spawning-biomass, depletion and exploitation outputs.

The limitations affect whether this assessment is suitable for a tinyAM source comparison. The age samples are from retained fish, while total catches include discards, so they do not establish total catch-at-age. Numerical Stock Synthesis inputs and full numeric N-at-age/F-at-age outputs were not found among the public files. The 2013 STAR Panel did not recommend the model for management because its biomass scale was uncertain relative to fishery-independent surveys. The assessment was accepted for status determination only. The 2016 PFMC SAFE states that harvest specifications continued to use a Category 3 approach; the available 2027-2028 Council draft also lists a Category 3 contribution based on the 2011 method revision.

## Screening decision

This is a new coastwide candidate with useful, official age-structured status-assessment material, but its source-curation eligibility remains unresolved. A faithful age-based catch series cannot be reconstructed from retained-fish age samples while discard catches remain unaged, and run-matched model inputs were not located. The 2013 model's status-only acceptance also means it should not be presented as the model used for current harvest specifications. No canonical values were imported.

This review distinguishes the available status assessment from the Category 3 harvest-specification method; it does not treat the age-structured assessment's biomass estimate as a precise harvest reference point.

## Sources

- [Pacific Council, Status of the U.S. Pacific Sanddab Resource in 2013](https://www.pcouncil.org/documents/2014/01/status-of-the-u-s-pacific-sanddab-resource-in-2013-august-2013.pdf), detailed assessment; age-composition observations are summarized in the fishery and survey sections, and status outputs appear in the results tables.
- [NOAA, Fish Stock Assessment Status, Q4 FY2023, Appendix D](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/s3/2021-04/Appendix-D.pdf), lists the 2013 operational assessment as inadequate and partially accepted for status determination only.
- [PFMC, 2016 Groundfish SAFE](https://www.pcouncil.org/documents/2016/06/g4_att4_safe_electonly_jun2016bb.pdf), explains why the 2013 model was not used to set harvest specifications and documents continued Category 3 treatment.
- [PFMC SSC, September 2021 minutes](https://www.pcouncil.org/documents/2022/05/september-8-9-2021-ssc-minutes.pdf/), notes that status determination was accepted for Pacific sanddab under a Category 3 assessment.
- [PFMC, draft 2027-2028 harvest specifications](https://www.pcouncil.org/documents/2025/10/f-3-attachment-1-draft-harvest-specifications-sections-of-the-pacific-coast-groundfish-fishery-2027-2028-harvest-specification-and-management-measures.pdf), lists the Pacific sanddab Category 3 contribution and its basis.
- [PFMC Amendment 37 stock definitions](https://www.pcouncil.org/actions/phase-2-identification-of-groundfish-in-need-of-conservation-and-management-and-defining-their-stocks/), identifies Pacific sanddab as a coastwide unit.
