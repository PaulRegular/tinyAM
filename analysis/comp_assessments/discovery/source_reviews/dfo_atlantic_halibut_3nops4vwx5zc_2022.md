# DFO Atlantic halibut, Scotian Shelf and southern Grand Banks (2022)

## Unit and assessment

The DFO Sustainability Survey entry “Atlantic Halibut - NAFO 3NOPs4VWX5Z” refers to the management unit 3NOPs4VWX5Zc. DFO defines 4RST as a separate management unit. The canonical database has no record for this DFO unit, so it remains a provisional new unit.

NOAA Stock SMART 10056 is the U.S. Atlantic halibut assessment. Its 2024 empirical assessment includes Canadian catches and discusses uncertainty about how the U.S. unit relates to the larger population. The reviewed evidence does not establish a one-to-one match with the DFO 3NOPs4VWX5Zc unit; keep the official management units separate and do not use the NOAA entry to fill DFO data gaps.

The latest full DFO assessment is the 2022 SISCAL model, with data through 2021. The detailed framework assessment was published as CSAS Research Document 2024/013, and the reviewed inputs were later documented in Research Document 2025/003. The 2024 and later status updates are separate interim records, not replacements for the full model's run-specific inputs.

## Model inputs and outputs

- **Catch:** The model is statistical catch-at-length. It uses annual landings/removals and sex-specific commercial proportions-at-length; the reviewed sources do not identify a catch-at-age likelihood or observed catch-at-age series.
- **Abundance indices:** It uses aggregate indices from the Maritimes RV survey and the Industry-DFO Halibut Longline Survey, including the fixed-station and stratified-random longline series. The model also fits proportion-at-length information from surveys. An age-specific survey index or age-composition likelihood was not verified.
- **Biology:** The 2025 input report documents updated growth and length-weight relationships. The framework report applies female maturity ages of A50=11.5 and A95=14.5. These support the age-structured internal population model but do not turn the observed likelihood into age-indexed data.
- **Mortality:** The 2022 SISCAL model estimates time-varying natural mortality. The framework report gives 2014-2021 estimates ranging from 0.119 to 0.137 for males and 0.111 to 0.128 for females.
- **Timing:** The RV survey is conducted in summer and the longline survey is annual. Exact within-year model timing was not established in the reviewed reports.
- **Outputs:** The assessment reports SSB, total and legal-sized biomass, recruitment, fishing mortality, and time-varying M. The reviewed reports do not provide a complete official N-at-age or F-at-age series. The framework assessment reports 2021 SSB of 31.1 kt with a 95% interval of 25-36 kt.

## Screening decision

This DFO unit is **ineligible for the age-indexed candidate set**. SISCAL is age-structured internally, but its observed catch and survey composition likelihoods are based on length, alongside aggregate abundance indices. Age samples inform growth. No age-composition input or age-specific index sufficient to specify the requested minimal age-indexed model was verified. This is a scope decision, not a claim that the assessment lacks age data for biological estimation or that its aggregate outputs are unavailable. No canonical numerical records were imported.
