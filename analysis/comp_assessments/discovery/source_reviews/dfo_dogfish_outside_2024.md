# Pacific Spiny Dogfish, Outside BC

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO assesses Pacific Spiny Dogfish (Squalus suckleyi) as separate inside and outside British Columbia stocks. This case is the outside stock in PMFC major areas 3CD5ABCDE. No corresponding canonical tinyAM stock record was found.

## Current assessment and age-data screen

The latest detailed production assessment located is the 2024 assessment, published as Research Document 2025/055 in February 2026, with advice in Science Advisory Report 2025/032. The model uses Stock Synthesis 3 and is a two-sex, age-structured population model fitted to fishery and survey catches, standardized abundance or biomass indices, and length-composition data.

The model's observation likelihood does not include catch-at-age or age-composition data. The report says historical age samples from research projects, surveys, tag returns, and some fisheries could not be linked to particular survey-year or fishing-event combinations. Those ages were used to characterize the growth curve, not as annual fishery or survey compositions. The survey indices are aggregate standardized series and cannot be split by age from the reported observations. The report explicitly notes that length compositions were less informative about depletion than age compositions would have been. Catch totals plus these length samples therefore do not support a source-based reconstruction of observed catch-at-age.

The assessment derives sex-specific growth, weight-at-length, maturity-at-age and fecundity from biological information. Its base model fixes shared natural mortality at 0.065; sensitivity models explore alternative and time-varying M. The surveys operate in summer or early fall, with different seasonal windows among surveys, but the report does not establish a single fractional-year timing for each index.

The official outputs include spawning output measured as pup production, depletion S/S0, fishing pressure relative to F0.4S0, and age-structured results illustrated for selected age classes. The advisory report gives 2023 base-model S/S0 of 0.09 (95% CI 0.08–0.09) and F/F0.4S0 of 1.5 (1.3–1.6). The reports do not provide a verified numeric annual N-at-age or instantaneous F-at-age matrix in the materials reviewed; pup production is not directly interchangeable with teleost SSB.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct, new DFO outside-water unit, screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated candidate set. The decisive limitation is the absence of age-indexed observations in the fitted likelihood and the lack of event-linked age compositions from which to reconstruct them. Its age-structured model and derived age plots do not establish age-specific observed inputs. No canonical data were imported.

This is an evidence-based exclusion from the current age-input screen, not a claim that no age data exist anywhere for the species. The separate DFO inside-water stock remains a distinct, unscreened candidate.
