# ICES white anglerfish (mon.27.78abd), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

The 2026 ICES advice record 7176 identifies white anglerfish in Subarea 7 and
divisions 8.a, 8.b and 8.d. It is a Category 1 assessment linked to Stock
Assessment Graphs key 23756.

## Assessment evidence

WGBIE 2026 section 3.2 describes a length-based age-structured Stock Synthesis
3 model. It uses landings and discards, catch numbers-at-length, three aggregate
survey-index series (FR_IE_IBTS, IAMS and Spanish SpGFS-WIBTS-Q4), and survey
length distributions. The report says about half of landings had associated
length data. Maturity, M, female growth and length-weight parameters are fixed;
some male growth parameters are estimated.

The public graph reports recruitment at age 0 (thousands), combined-sex SSB
(tonnes) and F ages 3–15. It does not provide N-at-age, F-at-age or M-at-age
surfaces. The report's detailed source material and the assessment data through
2025 are cached in the shared ignored WGBIE 2026 source directory documented in
the black-bellied anglerfish source review.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated candidate set. The
production model has age structure but its documented catch and survey
composition observations are at length. The published population outputs are
aggregate or age-group summaries, not age-specific annual surfaces. No
canonical assessment, input or output rows were added.
