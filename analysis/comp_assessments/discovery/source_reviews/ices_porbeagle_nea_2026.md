# ICES Northeast Atlantic porbeagle (por.27.nea), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

This ICES stock unit covers porbeagle (*Lamna nasus*) in subareas 1–10, 12,
and 14 (the Northeast Atlantic and adjacent waters). The latest published
advice located is the 2026 assessment, Standard Graphs key 26019 and Category
2.11. It advises that catches, including dead discards, be no more than 185
tonnes in each year from 2027 through 2030.

## Assessment evidence

The official 2026 source-data table reports a historical annual landings
series in tonnes for 1926–2025. The graph metadata has no stock-size or
fishing-pressure series. Its age and length fields list recruitment age zero
and no fishing-mortality age; the graph does not report numbers, fishing
mortality, or natural mortality at age. No age-specific catch, survey, weight,
maturity, or mortality inputs are documented for this assessment.

The current published materials therefore support an aggregate landings
history and advice, not the age-disaggregated inputs and outputs required for a
tinyAM comparison.

## Screening decision

This stock unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment set.
No earlier stock unit was substituted and no canonical stock, assessment,
input, or output rows were added.
