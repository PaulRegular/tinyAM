# Eastern Pacific bigeye tuna (NOAA:11685; IATTC assessment unit)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**Scientific name:** *Thunnus obesus*
**Assessment unit:** Eastern Pacific Ocean
**Latest accepted assessment located:** IATTC 2026 update; data through 2025
**NOAA Stock SMART:** assessment year 2026; Full acceptance; Operational; model SS

NOAA Stock SMART row 11685 is the U.S. inventory record for the Eastern Pacific bigeye stock assessed by IATTC. It is one regional stock unit, separate from Atlantic and Western/Central Pacific bigeye. No matching canonical tinyAM stock record was found.

## Inputs and model observations

The 2026 report updates the 2024 IATTC benchmark with 2024–2025 catches, longline abundance-index data, and length compositions; the report states that the remaining model assumptions and parameters are unchanged. The assessment defines 22 longline and purse-seine fishery fleets, including separate catch units for some longline fleets and retained/discard components for purse-seine fleets. Longline catches can be represented in thousands of fish or tonnes; purse-seine catches are in tonnes.

The fitted abundance series is a fishery-dependent Japanese longline CPUE index representing mature bigeye. Purse-seine and longline composition observations are length compositions. The assessment does not fit observed catch-at-age, fishery age-composition, or an age-specific abundance-index likelihood. The model report does discuss annual fishing mortality by grouped ages, but those model states do not make the fitted observations age-disaggregated. Length compositions alone are not sufficient to reconstruct source catch-at-age for this screen.

## Biology, time step, and outputs

The 2026 update carries forward the 2024 benchmark biological specification, with alternative reference-model hypotheses for growth, selectivity, and natural mortality. The update report does not provide a run-matched observed weight-at-age or maturity-at-age input table. It reports quarter-based age groups for fishing mortality, aggregate recruitment and spawning-biomass trajectories, reference-point comparisons, and uncertainty summarized over 36 reference models. It does not provide a numeric annual N-at-age or full annual F-at-age matrix in the reviewed report.

The update uses an aggregate longline index; a single annual survey timing is not applicable. Catch data are updated through the final quarter of 2025.

## Screening decision

This is a **new, screened, ineligible candidate** for the age-disaggregated input set. The current accepted assessment is an operational Stock Synthesis update, but its fitted composition data are lengths, and its abundance index is aggregate. No observed catch-at-age or age-specific abundance input was verified. No canonical database values were imported and no tinyAM translation was attempted.
