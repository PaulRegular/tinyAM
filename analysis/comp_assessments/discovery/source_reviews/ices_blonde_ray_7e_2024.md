# Blonde ray in ICES Division 7.e

## Identity and latest advice

ICES stock `rjh.27.7e` is the blonde ray unit for the western English Channel.
It is distinct from adjacent blonde ray units. No canonical database record
uses this stock code.

The 2024 advice covers 2025–2028 and remains the latest official advice listed
for this unit in the 2026 ICES inventory. The advice states that no assessment
is available and that the recommendation is based on landings data.

## Evidence and screening decision

The advice labels the assessment type “No assessment” and the input data
“Landings data”. WGEF reports low catch rates in a previous beam-trawl survey
and says no reliable survey trend is available for the stock. The report also
describes trawl-survey length-frequency information, but no age-specific
abundance index is used in the current advice.

**Screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set.** Aggregate
landings and landings advice are available, but the current advice is not an
age-structured population assessment and does not report comparable population
or age-specific outputs. No canonical data or tinyAM translation were added.
