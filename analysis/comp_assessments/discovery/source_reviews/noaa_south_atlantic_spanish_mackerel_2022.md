# NOAA South Atlantic Spanish mackerel (SEDAR 78)

## Assessment identity

NOAA Stock SMART identifies assessment 12822 as the 2023 operational assessment of Spanish mackerel in the Southern Atlantic Coast, using the Beaufort Assessment Model (BAM), with data through 2020 and full acceptance. The official assessment is SEDAR 78, completed in 2022 and revised in July 2022. The Atlantic States Marine Fisheries Commission describes SEDAR 78 as the most recent assessment and lists the next assessment for 2027. The Gulf Spanish mackerel unit (NOAA:10173) is a separate management unit.

## Inputs and assumptions

SEDAR 78 Table 2 reports annual commercial handline, gillnet, pound-net, and cast-net landings in thousands of pounds whole weight; recreational landings and shrimp/recreational discards are in thousands of fish. Tables 4-8 report observed age compositions for ages 0-10, with age 10 as the plus group. Handline and cast-net compositions are pooled across years; gillnet, pound-net, and recreational compositions are shown by year. These values and the catch totals support a documented approximate catch-at-age reconstruction, but pooled age samples and the conversion between numbers and commercial catch weight must remain explicit.

The final report gives numerical annual handline and recreational indices for 1986-2020 and a SEAMAP-SA age-0 index for 1989-2019, with CVs. The indices are relative abundance, not age-specific population abundance. The coastal trawl survey samples in spring, summer, and fall, so the sources do not establish one survey fraction of year; the model predicts indices from midyear abundance.

Table 1 reports fixed age-specific length and whole weight for the population, females, and fishery-dependent population, as well as female maturity and natural mortality for ages 0-10. Maturity and sex ratio are fixed. Natural mortality is fixed through time and follows a Lorenzen age schedule scaled to match survival under constant M=0.35 from age 2 through the oldest age.

## Outputs and limitations

The final report includes annual total abundance-at-age (thousands of fish), biomass-at-age (thousands of pounds), recruitment, spawning biomass (metric tons), and instantaneous F-at-age (per year) through 2020. Table 1 supplies fixed M-at-age. Annual uncertainty for the age surfaces was not located; uncertainty is reported for selected assessment quantities.

There is a timing discrepancy to resolve before extraction: Section 3.9 says peak spawning is June 1, while the Table 11 caption defines SSB at the end of July. Do not select one definition without checking the assessment code or another run-specific source. Age compositions are also pooled for two fisheries, and a single survey timing fraction is unavailable.

The assessment is eligible for source curation. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added during this screen. The 2027 assessment is scheduled and was not treated as a completed run.
