# Greenland halibut in ICES subareas 5, 6, 12, and 14: 2026 screen

## Identity and assessment

ICES stock `ghl.27.561214` is the current stock unit covering Iceland and Faroe grounds, West of Scotland, North of Azores, and East of Greenland. It is not a match to the separate DFO Greenland halibut units in the Canadian Arctic or Northwest Atlantic. ICES describes connectivity with the adjacent Northeast Arctic and NAFO stocks as known but unquantified; this does not make those assessment units interchangeable.

The latest advice in the inventory is the 2026 assessment, based on the approved GADGET age–length model benchmarked in 2023. ## Evidence relevant to age-disaggregated curation

- **Catch at age:** The current model is documented as using international commercial catches and length compositions by gear. It does not document a commercial catch-at-age likelihood. The report describes historical disagreements in catch data, so the official catch series should remain source-specific if later curated.
- **Survey information:** The model uses one combined biomass index from the Greenland deepwater survey (1998–2016 and 2022 onward) and Iceland's autumn bottom-trawl survey (since 1996). The Iceland survey has sex-specific age distributions from 2015 onward. Older age readings are considered biased and not representative; older otoliths cannot be re-read with the newer method. The recent survey ages do not by themselves establish fishery catch-at-age or a continuous age-specific survey index.
- **Biology:** The model derives weight at age from a weight–length relation and growth rather than supplying weights-at-age. Maturity is based on sparse survey observations at length; the report calls the survey maturity scale imprecise. A run-matched maturity-at-age series is not documented.
- **Mortality and timing:** Natural mortality is fixed at 0.15. GADGET uses two equal within-year time steps. The reviewed sources do not establish a single fractional survey timing for a tinyAM annual index.
- **Outputs:** The official 2026 ICES source-data table reports age-5 recruitment and F for ages 9–14, plus annual total biomass, SSB, and catches with uncertainty bounds. It does not report annual N-at-age or F-at-age surfaces.

## Screening decision

The stock is a distinct, new ICES candidate and is **screened with eligibility unresolved**. Its GADGET age–length structure and recent survey age compositions make it inappropriate to dismiss solely because it is not a conventional catch-at-age assessment. However, the current evidence does not establish commercial catch-at-age or a supported reconstruction over the assessment period, nor a run-matched age-specific biology series and age-specific population outputs. Resolving eligibility would require the native 2026 input bundle, including the fleet length samples, survey age-length observations, and the exact observation arrays used by GADGET. Do not substitute modeled age structure for observed age inputs.

No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added. No tinyAM translation was attempted.
