# WCPO silky shark (NOAA:11728)

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-02
**Inventory record:** NOAA Stock SMART 11728
**Assessment unit:** Western and Central Pacific silky shark (*Carcharhinus falciformis*)
**Screen:** Ineligible for the age-disaggregated input set. Current assessment sources are documented below; no canonical records were added.

## Identity and assessment status

Stock SMART identifies assessment 13564 (2024; data through 2022; PIFSC; Stock Synthesis V.30.22.1) as Full acceptance. The stock is the WCPO silky shark unit; there is no matching canonical stock in the database.

The WCPFC 2024 assessment report is a multi-model study. It recommends dynamic surplus production results as the most robust basis for management advice because the integrated Stock Synthesis assessment had unresolved fitting issues. It describes the length-and-age model (LAM) as experimental. WCPFC21 later records the Scientific Committee’s caution that biomass/depletion estimates were highly uncertain and reports no projections. The WPFMC 2024 SAFE report says the 2024 assessment did not provide reliable biomass and fishing-mortality estimates relative to MSY and retains the 2018 assessment as a status reference. These descriptions differ from Stock SMART’s summary label; this review preserves the difference rather than treating the labels as interchangeable.

The 2018 WCPFC addendum documents a WCPO-only Stock Synthesis model derived from the WCPO data of a Pacific-wide assessment. It is a historical status reference, not a substitute for the detailed 2024 run.

## Inputs and outputs located

- **Catch:** The 2024 assessment reconstructs historical catch and fits catch-at-length observations. The 2018 WCPO-only model also uses catch histories. Neither reviewed report provides catch numbers-at-age or age-composition observations.
- **Abundance indices:** The 2024 models use annual standardized fishery CPUE indices as aggregate relative-abundance series. The WCPFC review identifies 2020 as the latest year with a usable biomass index. The index observations are not age-specific.
- **Compositions:** The assessment uses length compositions for capture and index fleets. Its age-structured population states are model-derived; they do not turn length observations into source age-composition data.
- **Biology:** Growth, maturity and weight are represented through life-history assumptions and length-based biological inputs. The 2024 report notes maturity at roughly ages 5–6, but a complete source maturity-at-age and weight-at-age input set was not recovered.
- **Mortality and timing:** Mortality assumptions or priors are model-specific. The advisory model is annual, and no within-year survey timing fraction applies to the aggregate fishery CPUE indices.
- **Outputs:** The 2024 report and WCPFC21 review give aggregate depletion, harvest-rate and catch/status quantities with substantial uncertainty. They do not provide an advised-model age-specific output series. The report identifies the LAM as experimental and recommends the aggregate production model for advice.

## Screen decision

**Ineligible for the age-disaggregated input set.** The current detailed assessment uses catch histories, aggregate CPUE and length compositions, not verified age-specific observations. The age-structured Stock Synthesis and LAM population states are not age-input data; the LAM is explicitly experimental. The 2018 WCPO-only reference also uses catch, CPUE and aggregated length compositions. The 2024 report remains the current detailed assessment record, and the older assessment is not used to fill gaps in its inputs.

This is a discovery screen only. No stock, assessment, input or output records were added.
