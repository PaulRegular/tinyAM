# British Columbia Outside Lingcod, 2025 assessment

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO's current outside-water Lingcod assessment covers PMFC Areas 3CD + 5ABCDE and excludes Area 4B. DFO treats the inside Area 4B population as a separate unit. No corresponding canonical tinyAM stock record was found.

## Current assessment and age-data screen

The most recent full assessment located is the 2025 peer-reviewed assessment, based on a 2024 Terms of Reference and reconstructed population history from 1927 to the beginning of 2025. The 2025 Science Advisory Report identifies a two-sex, age-structured Stock Synthesis 3 model (v. 3.30.23.1), with equilibrium initialization in 1927, and summarizes 21 sensitivity runs.

The report confirms that the model used catch data through 2024, four abundance-index series, age-composition data from three fleets and three surveys, and fixed growth and maturity parameters estimated externally. It identifies age-composition sources as bottom-trawl catch, bottom-trawl discard, hook-and-line catch, and SYN, HBLL, and IPHC surveys. Bottom-trawl discard ages use cohort slicing. The report does not publish the numerical age-composition inputs, annual catch-at-age, numerical survey-index series, or run-matched biological surfaces. It refers to the detailed Research Document as in preparation; a public run-matched document or model archive was not located in the DFO publications search, regional assessment listings, or stock-specific repository search.

The report publishes fleet catch totals in tonnes for 1996–2024 and a chart of reconstructed commercial catch from 1927. It provides posterior quantiles for female B2025, B0, their ratio, F2024, and reference rates. SSB, fishing mortality, and age-0 recruitment trajectories are shown graphically with 90% credibility envelopes. Numeric annual N-at-age and F-at-age arrays were not found. The 2026 Groundfish Integrated Fisheries Management Plan confirms that the outside Lingcod assessment supplied current harvest advice for the 2025/26 season.

## Screening decision

This is a provisional new DFO stock unit. Its current full assessment is confirmed to use age-composition information, but the numerical age inputs needed to verify a source-ready age-disaggregated series are not available in the public report located. The eligibility screen therefore remains unresolved rather than treating age-structured model outputs as observed age inputs or declaring the source information absent. The report does provide partial run-specific catch and official outputs, so the latest assessment remains the primary record for any later source curation; older assessments must not be used to fill its data gaps.

NOAA Stock SMART separately lists Northern and Southern Pacific Coast Lingcod. No evidence was found that either U.S. assessment unit is identical to the Canadian outside unit; they are not crosswalked solely by species name.
