# Bay of Fundy Striped Bass

## Identity and assessment status

The DFO Sustainability Survey lists “Striped Bass - Bay of Fundy” with an
“Uncertain” status in its 2024 results. DFO sources identify this as the Bay of
Fundy Designatable Unit (DU), comprising spawning populations in the
Shubenacadie, Annapolis, and Saint John rivers. It is distinct from the
southern Gulf of St. Lawrence striped bass stock assessed by DFO.

The latest detailed DFO material located is the 2014 recovery potential
assessment, supported by a 2015 research document summarizing 2013 biology and
status. These documents are not a current production stock assessment. The
2014 report says projections cannot be calculated with available data and
identifies the lack of current adult age-structure, recruitment, and abundance
estimates for the extant Shubenacadie population. No newer DFO production
assessment for this unit was identified in the current search.

## Evidence and screening decision

| Component | Evidence and limitation |
|---|---|
| Catch at age | Directed and bycatch fisheries are described, but no unit-level catch numbers-at-age or catch-age likelihood is reported. DFO notes that catch and retention are not accurately monitored. |
| Abundance index and age data | The sources describe local abundance observations, tagging, and juvenile surveys. They do not provide an annual age-specific index or age-composition likelihood for the DU. |
| Weights and maturity | Life-history information includes age at first spawning, but no population weight-at-age or model maturity-at-age surface is supplied. |
| Mortality | Threats and sources of mortality are discussed qualitatively; age-specific fishing and natural mortality are not estimated. |
| Timing | Spawning occurs mainly in May-June, but no survey-index timing fraction is specified for a population model. |
| Outputs | Status and recovery information includes local abundance observations and targets, but no fitted N-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, recruitment, or spawning-biomass series. |

Screen this distinct DU **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment
set. The DFO inventory entry is a stock-status listing, not evidence that a
current production assessment exists. No canonical stock or assessment data
were added.
