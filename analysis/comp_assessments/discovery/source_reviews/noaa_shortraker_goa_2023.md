# NOAA Gulf of Alaska shortraker rockfish (2023)

## Identity and assessment

Stock SMART 10554 is the NOAA Gulf of Alaska unit of shortraker rockfish (*Sebastes borealis*), distinct from the Bering Sea/Aleutian Islands unit, NOAA 10553. No canonical tinyAM stock, assessment, input, or output record matches either unit.

The latest operational assessment located is the 2023 Model 23.3 REMA assessment. The final December 2025 SSC report confirms that 2023 was the most recent operational assessment; the planned 2025 update was not completed, and an operational assessment was proposed for the 2026 cycle. The 2026 assessment meeting is scheduled for November, after this review date. Thus the 2023 report remains the latest detailed model available as of 30 October 2026.

The 2023 assessment and 2025 SSC report are cached in source_cache/discovery_noaa_shortraker_2023_2025/; sources.csv records their URLs, revisions, retrieval date, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes.

## Assessment data and outputs

The 2023 model fits regional bottom-trawl survey biomass estimates for 1990–2023 and AFSC longline relative population weights for 1992–2023, with associated uncertainty. Fishery catch is aggregate; commercial length samples are few, and the report says the few fishery age samples had not been aged. The plotted age compositions describe estimated populations in a few survey years and are not age-composition likelihood inputs to REMA. No fishery catch-at-age or age-specific survey index is reported.

The report provides supporting length-weight and growth parameters and cites age/maturity studies, but these are not age-indexed inputs to the selected random-effects population model. Natural mortality is fixed at 0.03 per year under Tier 5. Outputs include aggregate exploitable-biomass trajectories with uncertainty and harvest limits; annual N-at-age, F-at-age, recruitment-at-age, and SSB surfaces are not reported by this model.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new current assessment unit, but it is ineligible for the age-disaggregated candidate set. The selected model is fit to aggregate survey biomass and relative-population-weight indices, not age-disaggregated observations. No canonical data were imported.
