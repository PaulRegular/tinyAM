# Thorny skate - NAFO 3LNOPs

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO's survey entry `Thorny Skate - NAFO 3LNO` corresponds to the Grand Banks thorny skate stock assessed by NAFO across Divisions 3LNO and Subdivision 3Ps. DFO and NAFO describe 3LNO and 3Ps as one biological stock, although 3Ps is also managed separately by Canada and France.

## Current advice and latest detailed assessment

The latest advice located is NAFO's June 2026 advice for 2027 and beyond. The Scientific Council could not complete either a full assessment or an interim monitoring report because the designated-expert position was vacant. It provided no updated status estimate. The latest detailed assessment document located is the 2024 NAFO SCR Doc. 24/038, with observations through 2023. Its cover is marked DRAFT and says not to cite it without prior reference to the authors, so it is treated as detailed screening material rather than a final approved assessment report. That assessment is a qualitative review of catch, survey biomass and abundance trends, a recruitment index based on skate length, and a relative fishing-mortality ratio. It is not an age-structured population assessment.

## Age-disaggregated evidence

The 2024 report describes commercial size distributions and research-survey indices; it does not use catch-at-age, survey abundance-at-age, or an age-structured population likelihood. NAFO's 2024 biological-sampling inventory lists Canadian thorny-skate commercial sampling as catch only, with no age, individual-weight, or maturity samples recorded in that table. The report's recruitment indicator counts skates at or below 21 cm total length and is not an age-1 abundance estimate. It reports aggregate survey indices and relative F, not N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age. Weight-at-age and maturity-at-age are not required by this assessment.

A 2025 peer-reviewed state-space research model for the same population is separate research and is not the production assessment underlying NAFO advice. It does not change this screening decision.

This current unit is ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set. No canonical assessment or numeric series was imported.
