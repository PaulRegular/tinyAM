# Red gurnard (`gur.27.3-8`), 2025 advice

## Assessment identity and current method

The 2026 ICES Stock Information Database lists red gurnard
(*Chelidonichthys cuculus*) in subareas 3-8, with the latest advice cycle in
2025. The 2025 ICES advice describes a Category 3 survey trends-based
assessment. It uses a survey-derived biomass index and reports that reliable
catch data are not available; the advice therefore provides no catch advice.

The advice lists multiple groundfish surveys contributing to the aggregate
index. It does not identify catch-at-age, age composition, age-specific survey
abundance, or age-specific biological inputs. The advice reports the aggregate
biomass index with uncertainty and landings, but does not provide age-specific
N, F, or M population outputs. This unit is ineligible for the age-disaggregated
inventory. This does not establish whether raw age samples exist outside the
current assessment materials.

The linked ICES Standard Graphs source page is for assessment year 2023 and
contains aggregate biomass-index bounds and landings. It is retained as a
run-labelled historical source and is not represented as the 2025 advice
itself. The 2025 advice sheet reports the current advice cycle through 2024.

## Database treatment

The assessment is represented as one unit spanning subareas 3-8, even though
the SID assigns it to several ecoregions. It is a distinct current stock code;
no canonical stock, assessment event, or numerical input/output rows were
added.
