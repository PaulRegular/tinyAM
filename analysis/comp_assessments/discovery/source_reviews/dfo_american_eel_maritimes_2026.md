# American eel and elver fisheries in the DFO Maritimes Region

## Identity and latest assessment information

The DFO inventory lists “Eel (Large)” and “Elvers” separately because they are
managed as different fisheries. They are life stages of American eel
(*Anguilla rostrata*), which the 2026 DFO research document describes as a
single panmictic population. The two inventory rows are therefore linked to
one Maritimes candidate unit; they are not counted as separate stocks.

The 2018 process remains the latest population assessment identified by DFO.
It used spawner-per-recruit analyses to define mortality reference points,
particularly for the elver fishery. The detailed 2026 research document updates
large-eel catch and effort, the East River-Chester elver recruitment index and
other regional abundance indicators. It does not report a new age-structured
population assessment.

## Evidence and screening decision

The available assessment and update do not provide population catch numbers at
age, annual weights-at-age and maturity-at-age, or age-specific survey indices
for an annual population model. Some juvenile age samples are discussed, but
they are not a population-at-age input series. The reported outputs are fishery
and regional abundance indicators rather than comparable annual N-at-age,
F-at-age, M-at-age, recruitment or spawning-biomass surfaces.

**Screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set.** The two DFO
entries are recorded as one new candidate unit. No canonical stock, assessment,
input or output record was created.
