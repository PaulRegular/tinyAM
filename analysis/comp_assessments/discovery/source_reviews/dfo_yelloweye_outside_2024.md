# Outside Yelloweye Rockfish

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO's Pacific inventory entry corresponds to the outside Yelloweye Rockfish population in British Columbia. It is a distinct current stock unit from Inside Yelloweye Rockfish. No canonical tinyAM stock record was found.

## Latest assessment and accessible detail

The latest assessment located is titled *Operating model code transition and stock status update (2024) for Outside Yelloweye Rockfish in British Columbia*. DFO published it as Science Advisory Report 2026/039 in August 2026. The official catalogue record is available, but the catalogue's linked PDF URL returned an archived-information placeholder when requested on 2026-10-29. Therefore, the report's text, current data cutoff, numeric inputs, model details, and reported output values could not be independently checked. The catalogue page and the placeholder response are cached locally for follow-up.

Earlier same-unit sources establish that the outside stock has been assessed with age-structured operating models. Research Document 2020/069 describes the 2019 rebuilding analysis, using data through 2018 and catch, survey age-composition, and life-history inputs. Science Response 2023/037 corrected an age-composition likelihood error in those models but explicitly added no new data; its stock-status estimates still refer to the end of 2018. These sources are historical context only and are not substituted for the 2024 update.

The earlier research document includes age-structured model and biology details, but this screening did not verify a complete run-matched catch-at-age series, weight-at-age table, fractional survey timing, or annual N-at-age/F-at-age output for the latest 2024 update.

## Screening decision

Identity is a provisional new current unit, with eligibility unresolved pending access to the official 2026/039 report and its detailed supporting material. The prior model family and age-composition inputs do not establish that the 2024 update has the needed numeric inputs and comparable outputs. Keep the 2024 update as the primary source target; do not use the 2019 data or outputs to fill gaps. No canonical assessment or numeric series was imported.
