# DFO Gulf Region Atlantic salmon inventory entry

Reviewed 2026-10-29. The discovery row `DFO:Gulf:Atlantic Salmon - Gulf` is a
regional grouping, not a single stock. DFO describes 98 salmon-bearing rivers
with river-specific reference points; the current advice reports separate
programs for the Restigouche, Southwest and Northwest Miramichi, PEI rivers,
and the Margaree. The candidate register therefore leaves the canonical and
candidate unit identifiers blank and marks identity as ambiguous.

The latest update is the June 2025 Science Response for indicators through
2024. It says the last full assessment covered 2019 returns and that the
2023–24 work was interim. It describes river-specific abundance programs:
fishery-dependent and juvenile-survey indices, mark–recapture, and redd
counts. The adult observations and returns are reported as small and large
size groups. Juvenile electrofishing age-class indices are reported in parts
of SFA 16, but they do not provide catch-at-age or a matched age-specific adult
abundance index. The update reports returns, spawners, egg deposition, and
juvenile indices; no population N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age series was
identified.

The row is screened ineligible as one age-structured candidate because it
combines river populations and its current assessment inputs are not an
age-structured catch/abundance series. This does not exclude a future review
of a specific river population as its own unit.

Sources:

- [DFO Science Response 2025/019](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/41293927.pdf), indicators through 2024.
- [DFO Science Response 2023/035](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/41199650.pdf), earlier update cited for regional methods.
- The 2025/019 report identifies the separate river-level methods and reports the most recent full assessment year.
