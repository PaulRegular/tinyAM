# NOAA Caribbean queen triggerfish, SEDAR 80 (2022–2024)

## Screen result

NOAA Stock SMART identifies three separate management units: Puerto Rico (stock 15649), St. Croix (15558), and St. Thomas/St. John (15496). The current accepted production assessment is the revised 2022 Puerto Rico assessment, with data through 2019, and the 2024 follow-up assessments for St. Croix and St. Thomas/St. John, also with data through 2019. The earlier 2022 USVI assessment records were not accepted; the 2024 reports are the current USVI assessments. NOAA Stock SMART identifies each as a Stock Synthesis assessment with full acceptance. The Council’s SSC found the 2024 USVI results suitable for short-term management advice, and NOAA’s 2026 final rule implemented management measures based on them.

All three assessment reports document a Stock Synthesis model and estimated age-based quantities. That does not mean observed age data were used. The St. Thomas/St. John and St. Croix reports state that available samples were insufficient to create a reliable age-length key or representative fishery age compositions. Both reports also state that fishery-independent age compositions were unavailable. The Puerto Rico report states that historical age-composition samples had not been collected and that no fishery-independent age compositions were available. The models instead use landings/removals, aggregate indices or reef visual census density, length compositions, and biological information. Therefore these cases do not have catch-at-age or an age-specific abundance index suitable for the age-disaggregated database.

The reports include fixed life-history assumptions and show estimated biomass, spawning biomass, recruitment, fishing mortality, and predicted numbers at age. The age-based population outputs are shown in figures; numerical age-by-year series were not found in the reviewed reports. Some biological surfaces are represented inside Stock Synthesis through growth and length-weight relationships rather than a stand-alone age-specific observation series. These data are not enough to satisfy the database’s required observed age inputs.

The three units are therefore screened as **ineligible** for age-structured source curation. Keep their management areas separate. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added.
