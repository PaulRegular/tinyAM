# British Columbia yellowtail rockfish: discovery review

The [2026 DFO Pacific groundfish plan](https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fm-gp/mplans/ground-fond-ifmp-pgip-sm-eng.html) identifies coastwide Yellowtail Rockfish advice from the 2024 assessment. The [2025 advisory report](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/41294804.pdf) defines one Canadian stock in 3CD5ABCDE excluding 4B. There is no *Sebastes flavidus* stock in the canonical stocks table. The NOAA northern Pacific Coast stock is a US assessment unit and should not be conflated with this Canadian unit. This candidate was absent from the 2024 Sustainability Survey snapshot and was added from the regional listing; it is not a canonical stock record.

The advisory report describes one two-sex Stock Synthesis model for 1935-2024, using total catch biomass, six survey abundance series and age-frequency proportions from the fishery and three surveys. Its 2024 catch repeats 2023 provisionally. Natural mortality is estimated by sex; female maturity and sex-specific growth and weight relationships are externally estimated. Figure 1 displays recruitment in millions of age-0 fish and spawning biomass relative to BMSY, with 90% credible intervals; the report also gives selected terminal and equilibrium summaries. The full numerical historical population output is not supplied as a table in that report.

The official [pinned model repository](https://github.com/pbs-software/pbs-synth/tree/16f1d4368ad7d3e854219ca1c2d9d6353252c861/models/input/2024/YTR) identifies B1 R02v2 as the coastwide base run and provides Stock Synthesis input and control files for `02.01`. The input sets model ages 0-45+, observed composition bins 1-45+, and seven fleets. The control file specifies externally supplied age maturity, while it does not read a weight-at-age file; evaluate the growth and weight-length inputs and their availability at age rather than treating an estimated output surface as an original observation.

Screening is complete, but eligibility remains unresolved. The current official advisory report and pinned base inputs establish the assessment and its broad data structure, but the reviewed sources do not verify the exact common-age/year reconstruction, survey abundance versus composition roles, full biological surfaces, or historical numerical official output. A search of current DFO publication listings and the regional plan did not locate the detailed research document cited as in preparation or a fitted output archive. Do not substitute the US assessment or infer historical point estimates from plotted curves.

The advisory report, regional listing, model README and `02.01` data/control files are cached in `source_cache/discovery_dfo_yellowtail_bc/`; `sources.csv` records URLs, retrieval date and SHA-256 checksums. The repository revision is `16f1d4368ad7d3e854219ca1c2d9d6353252c861`.
