# US coastwide chilipepper rockfish: discovery review

The [2025 US assessment](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/72420) models *Sebastes goodei* as a single population in US waters off California, Oregon and Washington. Stock SMART identifies this as `NOAA:17357`; its snapshot also includes a historical southern Pacific Coast entry `NOAA:10395`, whose most recent listed assessment is 2017. The report identifies a 2015 assessment, a 2017 catch-only update and a 2023 catch-only projection. Their relationship to the older inventory label needs verification before treating either label as a separate current unit. No *Sebastes goodei* stock was found in the canonical stocks table. The regional [Pacific Fishery Management Council assessment listing](https://www.pcouncil.org/stock-assessments-star-reports-stat-reports-rebuilding-analyses-terms-of-reference/groundfish-stock-assessment-documents/) links the 2025 report and model files.

The 2025 model spans 1875-2024 and ages 0-35+, with sex-disaggregated states, multiple catch fleets and four fishery-independent series. The report states age observations cover 1978-2024 with gaps, and describes survey age samples alongside biomass indices. Its model fits age-at-length data; these samples are not themselves an age-specific survey abundance index. Growth is estimated within the model and female maturity is a function of length. The report describes sex-specific mortality estimation and reports spawning output in **billions of eggs**, with an approximate 95% interval. This is not an SSB-in-tonnes series.

The [public model archive](https://www.pcouncil.org/documents/chilipepper-rockfish-2025-model-files-zip-file/) includes native Stock Synthesis data and control files. Confirm its run identity, fleet-specific age and length samples, survey timing, whether catch numbers-at-age can be defensibly reconstructed using original observations and biology, and whether the archive includes matching fitted numerical outputs. A Stock Synthesis age model and conditional samples alone do not establish eligibility. Keep the coastwide candidate unresolved pending that review; do not count the older southern inventory label as another current stock without further evidence.

The NOAA report, model archive and regional listing are cached under `source_cache/discovery_noaa_chilipepper_2025/`, with URLs, retrieval date and SHA-256 checksums in `sources.csv`. The report's NOAA repository supplies a separately published SHA-512 checksum. This is discovery work only; no canonical assessment records are created.
