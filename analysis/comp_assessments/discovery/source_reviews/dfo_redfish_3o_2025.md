# DFO/NAFO redfish in Division 3O, 2025

## Unit and latest assessment

NAFO treats redfish in Division 3O as one management unit. The fishery reports
deepwater redfish (*Sebastes mentella*) and Acadian redfish (*S. fasciatus*)
collectively, and the 2025 assessment retains the unit as distinct from 3LN.
NAFO SCR Doc. 25/045 is the latest detailed official assessment located.

## Evidence and screening decision

The 2025 assessment is based primarily on research-vessel survey data. It
reports Canadian spring and autumn and EU-Spain survey index trends, fishery
catches, length information, and aggregate status/reference-point outputs
through 2024. Its outputs include survey-biomass index trends and probabilities
relative to stock-status reference points; the report does not provide
age-specific population surfaces. The reviewed report does not document
catch-at-age, an age-specific survey likelihood, age-specific stock weights or
maturity, or age-specific mortality inputs.

The stock is screened out of the age-structured candidate pool because its
latest detailed production assessment is survey-index based and does not report
the required age-specific inputs and outputs. This conclusion is limited to
the assessment materials reviewed; it does not claim that no raw age samples
exist elsewhere. No canonical numerical records were imported.
