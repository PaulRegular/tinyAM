# ICES angel shark in the Northeast Atlantic, 2025 review

ICES unit `agn.27.nea` is angel shark (*Squatina squatina*), for which ICES
provides species-level advice across its Northeast Atlantic range. The
inventory lists 2023 as the latest advice update. The 2025 WGEF report is the
latest detailed source located and covers the species in section 22.

The report documents landings and very sparse discard information, but states
that commercial catch composition and commercial catch-and-effort data are
unavailable. It reports no Northeast Atlantic age-and-growth information and
no benchmarked quantitative stock assessment. An earlier exploratory
Tralee Bay analysis used recreational tagging data to examine population
decline; it is not an age-structured production assessment used for the latest
advice. The reported comparison of historical and contemporary survey catches
does not provide an age-specific abundance index or population-at-age output.

The report confirms that the 2023 advice recommended zero catches for
2024–2027. That precautionary advice is not an estimate of N-at-age, F-at-age,
M-at-age, SSB or recruitment. The unit is **ineligible for the age-disaggregated
inventory** on the production-assessment evidence reviewed. No canonical
numerical records or tinyAM translation were added.
