# ICES Iberian sand sole and Senegalese sole

Review date: 2026-10-30

## Screened units

The 2026 ICES Standard Graphs list identifies both units as category 5 assessments. The stock-specific pages show annual landings or catches and commercial-fleet effort/CPUE data. The reported stock-size, fishing-pressure, F-at-age, and F-at-length fields are blank. The pages do not provide age-specific observations, population-at-age outputs, or a comparable population or mortality estimate.

Both units are therefore **ineligible for the current age-disaggregated assessment comparison** on the published evidence reviewed. This does not establish that biological age samples do not exist; no detailed assessment input files were recovered in this screen.

| ICES stock | Assessment year | Category | Published series | Screen result |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| oal.27.8c9a, Senegalese sole | 2026 | 5 | Landings; polyvalent-fleet effort and CPUE | Ineligible |
| sos.27.8c9a, sand sole | 2026 | 5 | Landings; polyvalent-fleet effort | Ineligible |

The species and divisions are distinct units. Neither has a matching canonical stock record. No database inputs, outputs, or assessments were added.
