# DFO NAFO 4T winter flounder (2017 full assessment)

## Assessment identity

The candidate register includes a DFO Gulf Region unit named Winter Flounder - NAFO 4T. The latest full assessment located is the 2017 statistical catch-at-age assessment, published as Research Document 2019/026, with data through 2016. The 2025 Science Response 2025/010 updates indicators through 2024 and confirms that 2017 remains the last full assessment; it describes a new full assessment as planned for 2026. No 2026 full assessment document was located during this screen. This unit is separate from the three NOAA winter flounder areas and is not an assessment of one of those U.S. units.

## Inputs and assumptions

The full report documents one combined-sex population and 44 years (1973-2016) across ages 2-12+. Inputs include total commercial landings in tonnes, annual commercial catch-at-age proportions in numbers, RV survey trawlable biomass, survey abundance-at-age proportions, mature proportions-at-age, and annual fishery and survey weights-at-age in kg per fish. Catch and survey proportions are reported by age, not as direct catch or abundance numbers-at-age. The annual totals and corresponding weights-at-age permit documented number-scale reconstructions, but the derived values must not be presented as direct source observations.

The model estimates natural mortality in five-year time blocks, with either two age groups (2-4, 5+) or three (2-4, 5-7, 8+). It uses annual fishing mortality and logistic fishery and survey selectivity. The September RV survey is assigned a model timing fraction of 0.71. The report’s maturity-at-age surface is based on survey maturity observations and the age-length key. The 2025 interim update instead reports the survey-derived biomass indicator in kg per tow and does not replace the age-structured inputs from the 2017 assessment.

## Outputs and limitations

The full report presents estimated spawning stock biomass, age-2 and age-3 recruitment, fishing mortality, and time-varying natural mortality. It shows age-specific model results in figures, but no machine-readable fitted object or annual N-at-age output table was found in the reviewed public documents. The 2025 interim update provides the 2024 commercial-size survey indicator and its uncertainty, not updated age-specific population-model outputs.
