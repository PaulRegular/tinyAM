# Thornback ray in ICES Division 7.e

## Identity and latest advice

ICES stock `rjc.27.7e` is the thornback ray unit for the western English
Channel. It is distinct from adjacent thornback ray units, although the 2024
WGEF report notes that its biological stock boundaries are unclear. ICES still
provides advice for Division 7.e separately. No canonical database record uses
this stock code.

The 2024 advice covers 2025–2028 and remains the latest official advice listed
for this unit in the 2026 ICES inventory. The advice and the 2024 WGEF report
both identify a Category 5 approach based on landings, with no quantitative
population assessment.

## Evidence and screening decision

The advice labels the assessment type “No assessment” and the input data
“Landings data”. The WGEF report says the UK-Q1-SWBeam/Q1SWECOS survey provides
preliminary biomass and length-frequency information, but these are qualitative
and were not used in the 2022 or 2024 advice process. No age-specific abundance
index is used in the current advice. There are no age-specific catch, biology,
population, or mortality series reported for this stock's current advice.

**Screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set.** Aggregate
landings and landings advice are available, but the current advice is not an
age-structured population assessment and does not report comparable population
or age-specific outputs. The exploratory survey information does not supply an
age-specific observation series for the official advice. No canonical data or
tinyAM translation were added.
