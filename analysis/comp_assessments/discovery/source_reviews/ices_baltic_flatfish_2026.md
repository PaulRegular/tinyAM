# ICES Baltic Sea stock indicators and flatfish

Review date: 2026-10-30

## Current 2026 records screened

The 2026 ICES Standard Graphs list contains five current Baltic stock records in this group. Their pages report stock-level biomass or stock-size indicators, aggregate catches or landings, and—in some cases—length-based or relative fishing-pressure measures. The published F-at-age and F-at-length fields are blank. None of these pages provides age-specific observed inputs or N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age surfaces. Detailed assessment input files were not recovered in this screen.

These five records are **ineligible for the current age-disaggregated assessment comparison** on the published evidence reviewed. This does not establish that biological age samples do not exist.

| ICES stock | Category | Published indicators | Screen result |
|---|---:|---|---|
| tur.27.22-32, Baltic turbot | 3.2 | Biomass index (kg/hour), landings/catches, length-based pressure proxy | Ineligible |
| bwp.27.2729-32, Baltic flounder | 3 | Fishery stock-size index (numbers per trap), landings, length-based pressure proxy | Ineligible |
| bzq.27.2425, flounder 24–25 | 3.2 | Aggregate SSB, catches/landings, discards | Ineligible |
| bzq.27.2628, flounder 26 and 28 | 2.11 | Biomass and fishing-pressure ratios with uncertainty bounds | Ineligible |
| fle.27.21-23, flounder 21–23 | 2.11 | Biomass and fishing-pressure ratios with uncertainty bounds | Ineligible |

The other five unstarted Baltic entries in the source inventory—two salmon units, sea trout, dab, and brill—do not appear in the 2026 Standard Graphs list. They remain unreviewed for current-unit identity and assessment-source coverage; absence from this list is not treated as proof that no current assessment exists.

The five screened species/area units have no matching canonical stock records. No canonical numerical records were added.
