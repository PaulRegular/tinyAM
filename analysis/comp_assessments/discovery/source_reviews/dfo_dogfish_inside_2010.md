# Pacific Spiny Dogfish, Inside BC

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO manages Pacific Spiny Dogfish (currently named *Squalus suckleyi*) as separate inside and outside British Columbia units. This record is the inside unit in the Strait of Georgia and Johnstone Strait (DFO GMA 4B and Statistical Area 12). No corresponding canonical tinyAM stock record was found.

## Current assessment and age-data screen

The latest detailed assessment for the inside unit located is the 2010 assessment, published as Research Document 2011/034. DFO's 2025/032 advice report states that the 2024 assessment concerns outside waters and that the last assessment for Dogfish had been completed in 2010. DFO's Pacific Dogfish research page continues to list the 2010 research document and related proceedings and advice. The 2010 report treats inside and outside waters as separate stocks. Its historical title uses *Squalus acanthias*; current DFO sources use *Squalus suckleyi*.

The 2010 assessment uses generalized Schaefer and Pella-Tomlinson surplus-production models, with catch and aggregate catch-per-unit-effort indices. The report explicitly says that the paucity of catch-age data precludes an age-structured model. Its inputs include commercial catch and CPUE series and, for the inside stock, an abundance index from Strait of Georgia longline surveys. The model does not use annual catch-at-age or age-specific survey observations. Length and age biology described in the source does not supply the missing age-indexed likelihood data.

The detailed report gives inside-stock biomass, depletion, MSY and FMSY estimates for alternative production-model runs, including B2009 in Table 15. The assessment's peer review did not reach consensus on a model-based yield recommendation; the advice report describes the separate catch-based advice. These outputs do not include population-at-age or F-at-age surfaces. This is a source-screening conclusion, not a claim that no biological age observations exist for the species.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct, new DFO inside-water unit, screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated candidate set. The decisive limitation is explicit in the assessment: catch-age data were insufficient to fit an age-structured model. No annual catch-at-age or age-specific survey likelihood inputs can be reconstructed from the aggregate CPUE series. No canonical input or output data were imported.
