# ICES Northeast Atlantic mackerel: discovery review

ICES SID identifies `mac.27.nea`, *Scomber scombrus*, in subareas 1-8 and 14 and divisions 9.a, 12.a and 12.b. It is geographically distinct from the existing Northwest Atlantic mackerel records `nefsc_atlantic_mackerel` and `ck_dfo_qc_scombrus_4t`. The [2026 ICES advice](https://asd.ices.dk/viewAdvice/7228), published 30 September 2026, identifies assessment key 25875. Its scenario values are forecasts and should not be treated as historical fitted outputs.

The [2026 assessment code](https://github.com/ices-advice/2026_mac.27.nea/tree/708b4fcd2e7b5a14206beec1806015a7fbecbfda/2026_mac.27.nea_assessment) describes a SAM model, distinguishes data/model revisions and uses model 0.01 in its forecast output script. Its `data0.01.r` reads catch numbers, stock and catch weights, landings and discard weights, proportions mature, natural mortality, survey observations and spawning fractions. It also reads steel-tag recaptures. The repository bibliography identifies the 2026 WGWIDE inputs as local files, but those numerical files and fitted objects are not present in the pinned repository archive. Code confirms intended data types, not their recoverability, values, units or final model selection.

The [2026 Icelandic marine research advice reproduction](https://www.hafogvatn.is/static/extras/images/36_mac_2026_1_advice_en.html) describes the current model's inputs: egg survey SSB index in 1992-2025, RFID tagging age indices 2-11 in 2013-2025, and IESSNS indices ages 3-11 in 2010 and 2012-2026 and age 2 in 2018-2026. These are distinct input types, and tagging observations should not be represented as ordinary trawl observations. Survey timing, catch and biology units, historical input matrices, and age-specific N and F must be verified before classifying eligibility.

The pinned repository archive and the two advice pages are cached in `source_cache/discovery_ices_mackerel_nea_2026/`, with retrieval dates and SHA-256 checksums in `sources.csv`. The repository revision is `708b4fcd2e7b5a14206beec1806015a7fbecbfda`. A bounded search of public stockassessment.org listings and the exact source filenames did not locate run-matched 2026 numerical input objects; the visible mackerel directories are historical. The 2025 assessment data must not be substituted into the 2026 run. This review does not add a canonical assessment record.

The official [ICES stock assessment graph source data, key 25875](https://standardgraphs.ices.dk/ViewSourceData.aspx?key=25875), provides numerical historical SSB and total biomass in tonnes, age-2 recruitment in thousands, and average F for ages 4-8 for 1998-2025, with low and high values; it also lists SSB, total biomass and recruitment for 2026. Confirm the interval level and point-estimate convention before import. This resolves the lack of an official aggregate historical series, but not the age-specific input matrices or age-specific fitted outputs. The source page is cached as `sag_25875.html`, SHA-256 `FA0C9E5A0F9F908BECD93B509226A1B4A9446F77F22248CE3E855AE6744D8A9B`, retrieved 2026-10-28.


Screening is complete, but eligibility remains unresolved. The public code confirms age-disaggregated catch and biological inputs are expected, and the official graph provides aggregate outputs. The numerical production inputs, age-specific fitted outputs, exact timing and point-estimate/interval definitions are not verified from the available 2026 sources. The RFID tagging series is also not directly interchangeable with a conventional survey-index likelihood.
