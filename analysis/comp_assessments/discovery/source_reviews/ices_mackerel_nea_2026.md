# ICES Northeast Atlantic mackerel: discovery review

ICES SID identifies `mac.27.nea`, *Scomber scombrus*, in subareas 1-8 and 14 and divisions 9.a, 12.a and 12.b. It is geographically distinct from the existing Northwest Atlantic mackerel records `nefsc_atlantic_mackerel` and `ck_dfo_qc_scombrus_4t`. The [2026 ICES advice](https://asd.ices.dk/viewAdvice/7228), published 30 September 2026, identifies assessment key 25875. Its scenario values are forecasts and should not be treated as historical fitted outputs.

The [2026 assessment code](https://github.com/ices-advice/2026_mac.27.nea/tree/708b4fcd2e7b5a14206beec1806015a7fbecbfda/2026_mac.27.nea_assessment) describes a SAM model, distinguishes data/model revisions and uses model 0.01 in its forecast output script. Its `data0.01.r` reads catch numbers, stock and catch weights, landings and discard weights, proportions mature, natural mortality, survey observations and spawning fractions. It also reads steel-tag recaptures. The repository bibliography identifies the 2026 WGWIDE inputs as local files, but those numerical files and fitted objects are not present in the pinned repository archive. Code confirms intended data types, not their recoverability, values, units or final model selection.

The [2026 Icelandic marine research advice reproduction](https://www.hafogvatn.is/static/extras/images/36_mac_2026_1_advice_en.html) describes the current model's inputs: egg survey SSB index in 1992-2025, RFID tagging age indices 2-11 in 2013-2025, and IESSNS indices ages 3-11 in 2010 and 2012-2026 and age 2 in 2018-2026. These are distinct input types, and tagging observations should not be represented as ordinary trawl observations. Survey timing, catch and biology units, historical matrices, and run-specific numerical SSB, recruitment, N and F must be verified before classifying eligibility.

The pinned repository archive and the two advice pages are cached in `source_cache/discovery_ices_mackerel_nea_2026/`, with retrieval dates and SHA-256 checksums in `sources.csv`. The repository revision is `708b4fcd2e7b5a14206beec1806015a7fbecbfda`. Continue searching the 2026 WGWIDE report, assessment graph data and any public SAM run or TAF data archive; the 2025 assessment data must not be silently substituted into the 2026 run. This review does not add a canonical assessment record.
