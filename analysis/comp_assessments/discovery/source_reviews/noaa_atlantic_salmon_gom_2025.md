# NOAA Gulf of Maine Atlantic salmon (Stock SMART 10498)

## Unit and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART identifies 10498 as the Gulf of Maine Atlantic salmon unit
(*Salmo salar*). Its latest pinned Stock SMART entry is the 2024 Index Method
assessment, using data through 2023 and marked Full acceptance. This entry is
not an age-structured fishery assessment.

The most recent detailed annual report found is USASAC Report 37, published in
2025 for 2024 activities. It reports 1,517 adult returns to the Gulf of Maine
Distinct Population Segment in 2024: 1,386 hatchery-origin and 131 naturally
reared fish. The report uses trap counts and redd-count estimates, and provides
selected summaries by sea age and origin, including smolt-to-adult return
rates.

## Inputs, assumptions and outputs

NOAA states that directed Atlantic salmon fisheries in U.S. waters are closed
and possession is prohibited. Monitored bycatch is uncommon. The assessment
therefore has no managed fishery catch-at-age series. Adult river returns are
population-monitoring quantities; age-specific returns and return-rate
indicators do not form a fishery catch-at-age and independent survey likelihood
for an age-structured population model.

The latest assessment does not report weight-at-age or maturity-at-age inputs
for an age-structured population model, mortality-at-age estimates, or
population N-at-age, SSB, recruitment, F-at-age or M-at-age surfaces. The sea
age and origin categories are useful life-history and survival information,
but they are not equivalent to modeled abundance-at-age. This screening does
not claim that biological age samples are absent outside the assessment.

**Eligibility:** ineligible for the age-structured comparison set. The current
production method and publicly reported outputs do not supply the required
assessment inputs and comparable age-structured population outputs. No
canonical numerical records were added.

## Identity and source limitations

NOAA Stock SMART 10498 is kept separate from the broader ICES North American
Atlantic salmon candidate; their geographic units are not a one-to-one match.

adult returns, adult and juvenile stocking, egg production and aquaculture
records. The local database ZIP is cached with its SHA-256 in the ignored
