# Pacific grenadier, U.S. Pacific Coast (NOAA:10526)

Reviewed 2026-11-01. NOAA Stock SMART identifies Stock 10526 as Pacific grenadier (*Coryphaenoides acrolepis*) under the Pacific Fishery Management Council. Its latest listed detailed assessment is the 2010 DB-SRA, using data through 2009, with a full-acceptance review result. No canonical record for this U.S. unit was found.

The primary 2010 PFMC/NOAA report says grenadiers were managed in the "other fish" complex and generally landed in unspecified grenadier market categories. Because species-composition data were unavailable, the assessment reconstructed catch for the aggregate grenadier complex and applied DB-SRA to that complex using life-history attributes assumed similar to Pacific grenadier. Thus, the accepted method and outputs do not constitute a Pacific-grenadier-specific age-structured assessment. The 2024 Groundfish SAFE (published in 2025) provides current stock and fishery context but no newer Pacific grenadier age-structured model or species-specific population-at-age outputs.

## Age-disaggregated screening

- **Catch:** the 2010 report contains complex-level catch reconstruction; it does not identify Pacific-grenadier catch-at-age or a species-specific age composition.
- **Survey:** the DB-SRA is catch-based and does not use an age-specific abundance index. No Pacific-grenadier age-indexed survey input was found in the reviewed current SAFE.
- **Biology and mortality:** the report uses Pacific grenadier life-history attributes as proxies for the grenadier complex, but no assessment-ready weight-at-age, maturity-at-age, or age-specific mortality surface was found.
- **Outputs:** the report gives sustainable-yield/OFL results for the grenadier complex. It does not report Pacific-grenadier-specific N-at-age, F-at-age, SSB, or recruitment.
- **Unit boundary:** NOAA's candidate stock is the current PFMC Pacific grenadier species unit; the 2010 DB-SRA output is for a multispecies grenadier complex. These are not interchangeable units.

Screening result: **new current stock unit; ineligible for the age-disaggregated source set on reviewed evidence**. This reflects the form and unit of the available production assessment, not a claim that no Pacific grenadier age samples exist for other purposes. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added.
