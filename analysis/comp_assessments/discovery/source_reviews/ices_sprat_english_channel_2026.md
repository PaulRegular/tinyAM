# ICES English Channel sprat (spr.27.7de), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

This is the ICES unit for Sprat (*Sprattus sprattus*) in divisions 7.d and 7.e.
It is distinct from the Baltic sprat unit already in the database. The latest
advice located is the 2026 ICES Herring Assessment Working Group assessment,
with advice record 4076 and Stock Assessment Graphs key 21306. The advice covers
1 July 2026 through 30 June 2027 and is based on the 2025 survey.

## Assessment evidence

ICES classifies the 2026 assessment as Category 3.3. The HAWG report describes
the English Channel stock as a data-limited assessment based on the PELTIC
survey biomass and a constant harvest-rate (CHR) rule. The 2026 ICES graph
metadata describes the stock-size quantity as a biomass index in tonnes. HAWG
reports a 2025 biomass index of 91,577 tonnes; the advice is for catches of no
more than 7,848 tonnes.

The ICES Working Group on International Pelagic Surveys (WGIPS) 2025 report
states that PELTIC collects additional age and length composition for sprat,
but that those data are not yet used in the assessment. This distinction is
important: biological age information exists in the survey program, but the
production assessment does not use age-specific abundance observations or an
age-structured population likelihood. The 2026 outputs provide an aggregate
biomass index and harvest-rate/advice quantities, not N-at-age, recruitment,
F-at-age or M-at-age population trajectories. The graph metadata labels stock
size “Biomass index”; a table heading that says “SSB” is not treated as a
spawning-biomass estimate.

The reviewed assessment does not use catch-at-age, age-specific weight or
maturity inputs, or an age-specific natural-mortality surface. PELTIC is an
annual October survey and its preceding autumn biomass informs the spring
advice; an exact within-year fraction was not found in the reviewed sources.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. Its
current official method is aggregate-index based, and the collected age and
length compositions are explicitly not used in the assessment. No earlier
assessment was substituted, and no canonical stock, assessment, input or
output rows were added. This screen is about the assessment's inputs and
outputs, not whether survey age data exist or whether the aggregate assessment
is scientifically useful.
