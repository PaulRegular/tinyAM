# ICES sandeel Area 5r

**Stock code:** `san.sa.5r` (Ammodytes spp.), northern North Sea, Viking and Bergen banks
**Latest advice located:** 2025, applying to 2025 and 2026
**Screening result:** ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database identifies the 2025 assessment as
Category 5 and recommends zero catches in 2025 and 2026. The 2025 Herring
Assessment Working Group report describes Area 5r under the Category 5.4
method, for which catch data are the only available assessment data. The
official ICES stock-graph record provides annual catches for 1983–2024 and
does not provide age-specific catch or survey observations, weights-at-age,
maturity-at-age, age-specific mortality, or population-at-age outputs.

The record therefore does not support the age-disaggregated inputs and
population outputs required for the current comparison set. Catch advice is
available, but it is not a comparable population-state estimate. No canonical
assessment, input, or output rows were added.
