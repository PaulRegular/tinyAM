# ICES white skate in the Northeast Atlantic, 2025 review

ICES unit `rja.27.nea` is white skate (*Rostroraja alba*) across subareas 1–10,
12 and 14. The inventory lists 2023 as its latest advice update. The 2025
WGEF report is the latest detailed review located and covers the stock in
section 23.

WGEF states that no exploratory or formal stock assessment has been
undertaken. It describes scarce and unreliable nominal landings, limited
discard observations, no commercial catch-composition data, and very rare
trawl-survey encounters. The report says existing surveys are not appropriate
for monitoring abundance. Reported life-history information for Northeast
Atlantic white skate is largely unknown; it does not supply age-structured
model inputs or outputs.

The 2025 report confirms that the latest advice was issued in 2023 and
recommended zero catches for 2024–2027 under the precautionary approach. This
conservation recommendation is not an estimate of population-at-age, SSB or
recruitment. The unit is **ineligible for the age-disaggregated inventory** on
the production-assessment evidence reviewed. No canonical numerical records or
tinyAM translation were added.
