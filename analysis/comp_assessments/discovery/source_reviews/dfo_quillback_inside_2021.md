# DFO Inside Quillback Rockfish

## Assessment unit and run

This is the distinct British Columbia Inside management unit in Groundfish Management Area 4B. The latest detailed assessment located is the 2021 management-procedure analysis, published as DFO Research Document 2024/017, with its peer-reviewed Science Advisory Report published as 2023/033. The analysis uses a conditioning model to inform management-procedure operating models; it is not a conventional annual production assessment.

## Inputs and outputs found

The conditioning model uses commercial and recreational catches and composition data, age compositions from the inside hard-bottom longline (HBLL) and Jig Area 12 research surveys, and aggregate abundance indices from those two surveys. The age-composition likelihood uses capped sample sizes of 100 for HBLL and 50 for Jig Area 12. Commercial age samples are documented for 1984–1994, 1996, and 2000–2001; the report says no commercial age samples were collected after 2001. This evidence does not establish a complete numeric annual catch-at-age series.

The report describes female maturity as an age-based input estimated from survey biology, and commercial mean weight is used. The complete run-matched numeric weight-at-age and maturity-at-age vectors were not recovered in machine-readable form. Natural mortality is not estimated as one annual series; reference operating models use mean values of 0.055, 0.067, and 0.088 per year. The report identifies the surveys but does not establish a model-scale fractional timing for each index.

Outputs include spawning-biomass, depletion and fishing-mortality trajectories, recruitment summaries, survey fits, and age-composition fits across operating-model replicates. Numeric, run-matched annual N-at-age and F-at-age arrays were not located. Survey age proportions are composition data, not absolute age-specific abundance indices.

## Screening decision

The unit is a verified provisional new DFO stock unit, but eligibility for the age-input set remains unresolved. The sources establish that age compositions inform the conditioning model; they do not establish a complete annual catch-at-age series or recoverable run-matched numeric age inputs and outputs. The age samples are also incomplete across the commercial time series. No canonical database values were added.
