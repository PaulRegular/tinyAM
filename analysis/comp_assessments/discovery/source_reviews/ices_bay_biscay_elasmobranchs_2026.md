# ICES Bay of Biscay and Iberian elasmobranchs: current age-data screen

Review date: 2026-10-30

## Screened units

The 2026 ICES stock-specific tables for seven thornback, spotted, blonde, and cuckoo ray units publish aggregate biomass or exploitable-biomass indicators, landings/catches, and relative or length-based fishing-pressure measures. Their F-at-age and F-at-length fields are blank; none of these tables reports N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age surfaces. The 2024 advice for three undulate-ray units is data category 6 and provides stock-level catch/landing advice rather than an age-structured population assessment.

The ten entries are therefore **ineligible for the current age-disaggregated assessment comparison**. This is a finding about the information in the latest advice-linked public records reviewed. It does not establish that no biological age samples exist, and detailed assessment-input files were not recovered in this screen.

The flatfish, anchovy, European seabass, and sole inventory entries in the same regional list were not part of this screen; their assessment methods need separate review.
