# Gulf of Alaska Atka mackerel (NOAA:10465): current-unit screen

## Current unit and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART lists the Gulf of Alaska (GOA) unit separately from Bering Sea/Aleutian Islands (BSAI) Atka mackerel, but its GOA assessment year is stale (2016). The latest accepted full GOA assessment located is the 2021 chapter. The December 2025 chapter is a catch report, and the December 2025 Scientific and Statistical Committee (SSC) report confirms that the planned 2025 operational assessment was not completed during the federal funding lapse. The GOA and BSAI units remain distinct.

## Age data, assumptions, and outputs

The 2021 chapter says the GOA stock is managed under Tier 6 because reliable biomass estimates are unavailable, and explicitly states that no population assessment model is used. Its recommended OFL is based on average catch from 1978–1995, with a maximum permissible ABC of 75% of OFL. The 2025 catch report carries those specifications forward.

The report presents sparse fishery otolith ages from historical directed fisheries and recent fisheries, plus survey otolith samples from 2017, 2019, and 2021. It does not fit an age-structured population model or report population N-at-age, F-at-age, recruitment, or spawning biomass. Survey biomass estimates are discussed, but the authors reject them as reliable measures or trends because catches are highly patchy and dominated by single hauls. Those quantities therefore do not provide a comparable age-structured abundance output.

The chapter gives a fixed natural-mortality assumption of 0.3 and published age and length maturity schedules. It also describes historical growth and weight-length relationships. These life-history details do not change the absence of age-structured model inputs and outputs for the current assessment. There is no modeled survey timing to translate into an age-specific index.

## Screening decision

Treat NOAA:10465 as one distinct GOA unit and screen it as ineligible for the age-disaggregated comparison set. Its sparse sampled ages and life-history tables are useful descriptive source data, but they are not age-structured assessment inputs, and the latest accepted assessment has no population model or comparable fitted population outputs. Do not create canonical assessment, input, or output records from the catch-limit report.
