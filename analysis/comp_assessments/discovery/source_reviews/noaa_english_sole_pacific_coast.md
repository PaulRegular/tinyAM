# NOAA Pacific Coast English Sole

## Stock identity and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART ID 10046 is the U.S. Pacific Coast English sole stock, Parophrys vetulus. The stock definition remains coastwide under Pacific Fishery Management Council Amendment 35. No corresponding canonical database stock or cross-inventory match was found, so the candidate is provisionally new.

The latest full assessment located is the coastwide data-moderate assessment conducted in 2013 and published in 2015. The 2019 work was a catch-only projection update based on the 2013 assessment, not a new age-structured assessment. Council planning material states that 2013 was still the latest assessment in 2024 and that the stock was being considered for a future benchmark. Stock SMART's assessment year of 2013 agrees with this run identification.

The 2013 assessment report, a 2024 Council assessment-priority document and NOAA's 2025 stock-definition analysis are cached under analysis/comp_assessments/source_cache/discovery_noaa_english_sole_2013/. The local sources.csv records source URLs, retrieval dates, filenames and SHA-256 checksums.

## Age-input screen

The 2013 assessment uses catch and aggregate survey indices only. It is a data-moderate assessment, not an age-structured population fit, and it does not include fishery catch-at-age, fishery age compositions or age-specific survey indices in its likelihood. The Council's 2024 source review explicitly distinguishes this run from older full assessments that used age and length composition data.

The 2024 Council document reports that substantial commercial and NWFSC survey ages exist historically. Those samples were not part of the 2013 model likelihood and cannot be treated as run-matched inputs. The 2005 benchmark and 2007 update are for the same coastwide unit, but the current-unit rule does not permit replacing the latest assessment with those older model inputs merely to obtain an age-based case.

The 2013 report contains stock-level spawning-biomass/depletion estimates and catch decision projections. Council sources identify a 2019 catch-only projection update used for harvest management; its run-specific projection files were not located in this review. Neither source provides a verified age-specific N, F or M series for the current assessment.

The reviewed source set does not establish a run-matched age-specific weight, maturity or mortality schedule for the 2013 assessment. The assessment uses NWFSC West Coast Groundfish Bottom Trawl Survey indices, but the exact fractional within-year timing was not verified from the current run files. These unresolved at-age details do not change the screen decision because the latest assessment has no age-composition likelihood to support the required age-specific inputs.

## Decision

Screening decision: ineligible for the current age-specific source set. Catch totals and aggregate survey indices are recoverable, and stock-level outputs are available, but the latest assessment has no catch-at-age or age-composition likelihood. Do not substitute the earlier 2005/2007 age-structured assessments or use historical read ages as if they were inputs to the 2013 run. Keep the 2019 projection output gap separate from the age-input exclusion.
