# Blackgill rockfish (NOAA:10404): 2017 source and identity screen

## Current unit and assessment identity

NOAA Stock SMART lists identifier 10404 as Blackgill rockfish in Southern California, with a 2019 assessment year. The latest detailed assessment located is the 2017 update for Blackgill rockfish in the Conception and Monterey INPFC areas. That report models those areas as one stock. The 2026 Pacific Coast Groundfish FMP defines Blackgill rockfish as a current Pacific West Coast stock unit. The NOAA inventory label, the report area, and the current FMP unit therefore do not yet establish a safe one-to-one crosswalk.

## Data and output evidence

The 2017 report describes an integrated length- and age-structured Stock Synthesis model using data through 2016. It reports three commercial fleets, length compositions, an abundance index, updated maturity and fecundity information, and fixed sex-specific natural mortality of 0.063 for females and 0.065 for males. Commercial age-composition data were carried forward unchanged from 2011. New NWFSC survey age observations were used as conditional age-at-length data to inform growth, not as a direct age-specific abundance index.

The report provides annual aggregate spawning output, recruitment, and depletion estimates. Spawning output is larval production, not SSB in tonnes. Numeric age-specific population outputs and a run-matched public model archive were not located. The Council’s document archive lists the 2017 assessment report but does not list a corresponding model-file archive. The report says some CAAL fits are available in an r4ss output package on request.

There is also a source-description conflict: the 2026 Amendment 37 stock-definition analysis describes the 2017 work as catch-only projections with updated maturity and fecundity, while the 2017 report describes an updated integrated age- and length-structured model. This needs reconciliation before selecting a run for curation.

## Screening decision

Mark NOAA:10404 as identity-ambiguous and eligibility-unresolved. Do not assign it to the current Pacific West Coast unit or import it until the inventory-to-assessment boundary and the 2017 run’s production status are reconciled. The age data and aggregate outputs described in the report are useful evidence, but they do not establish that the complete run-matched age inputs and age-specific outputs are available.
