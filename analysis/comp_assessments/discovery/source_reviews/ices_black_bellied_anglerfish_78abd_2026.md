# ICES black-bellied anglerfish (ank.27.78abd), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database identifies `ank.27.78abd` as the
black-bellied anglerfish unit in Subarea 7 and divisions 8.a, 8.b and 8.d.
The latest advice located is record 5112, based on the 2026 Category 1
assessment and linked to Stock Assessment Graphs key 23768.

## Assessment evidence

WGBIE 2026 describes the production assessment as a **length-based,
age-structured Stock Synthesis 3 model**. It uses landings and discards,
catch numbers-at-length, survey indices and length distributions. In 2025,
65% of landings had length data associated with them. The assessment combines
the French EVHOE-WIBTS-Q4 and Irish IGFS-WIBTS-Q4 surveys as FR_IE_IBTS, and
also uses the Irish IAMS survey. IAMS occurs early in the year but is entered
as an index on 31 December of the preceding year to include it in the in-year
assessment.

The production report says maturity, natural mortality, growth and length-
weight parameters are fixed and documented in the Stock Annex. This review did
not recover a run-matched catch-at-age matrix, survey age index, age composition,
or weight-at-age series. It did not recover the Stock Annex's numeric biological
arrays, so those details remain unresolved rather than inferred.

The 2026 Stock Assessment Graphs data report age-0 recruitment (in thousands),
combined-sex SSB (tonnes), and F for ages 3–10. These are useful aggregate
outputs but do not provide N-at-age, F-at-age or M-at-age surfaces.

## Screening decision

This current ICES unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated candidate
set. Its accepted production model has latent age structure, but the documented
likelihood observations are at length and the publicly reported population
outputs are aggregate or age-group summaries. No canonical assessment, input
or output rows were added.
