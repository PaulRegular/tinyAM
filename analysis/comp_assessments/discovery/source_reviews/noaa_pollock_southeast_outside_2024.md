# NOAA Stock SMART 10514: Southeast Outside Gulf of Alaska pollock

Reviewed 2026-10-29. Southeast Outside is the separately managed Gulf of Alaska pollock stock east of 140° W. It is not represented by the canonical Gulf of Alaska record, which covers the C/W/WYK stock west of 140° W.

## Assessment and current status

The latest detailed unit-specific production assessment located is the 2024 GOA SAFE report, with the Southeast Outside survey series ending in 2023. The Council approved that SAFE and recommended the 2025-2026 harvest specifications in December 2024; later specification materials continue to refer to the 2024 SAFE. NOAA's management page lists Southeast Outside and C/W/WYK as separate pollock stocks and still labels the latest Southeast Outside assessment as 2023. The detailed report/run year and terminal survey year are therefore recorded separately.

## Inputs, assumptions, and outputs

The 2024 report distinguishes the age-structured C/W/WYK assessment from the Southeast Outside assessment. C/W/WYK is modeled with age-specific catch, survey age compositions and biological inputs, and its survey and age-at-age inputs are limited to the area west of 140° W. Southeast Outside is assessed separately under Tier 5 using aggregate NMFS bottom-trawl biomass estimates from 1990-2023 and a random-effects smoother. The assessment appendix does not provide catch-at-age or an age-specific survey index for Southeast Outside.

The Tier 5 calculation uses scalar M = 0.30, with FABC = 0.75 M and FOFL = M. The accepted report gives smoothed biomass of 43,328 t and 2025/2026 ABC of 9,749 t and OFL of 12,998 t. No age-specific population or fishing-mortality outputs are reported for this unit. Weight-at-age and maturity-at-age inputs are not required or reported for the Tier 5 model.

This unit is screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment pool. That screening applies to the current Southeast Outside production method and does not imply that age observations are absent from the broader C/W/WYK assessment.

No assessment or numeric series has been imported.
