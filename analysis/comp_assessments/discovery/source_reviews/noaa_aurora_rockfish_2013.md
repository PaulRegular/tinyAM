# NOAA Aurora rockfish - Pacific Coast (Stock SMART 10547)

## Screening decision

**Identity:** new NOAA/PFMC candidate; no matching canonical tinyAM stock was found.

**Latest assessment:** the accepted coastwide 2013 Stock Synthesis assessment, with data through 2012. The 2025 Groundfish SAFE and NMFS's February 2026 assessment-priority report still identify 2013 as the most recent assessment. The Council's Amendment 37 action defines Aurora rockfish as coastwide in the Pacific Coast Groundfish FMP; NMFS review of that amendment was still underway at the time of this review. The 2013 model also treated the U.S. Pacific Coast population as a single stock, off Washington, Oregon and California. No separate older stock-unit fallback is indicated by the evidence reviewed.

**Age-input eligibility:** unresolved. The report includes detailed fishery and survey length compositions and observed conditional age-at-length samples, but it does not use or report annual fishery catch-at-age. Fishery age-at-length samples are limited to the trawl fishery in 2003, 2008 and 2009. A catch-at-age reconstruction would need a documented age-length key and weight conversion that is valid across years. The report's fitted growth parameters should not be used to manufacture input observations. The report's survey age samples are also conditional age-at-length compositions, not direct annual age-specific abundance indices.

**Do not count as eligible or excluded yet.** Resolve whether the reported source quantities support an observation-only catch-at-age reconstruction under the database protocol. If reconstruction requires fitted growth parameters or unsupported extrapolation across years, classify the candidate ineligible for the age-disaggregated set.

## Assessment and data evidence

The 2013 report identifies the run as Stock Synthesis 3.24o, with commercial landings for 1916-2012 and fishery length compositions for 1978-2012. The input file has two fishery fleets, four survey indices, one area, two sexes and catch in biomass units (metric tons). All fleet and survey timing values in the model input are 0.5. This is the assessment's within-year setting, not evidence of the actual survey sampling fraction.

The domestic trawl fishery supplied conditional age-at-length observations in 2003, 2008 and 2009. The assessment used 61 observed age bins, 0-60; the population dynamics input uses 80 ages. Survey age observations came from the NWFSC shelf-slope survey in 2003, 2005, 2007 and 2009-2012. They were conditional ages-at-length, with length compositions and survey abundance indices. The report does not provide annual age-specific survey abundance as a directly observed series.

The source documents sex-specific growth and length-weight relationships and length-based maturity. Natural mortality was fixed at 0.0350 per year for females and 0.0371 for males. Numeric age-specific weight and maturity surfaces are not supplied as standalone observed input series; they would require an explicitly documented translation from the source biological relationships.

The accepted assessment's Table 13 reports annual total and summary biomass, spawning biomass, depletion, age-0 recruitment (thousands of fish), and exploitation rate for 1916-2012. The report provides point estimates for these summary series. It does not provide annual numeric N-at-age or F-at-age tables in the reviewed materials. The 2025 SAFE repeats management quantities based on the 2013 assessment; it is not a new fit.
