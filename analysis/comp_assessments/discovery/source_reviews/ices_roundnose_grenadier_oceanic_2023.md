# Oceanic Northeast Atlantic roundnose grenadier: 2023 advice screen

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**Official stock code:** `rng.27.5a10b12ac14b`
**Scientific name:** *Coryphaenoides rupestris*
**Assessment area:** Oceanic Northeast Atlantic and northern Reykjanes Ridge (Divisions 10.b and 12.c; Subdivisions 12.a.1, 14.b.1, and 5.a.1)

## Stock identity and current advice

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database identifies this current roundnose
grenadier unit in the Oceanic Northeast Atlantic and northern Reykjanes Ridge.
Its latest advice record is assessment year 2023, classified as category 5 and
active through 2027. The advice limits landings to 459 tonnes in each year
2024–2027 and says the corresponding catches cannot be quantified. ICES defines
category 5 stocks as landings-only. This is a distinct unit from roundnose
grenadier in Division 3.a and from the North Sea and western-area assessment
units.

## Inputs and outputs

The reviewed current advice is landings-only. It does not provide catch-at-age,
age compositions, age-specific survey indices, age-specific biology, or fitted
age-structured population outputs. No recruitment, SSB, N-at-age, or F-at-age
series was identified in the current advice record. No separate detailed
assessment report or model files were located in this review. This evidence
does not support a tinyAM age-structured translation.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new ICES unit, screened **ineligible** for the current
age-disaggregated source-curation set because the current ICES category is
landings-only and the reviewed advice provides no age-disaggregated inputs or
fitted population outputs. This is a source-availability screen; it does not
assert that the species lacks biological age data outside this assessment. No
canonical stock, assessment, input, output, or tinyAM translation records were
added.
