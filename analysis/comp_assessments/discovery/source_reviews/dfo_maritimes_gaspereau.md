# DFO Maritimes Gaspereau inventory entry

## Identity and latest assessment

The DFO Sustainability Survey lists “Gaspereau” for the Maritimes Region. This
is a fishery label, not one stock unit: DFO uses “gaspereau” for alewife
(*Alosa pseudoharengus*) and blueback herring (*Alosa aestivalis*) harvested
from many river systems. DFO’s framework treats populations of each species
in individual rivers as separate units. The 2021 regional management plan also
describes separate river fisheries and identifies the Gaspereau and Tusket
rivers as distinct assessment cases.

The newest specific assessment process located is “Framework Review and
Assessment of Alewife in the Gaspereau River, Nova Scotia,” held in 2025 and
2026. DFO’s 2025–2026 work-plan results say a limit reference point for this
Alewife unit was developed and formally accepted. The current work plan says
development of its upper stock reference continues. The official science
schedule still lists a Research Document as an expected publication; I did not
find that detailed report in the public sources available on 2026-10-30.

This is useful evidence of a current assessment for one river and one species,
but it does not establish that the broad inventory entry is a single stock or
that the detailed assessment inputs and outputs are publicly available. Keep
the regional row unlinked and create a separate Gaspereau River Alewife
candidate if its assessment materials become available. Do not combine the
separate Tusket River assessment with it.

## Source evidence and screening

The 2016 Maritimes framework describes individual river populations as the
appropriate assessment scale, distinguishes fishery-dependent catch-curve
analyses from longer-term statistical catch-at-age models, and identifies
limitations in apportioning mixed-species and mixed-stock landings. The 2021
management plan reports river-specific escapement and run-size monitoring and
notes that most Maritimes river herring populations were not regularly
assessed. It presents historical Gaspereau River results and separate Tusket
River indicators, not a common regional population series.

For the broad “Gaspereau” row, no run-matched catch-at-age, age-specific survey
index, current weights-at-age, or maturity-at-age series was verified. The
2025–2026 LRP is a reference-point result for Gaspereau River Alewife, not a
complete age-specific output set for the regional fishery. No annual N-, F-,
or M-at-age or comparable recruitment and SSB series was verified for this
inventory row. The source information is therefore insufficient to decide
age-input eligibility for the specific new assessment.

**Identity is ambiguous; age-input eligibility remains unresolved.** The row
is screened, but no candidate stock unit, canonical stock, assessment, input,
or output record is created. Follow up when DFO releases the detailed Research
Document and assess the Gaspereau River Alewife unit separately.
