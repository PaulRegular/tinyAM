# Atlantic white marlin (NOAA:10955; ICCAT 2025 assessment)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**Scientific name:** *Kajikia albida*
**Assessment unit:** Atlantic white marlin; the 2025 assessment combines white marlin and roundscale spearfish because catches and species identification overlap.
**Latest NOAA Stock SMART assessment:** 2025; operational; full acceptance; JABBA; data through 2023.

NOAA Stock SMART ID 10955 is the Atlantic-wide inventory row. No canonical tinyAM stock matches it. The 2025 ICCAT assessment is the current run. ICCAT's meeting report says the report's conclusions were preliminary pending SCRS adoption, while the current ICCAT assessment listing identifies the 2025 JABBA model as the model used for advice. The meeting also evaluated Stock Synthesis, but explicitly decided not to use its results for stock-status determination because of poor fits, conflicting indices, and strong retrospective patterns.

## Inputs and outputs

The assessment uses annual total removals by fleet, including catches, dead discards and estimated post-release mortality, plus aggregate fleet CPUE series and length compositions. The 2025 assessment used data through 2023. It does not document catch-at-age, an age-composition likelihood, or age-specific abundance observations. The Stock Synthesis model is internally age-structured, but that does not make its fitted observations age-disaggregated.

The reviewed reports do not provide a run-matched weight-at-age or maturity-at-age matrix. Maturity is described by length-at-50%-maturity and the JABBA life-history prior uses a single M value with uncertainty; the report recommends developing an age-specific M vector in future work. The fitted indices are fishery CPUE, so a survey timing fraction is not applicable.

The selected JABBA results report aggregate biomass and fishing-mortality status relative to reference points, including annual B/BMSY and F/FMSY with credibility intervals. Numeric annual N-at-age and F-at-age surfaces are not reported. Stock Synthesis-derived quantities are exploratory results and were not selected for stock-status determination.

## Screening decision

This is a **new, screened, ineligible candidate** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. The latest operational assessment uses aggregate catches, CPUE and length compositions, and its selected outputs are aggregate biomass and fishing-mortality indicators. The available source evidence does not provide the age-specific observations and population surfaces required for this comparison. No canonical numerical records were added and no tinyAM translation was attempted.
