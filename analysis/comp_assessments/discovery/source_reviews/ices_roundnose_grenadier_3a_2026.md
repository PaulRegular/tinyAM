# Roundnose grenadier in Division 3.a: 2026 source screen

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**Official stock code:** `rng.27.3a`
**Scientific name:** *Coryphaenoides rupestris*
**Assessment area:** Division 3.a (Skagerrak, Kattegat)

## Stock identity and current advice

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database identifies the current Division 3.a
roundnose grenadier unit as assessment year 2026, category 3.2. Its advice is
for zero catch in 2027 and 2028. This is a distinct assessment unit from
Oceanic Northeast Atlantic roundnose grenadier `rng.27.5a10b12ac14b` and the
other roundnose grenadier units in the ICES inventory.

## Published inputs and outputs

The 2026 ICES Stock Assessment Graphs source reports an aggregate B_index in
kilograms per hour, annual total landings and discards, and B_index uncertainty.
It does not report catch-at-age, age compositions, age-specific survey indices,
age-specific weights or maturity, or population outputs by age. Its XML metadata
has blank F-at-age and recruitment-age fields. The generic source-data table
labels index columns “Low SSB / SSB / High SSB,” but the stock metadata identifies
the series as `B_index`; these values are not SSB.

## Screening decision

This distinct new ICES unit is screened **ineligible** for the current
age-disaggregated source-curation set. The available official current sources
support an aggregate survey-index and catch-trend assessment, not the age-based
inputs and outputs required for this screen. This is a public-source finding;
it does not claim that age observations or biological samples do not exist
outside the reviewed assessment materials. No canonical stock, assessment,
input, output, or tinyAM translation records were added.
