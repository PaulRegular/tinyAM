# ICES boarfish in subareas 6-8 (`boc.27.6-8`), 2026

## Assessment identity and run

The 2026 ICES Stock Information Database identifies `boc.27.6-8` as boarfish
(*Capros aper*) in subareas 6-8. The 2026 ICES Stock Assessment Graphs page
(key 25915) reports recruitment at age 0, SSB, and F for ages 4-14, including
uncertainty. The latest detailed public method source located is the 2024
WKBHMB benchmark report; WGWIDE 2025 identifies that benchmarked model as the
current assessment method. The 2026 outputs are retained as evidence for the
latest advice cycle, not as evidence of age-specific model inputs.

## Input and method screen

The final WKBHMB Stock Synthesis 3 model has a 30-age population structure but
is fitted with total annual catch, an acoustic total-stock-biomass index, a
VAST groundfish biomass index, and fishery and groundfish length compositions.
The benchmark explicitly describes a length-based assessment. Its biological
inputs include weight-at-length, a time-invariant maturity-at-length ogive
(L50 = 88 mm), and fixed natural mortality of 0.174. The acoustic and
VAST indices are assigned month 6 and month 11 timing, respectively. No
observed catch-at-age or age-specific survey index was recovered from the
current production assessment sources.

The assessment is therefore screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated
input set. The age-0 recruitment and ages-4-14 F shown in the 2026 ICES graph
are derived outputs; they do not show that age-specific observations were
fitted. There is no canonical stock, assessment event, or numerical input or
output record for this candidate. Model-input assessment, exact run files,
and any tinyAM translation are outside this identity/eligibility screen.
