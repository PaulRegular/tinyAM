# NOAA Washington and Oregon copper rockfish, 2021

## Assessment units and sources

Stock SMART IDs 15330 and 15331 refer to two distinct assessment units: copper
rockfish off Washington and copper rockfish off Oregon. Both were assessed in
2021 using data through 2020. The reports treat them as separate populations
because of differences in movement, habitat, and fishery history. They are not
parts of the 2023 California north/south assessment family. The reports and
their source checksums are recorded in the ignored local cache at
`source_cache/discovery_noaa_copper_washington_oregon_2021/sources.csv`.

## Input evidence and eligibility

Both reports describe Stock Synthesis catch-and-length assessments. Neither
base model fits annual catch-at-age or an age-composition likelihood. The age
samples described in the reports are used to inform biological growth and
related age-specific quantities, rather than to provide annual age-specific
observations. Aggregate catch histories and length compositions therefore do
not meet the current age-input requirement on their own.

The Washington report says that no fishery-independent data source was
available for its assessment. The Oregon report says that no fishery-independent
index was included in the base model; the ODFW Marine Reserve hook-and-line
survey was examined but its copper observations were limited and not used. An
Oregon CPFV index is included only in a sensitivity analysis, not the base
assessment. Neither unit therefore supplies a base-model abundance index that
can be translated to age-specific observations.

The reports provide sex-specific growth and length-weight relationships,
maturity-at-length information, and derived age-specific biological tables.
Natural mortality is fixed at 0.108 per year for both sexes. These biological
inputs do not resolve the missing age-disaggregated catch and abundance
observations. The assessments report official stock-status and spawning-output
results, but this screen does not confirm a published official age-specific
population-output table.

Both units are screened as **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated comparison
set. This decision is about the available assessment observations; it does not
mean the stock lacks age samples or biological age information.
