# NOAA Gulf of Alaska Yelloweye Rockfish — 2024 assessment

Reviewed 2026-10-30. NOAA Stock SMART ID 10425 identifies Gulf of Alaska
Yelloweye Rockfish (*Sebastes ruberrimus*). No canonical tinyAM stock record
matches this unit. The latest fully accepted completed run in the cached NOAA
Stock SMART inventory is assessment 13892, assessment year 2024, with data
through 2024. Stock SMART classifies it as an index method (REMA 22.2).

The 2024 GOA SAFE chapter says the assessment continues Model 22.2 for the
yelloweye component in the Southeast Outside (SEO) Subdistrict. It is a
spatially stratified, two-index random-effects model using ADF&G submersible/ROV
survey biomass and standardized IPHC longline CPUE in kilograms per hook. The
other DSR species in SEO and the DSR complex in western/central GOA and West
Yakutat use Tier 6 maximum-catch rules. This is not an age-structured
population assessment.

The report contains annual aggregate commercial catch, annual management-area
average weights, survey indices, supporting biological age-composition
figures, and SEO yelloweye biomass estimates with 95% intervals for 1994–2024.
The age samples are not catch-at-age likelihood inputs. The REMA fit does not
use age-specific survey observations, weights-at-age, maturity-at-age, or an
age-specific mortality process, and it does not report population N, F, or M at
age or recruitment. The fixed M value of 0.044 documented in the 2024 chapter
is used in Tier 5 harvest-reference calculations, not estimated as an
age-specific population process.

The 2026 DSR model proposals were listed for the November 2026 Plan Team
meeting, which had not occurred at the review date; they are not treated here
as an accepted production run. NOAA's 2024 SSC report confirms use of the
operational REMA approach for the current cycle. No canonical assessment,
input, or output values were added. The unit is screened ineligible for the
age-disaggregated candidate set, while the reported biomass remains a useful
aggregate assessment output for other comparisons.
