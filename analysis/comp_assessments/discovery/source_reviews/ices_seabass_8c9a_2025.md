# Seabass in divisions 8.c and 9.a: 2025 source screen

- **Review date:** 2026-10-31
- **Official stock code:** `bss.27.8c9a`
- **Scientific name:** *Dicentrarchus labrax*
- **Assessment area:** Divisions 8.c and 9.a (southern Bay of Biscay and Atlantic Iberian waters)

## Stock identity and current advice

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database identifies `bss.27.8c9a` as a distinct
assessment unit for the southern Bay of Biscay and Atlantic Iberian waters. The
latest advice located is assessment year 2025, Category 5. It limits commercial
catches to 306 tonnes in each of 2026, 2027, and 2028. This unit is separate
from the northern and central Bay of Biscay unit `bss.27.8ab`, which has a
different boundary and assessment method.

## Published inputs and outputs

The 2025 Stock Assessment Graphs source-data table and XML provide annual
landings in tonnes from 1978 through 2024. They do not provide catch-at-age,
age compositions, survey indices, age-specific weights or maturity, or
age-specific fishing mortality. The XML has blank recruitment, stock-size,
and fishing-pressure values. Its generic metadata says “SSB,” but there is no
numeric SSB series in the published source table or XML. ICES defines Category
5 as landings-only; the advice limit is not a fitted population output.

## Screening decision

This distinct new ICES unit is screened **ineligible** for the current
age-disaggregated source-curation set. The reviewed current official sources
support a landings-only advice record, not the inputs and fitted outputs needed
for an age-structured assessment. This is a finding about the public current
assessment source set, not a claim that age samples do not exist elsewhere. No
canonical stock, assessment, input, output, or tinyAM translation records were
added.
