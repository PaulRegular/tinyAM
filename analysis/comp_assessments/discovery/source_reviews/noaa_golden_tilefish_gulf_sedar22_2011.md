# Gulf golden tilefish, SEDAR 22 (2011)

**Screened:** 2026-10-31  
**Unit:** NOAA Stock SMART 10383, Gulf of Mexico golden tilefish  
**Scope:** Source discovery only; no canonical records were added.

## Assessment identity and current status

NOAA's current golden-tilefish resources page identifies the Gulf assessment as
SEDAR 22 (2011). NOAA Stock SMART identifies assessment 3057, fitted with
Stock Synthesis 3 to data through 2009, and labels it **Partial acceptance -
Status determinations only**. The Gulf unit is distinct from the South Atlantic
and Mid-Atlantic golden-tilefish units.

SEDAR's project page lists a 2026 Gulf tilefish data triage with preliminary
composition and age-data working papers. These are new data documentation, not a
completed replacement assessment. Keep them separate from SEDAR 22's inputs.

## Inputs and biology

The SEDAR 22 report documents removals, length compositions, regional commercial
longline and NMFS bottom-longline indices, and age compositions. The assessment
used two regions (east and west Gulf), handline and longline commercial fleets,
and NMFS bottom-longline survey data. Appendix B prints age counts conditional
on 2-cm length bins, with sample size and age counts by year and fleet/survey;
the observed age bins run from age 2 to age 30+, varying with sampling. The
central model capped age-composition effective sample sizes at 100. These are
not direct annual catch numbers-at-age. Catch-at-age and age-specific index
series can be reconstructed by combining the conditional age counts with the
matching length compositions and removals or aggregate indices, but this is a
documented expansion from the model's conditional age-at-length inputs.

The assessment's region- and gender-specific growth, fixed weight-length
relationship, and maturity schedule come from SEDAR 22 Data Workshop life-history
sources. The report gives a female age-maturity curve with 50% maturity at age
2. Its spawning-biomass output is based on gonad weight, so it should not be
treated as ordinary mature somatic biomass. The central run fixes reference M
at 0.137 at age 4 and uses region- and gender-specific Lorenzen curves derived
from growth. The indices are annual; the reviewed sources do not provide a
fractional within-year observation time.

## Outputs and limits on interpretation

SEDAR 22 Table 3.7 reports annual east, west and Gulf total biomass and
gonad-weight spawning biomass, age-0 recruitment and fishing mortality for the
central Stock Synthesis run. Numeric annual N-at-age and F-at-age surfaces were
not located in the reviewed report. NOAA's partial-acceptance label is material:
the Gulf SSC concluded that the model did not capture stock dynamics well
enough to provide useful management advice. The SSC recommended using historical
landings for the OFL and ABC calculation. The reported model outputs are
therefore recoverable assessment results, but they are a cautious reference for
comparison rather than an endorsed quantitative advice series.

**Screening decision:** Eligible for source curation because the report provides
run-specific age-composition counts, matched length compositions, removals,
biology and aggregate population/mortality outputs. This status records source
recoverability; it does not imply that the assessment is reliable for management
advice. The main extraction work would be reconstruction from the conditional
age-by-length tables, and output comparisons would be limited to aggregate
quantities.
