# Greater silver smelt in subareas 1, 2, and 4 and division 3.a

Review date: 2026-10-30

## Unit and current assessment

ICES 2026 Standard Graphs identify `aru.27.123a4` as Greater silver smelt
(*Argentina silus*) in subareas 1, 2, and 4 and division 3.a. This is a
separate management unit from the other *Argentina silus* entries in the ICES
inventory. The latest advice-linked assessment located is the 2026 assessment
(Standard Graphs key 22376; advice record 6155).

The current method is an aggregate SPiCT surplus-production assessment. ICES
documents the benchmarked method in its 2024 request letter; the Scottish
Government's 2026 fisheries management plan describes the assessment as ICES
Category 2 SPiCT using a biennial acoustic biomass index. The current source
table reports annual aggregate SSB, landings, discards, and F summaries. The
Standard Graphs page labels stock-size and fishing-pressure indicators
`B/Bmsy` and `F/Fmsy`, while the source table uses SSB and F headings and the
stock-size unit field is inconsistent with the ratio label. Treat these as
native aggregate indicators; do not reinterpret or compare their levels without
resolving the source metadata.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible for the age-disaggregated comparison set**. The
current assessment is not age structured and the reviewed advice sources do
not provide catch-at-age, age-specific survey abundance, weights-at-age,
maturity-at-age, N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age for the assessed unit. The
available acoustic information is a biennial aggregate biomass index. This
decision concerns what the current production assessment uses and publishes;
it does not establish that biological age samples do not exist elsewhere.

Older trend-based advice is not substituted for the current assessment. No
canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added.
