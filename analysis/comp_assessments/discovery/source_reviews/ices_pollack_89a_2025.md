# Pollack in ICES subarea 8 and division 9.a: 2025 screen

**Review date:** 2026-10-30  
**Official stock code:** `pol.27.89a`  
**Scientific name:** *Pollachius pollachius*

## Stock identity and assessment

The ICES inventory identifies pollack in subarea 8 and division 9.a as the
current Bay of Biscay and Atlantic Iberian unit. ICES advice key 19891 is for
the 2025 assessment and category 3.2. The advice applies to 2026–2027 and
limits commercial catches to 703 tonnes per year; ICES says corresponding
total catches cannot be quantified because recreational catches are unknown.

## Inputs and assumptions

The official 2025 Standard Graphs display a biomass index (`B_index`) in
kilograms per hour with uncertainty bounds, landings, discards, and a
length-based fishing-pressure proxy. The source-data page reports recruitment
age 0 and leaves the F age blank. The current assessment pages do not provide
catch-at-age, age compositions, age-specific survey indices, age-specific
weights or maturity, or age-specific mortality assumptions.

The Standard Graphs source page uses generic SSB column labels in its table,
but identifies the stock-size measure as `B_index` in kilograms per hour. That
index is not treated as spawning-stock biomass.

## Official outputs

The assessment reports the biomass-index series, its uncertainty bounds,
landings, discards, and the length-based fishing-pressure proxy. It does not
provide recruitment, SSB, or N/F/M-at-age trajectories for this unit.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new candidate, screened **ineligible** for the
age-disaggregated assessment set. The current 2025 category 3.2 assessment
provides an aggregate biomass index and length-based pressure proxy, without
age-specific model inputs or population outputs. The source pages do not
support constructing a catch-at-age or age-specific abundance series.

No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added. No tinyAM
translation was attempted.
