# NOAA Ocean pout (2022)

NOAA Stock SMART identifies Stock ID 10525 as the Northwestern Atlantic Coast
ocean pout unit. The 2022 operational assessment is the latest row in the pinned
Stock SMART snapshot, which labels its review result “Full acceptance.” The
technical memo states that the update was not peer-reviewed in the Fall 2022
panel and records it as a Level 1 direct delivery. It is listed as an Index
Method, Model Category 2
(index-based), with Composition Input Data level 2. The NOAA dictionary defines
that composition level as sufficient size or age-composition information for
data-limited methods; it does not establish an age-composition likelihood in
this assessment.

The 2022 Northeast Fisheries Science Center report describes an index-based
update. Its inputs are aggregate catch (commercial discards and other catch,
in metric tonnes) and the NEFSC spring bottom-trawl survey biomass index
(kg/tow). The reported exploitation ratio is catch divided by the three-year
moving average of that survey index. The report does not describe catch at age,
an age-specific survey index, or age-structured population states. Its 2021
outputs include a biomass proxy of 0.263 kg/tow and a fully selected F proxy
(exploitation ratio) of 0.234; these are aggregate quantities, not SSB,
recruitment, or N/F/M at age.

This candidate is therefore **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated comparison
set. That decision concerns the assessment's inputs and outputs; it does not
claim that age samples or biological age data do not exist outside this model.
No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added.
