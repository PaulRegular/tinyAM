# Western Atlantic sailfish (NOAA:10180; ICCAT 2023 assessment)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**NOAA Stock SMART name:** Sailfish - Western Atlantic
**Stock SMART scientific name:** *Istiophorus platypterus*
**ICCAT assessment unit:** West Atlantic sailfish; the report uses *Istiophorus albicans* for Atlantic sailfish.
**Latest NOAA Stock SMART assessment:** 2023; operational; full acceptance; BSP metadata; data through 2021.

No canonical tinyAM sailfish stock was found. The NOAA row identifies the Western Atlantic management unit. ICCAT's assessment report treats East and West Atlantic sailfish as separate management units; it records the western assessment period through 2021. NOAA's scientific-name label differs from the name used in the ICCAT report, so the crosswalk is based on the matching common name and western Atlantic management area, not an asserted taxonomic synonym.

## Inputs and outputs

The 2023 assessment used fleet catches, annual length compositions, and eight aggregate abundance indices for the western unit. The Stock Synthesis description explicitly says that no age data were included; its age-based selectivity was derived from length-based selectivity and the growth model. The report's age bins describe model structure, not age-composition observations. The abundance indices are fishery CPUE series, not age-specific survey indices.

The report describes fixed natural mortality of 0.35 across ages, a length-weight relationship, growth assumptions, and maturity as a logistic function of age. These biological settings do not supply the missing age-composition observations. The selected western JABBA scenario reports aggregate B/BMSY and F/FMSY trajectories; the alternative Stock Synthesis results include aggregate relative SSB, F and recruitment series. Neither platform's reviewed report provides annual N-at-age and F-at-age surfaces.

Because the abundance inputs are fishery indices rather than seasonal surveys, a within-year survey timing fraction is not applicable.

## Screening decision

This is a **new, screened, ineligible candidate** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. The latest accepted operational assessment includes an internally age-structured Stock Synthesis platform, but the report states that no age data were included in that model. Its actual population observations are catches, CPUE and length compositions, and the official report does not provide age-specific population outputs. The biological age structure and fixed mortality assumption do not meet the required age-observation and output evidence.
