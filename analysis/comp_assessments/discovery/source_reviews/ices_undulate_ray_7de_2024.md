# Undulate ray in the English Channel: current assessment screen

**Review date:** 2026-10-30  
**ICES stock code:** `rju.27.7de`  
**Scientific name:** *Raja undulata*

## Unit and current assessment

The candidate is the ICES unit in divisions 7.d and 7.e (English Channel). The
latest advice-linked assessment located is the 2024 assessment (key 19173),
which provides advice for 2025 and 2026. The 2026 SID row is an inventory entry,
not evidence of a 2026 assessment run.

## Inputs and model structure

The 2022 ICES benchmark reviewed and accepted an aggregate SPiCT assessment for
this stock. Its inputs are removals and survey biomass indices, including the
FR-CGFS and Q1SWECOS series. They are not catch-at-age or survey abundance at
age. The advice-linked 2024 Standard Graphs report aggregate relative biomass
and relative fishing pressure, and its source table contains no age-specific
population surface.

Age-specific weights, maturity, and natural mortality are not required by this
aggregate production model and are not provided as age-structured assessment
inputs in the reviewed current sources. This is a confirmed method mismatch for
the age-disaggregated assessment set, rather than a claim that biological
information for the species does not exist.

## Outputs and decision

The 2024 assessment reports aggregate biomass and fishing-pressure indicators
with uncertainty bounds and catch advice. It does not report numbers-, fishing
mortality-, or natural mortality-at-age.

This is a distinct new ICES unit screened **ineligible** for the
age-disaggregated assessment set. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or
output records were added, and no tinyAM translation was attempted.
