# Gulf of Alaska flathead sole

## Assessment and inputs

NOAA Stock SMART ID 10016 is the Gulf of Alaska flathead sole unit. The latest full operational assessment located is the 2022 Model 17.1a SAFE chapter. The 2024 and 2026 reports are projection updates and do not rerun the full assessment. The [2022 SAFE chapter](https://apps-afsc.fisheries.noaa.gov/Plan_Team/2022/GOAflathead.pdf) is cached under `source_cache/noaa_flatfish_screening_20261029/` with its source details and checksum.

The model uses catch biomass and fishery length compositions, plus survey biomass, survey lengths and conditional age-at-length data. The assessment explicitly notes that marginal survey age compositions in the input file are ghosted and do not contribute to the likelihood. No fishery catch-age observations or fishery age compositions were verified. Modelled age structure, survey CAAL and fitted N-at-age do not replace catch-age data; length compositions alone do not establish eligibility.

The assessment documents growth and age-length biology, fixed M, maturity and fitted population outputs. The missing fishery age input makes this candidate ineligible for the current age-input screen, despite its age-structured model label.
