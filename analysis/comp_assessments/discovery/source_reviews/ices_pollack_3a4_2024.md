Review date: 2026-10-30

## Unit and latest advice

ICES identifies this as pollack (*Pollachius pollachius*) in Subarea 4 and
Division 3.a, covering the North Sea, Skagerrak and Kattegat. Advice record
3768 is the latest assessment record located for this unit: assessment year
2024, stock category 5, with advice applicable from 2025 through 2027. The
advice recommends catches no greater than 1,462 tonnes.

also provide no weights-at-age, maturity-at-age, or age-specific mortality

age-disaggregated comparison set on the evidence in the latest official
advice-linked sources. Those sources do not provide age-specific inputs or a
comparable population or mortality output series. This decision describes
