# DFO North Coast Chinook inventory entry

The inventory label `Chinook Salmon - North Coast` is regional and does not
identify one current assessment unit. DFO's 2026 Pacific salmon summary lists
22 North Coast stock management units across salmon species. The Northern BC
Integrated Fisheries Management Plan treats Skeena-Nass ISBM Chinook and
Central Coast ISBM Chinook separately. No one-to-one match to a canonical stock
or a specific production assessment was verified, so the row remains ambiguous
and is not linked to a candidate unit.

## Assessment evidence

The latest publicly available regional summary is DFO's 2026 salmon outlook.
DFO describes it as an early scan that may be superseded by formal assessments.
The current Northern BC IFMP describes separate Skeena-Nass and Central Coast
Chinook management units. For Skeena-Nass, the plan says status is evaluated
primarily from stream escapements; Nass monitoring includes fish-wheel catch
and post-season mark-recapture, while the Tyee test fishery is a relative
abundance tool that is not calibrated for Chinook. This establishes that the
regional inventory entry combines distinct management and monitoring contexts;
it does not establish a single age-structured production assessment.

No unit-matched catch-at-age, age-specific index, weight-at-age,
maturity-at-age, mortality surface, or numeric N/F/M-at-age output was verified
for the broad inventory row. Because identity is unresolved, absence of those
items at this aggregate level is not evidence that every constituent unit
lacks them. Eligibility therefore remains unresolved rather than ineligible.

## Decision

Mark the regional inventory row as identity-ambiguous, screened, and
age-structured eligibility unresolved. Do not create canonical stock or
assessment records, and do not treat this label as a stock unit. Review the
Skeena-Nass and Central Coast Chinook units separately if detailed,
run-matched production assessment sources become available.
