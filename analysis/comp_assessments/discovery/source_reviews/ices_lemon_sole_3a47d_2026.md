# ICES lemon sole in Subarea 4 and divisions 3.a and 7.d (2026)

**Review date:** 2026-10-30  
**Official stock code:** `lem.27.3a47d`  
**Scientific name:** *Microstomus kitt*  
**Assessment year:** 2026

## Stock identity and assessment

The 2026 ICES stock list identifies `lem.27.3a47d` as lemon sole in the North
Sea, Skagerrak, Kattegat and eastern English Channel. The unit is not present
in the canonical stocks table.

The [2026 WGNSSK report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32676345.v2) says
the current advice method is a survey-based stock-size indicator using SURBAR
relative SSB with the chr rule. This has been the advice method since 2023.
The report says a benchmark is planned for 2026–2027, when other assessment
methods may be explored. This screen applies to the current advised method.

## Inputs and assumptions

The current assessment summary does not describe a catch-at-age likelihood,
age-specific survey index, or age-structured population model. It therefore
does not establish the age-specific catch, survey, weight and maturity inputs
needed for a tinyAM age-structured translation. Age information that may be
collected in the fisheries or surveys is not evidence that it is used as an
age-specific assessment input.

The reviewed report does not specify a run-matched age-structured mortality
assumption or survey timing for such a model. Those settings are not inferred.

## Official outputs

The report identifies SURBAR relative SSB as the stock-size indicator used for
advice. It does not identify annual abundance-at-age or fishing-mortality-at-age
outputs for this current method.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct current stock and is **ineligible for the age-disaggregated
set under the 2026 advised method**. The advice is based on a survey-derived
stock-size indicator, rather than an age-structured population assessment with
the required age-specific inputs. No canonical assessment, input, or output
records have been added, and no tinyAM translation has been attempted.
