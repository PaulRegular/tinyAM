# NOAA Cape Cod/Gulf of Maine yellowtail flounder — 2025 assessment

## Identity and official run

NOAA Stock SMART ID 10035 is the Cape Cod/Gulf of Maine yellowtail flounder
(*Limanda ferruginea*) unit. The canonical database already contains distinct
Georges Bank and Southern New England/Mid-Atlantic units, not this area. The
candidate is marked provisionally new until NOAA, DFO and ICES inventory matches
and stock-boundary relationships are fully reconciled.

The 2025 Management Track assessment updates the 2024 research-track WHAM model
with data through 2024. NOAA's final September 2025 peer-review panel report
concludes that it met the terms of reference and represented best available
science. The assessment archive identifies the stock as Gulf of Maine–Cape Cod,
assessment year 2025, Level 2 review.

## Evidence and source limits

- The final panel report documents catch data through 2024 and discusses
  catch-at-age diagnostics. The portal's 2025 support ZIP contains PDFs only;
  no numeric annual catch-at-age input or age-composition arrays were provided.
  The ZIP also contains a 2024 research-track working paper; its values and
  results are contextual only and are not substituted for the 2025 production
  run.
- Six abundance-index series are described, separating NEFSC spring/fall
  Albatross and Bigelow series and including Massachusetts and Maine–New
  Hampshire fall surveys. They are aggregate indices. Run-matched survey age
  compositions or age-specific survey observations were not found in the
  support bundle.
- The assessment uses time-varying age-specific weights, three-year moving
  average maturity and age-specific natural mortality. The results PDF plots
  age-specific mortality, but the portal bundle does not provide their numeric
  input/output arrays. Exact within-year survey timing also remains unverified.
- The assessment report provides numerical annual SSB, fully selected F and
  age-1 recruitment for 2015–2024 (SSB in metric tonnes, recruitment in
  thousands of fish, and F as a rate), plus short-term projections. The results
  PDF plots SSB-at-age, January 1 numbers-at-age and M-at-age, but those
  age-specific series are not available as numeric tables in the recovered
  files.

The current status is **screened, eligibility unresolved, not imported**. The
official age-structured assessment and aggregate outputs are confirmed, but the
available run-matched support files do not establish the numeric age-based
inputs and outputs needed for a defensible canonical import. This is a source
availability gap, not evidence that the assessment lacks age data. Do not
substitute 2024 research-track inputs for this 2025 production run.
