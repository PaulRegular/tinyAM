# ICES megrim in divisions 7.b-k, 8.a-b, and 8.d

## Stock identity and latest assessment

`meg.27.7b-k8abd` is a distinct ICES stock unit for *Lepidorhombus whiffiagonis* in divisions 7.b-k, 8.a-b, and 8.d. No matching canonical stock record was found. It is separate from the existing review of southern megrim, `meg.27.8c9a` (divisions 8.c and 9.a).

The latest detailed assessment located is the 2025 WGBIE assessment, with data through 2024. The report identifies it as a category 1 statistical catch-at-age assessment using a4a. ICES Advice and Scenarios Database key 3866 links assessment key 21222 to advice released in October 2025, applicable in 2026. The advised catch was no more than 14,869 tonnes. No later assessment key for this stock was present in the official advice listing reviewed on 2026-10-29.

The accompanying review document WD12 says the initial 2025 base run was not accepted and a revision was requested. WD12 then tests input corrections and model changes, and recommends the corrected category 1 approach. The 2025 report and advice record identify the revised 2025 assessment as the basis for 2026 advice, but the downloadable stock annex is still dated 2018 even though the report says it was updated in 2022 and corrected in 2024. Keep this run-to-annex mismatch visible; do not assume the downloaded annex contains the current model's complete specifications.

## Inputs

The report says France, Ireland, the UK, and Belgium provided numbers-at-age and weights-at-age through 2024. Spain supplied length compositions converted to age distributions using length-weight relationships. The assessment uses landed, discarded, and total catch numbers-at-age. Annual landings and catches are tabulated for 1984-2024, but the run-matched numerical catch-at-age matrices are not included in the accessible report. Catch-age compositions for 2011-2024 and caught, landed, and discarded numbers-at-age over the series are shown as figures. Their exact numerical values cannot be recovered from the report tables alone; digitizing them would be an approximation requiring validation against the source data.

Two age-specific survey inputs used by the assessment are tabulated:

- The combined French and Irish Q4 IBTS, G9527_G7212, has ages 1-10 for 2003-2024, in numbers per 10 hours fished. The 2017 row is missing.
- The Spanish Porcupine Bank survey, G5768, has ages 1-10 for 2001-2024, in mean numbers per 30-minute haul.

The report describes G5768 as occurring at the end of Q3 and beginning of Q4, and the combined French-Irish survey as Q4. An exact fractional-year timing is not stated. The report specifies fixed natural mortality, M = 0.2 for all ages and years. It describes a female-only maturity ogive based on histological methods. The cited maturity study and stock annex are follow-up sources for biological definitions, but the annex file currently linked by ICES is dated 2018 and the 2025 report does not reproduce the complete numeric vectors.

## Outputs

The assessment reports annual age-1 recruitment, SSB, catch and mean F at ages 3-6 for 1984-2024. The report presents 5th and 95th percentiles for recruitment and SSB, and assessment-graph data provide low/high series for recruitment, SSB, and F. Numerical N-at-age and F-at-age output matrices were not recovered in the reviewed public sources.
