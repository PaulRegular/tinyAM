# NOAA Yellowedge Grouper inventory unit: SEDAR 85

Reviewed 2026-10-30. NOAA Stock SMART row 10323 identifies the Gulf of Mexico management unit for *Hyporthodus flavolimbatus* (listed under the older *Epinephelus flavolimbatus* name). No matching tinyAM canonical stock record was found. The assessment treats eastern and western Gulf areas as model subregions within one management unit.

The latest official fitted assessment located is the SEDAR 85 operational assessment, completed in November 2023 using data through 2021. The Gulf SSC reviewed it in February 2024. The 2024 executive summary and additional projections derive advice and projection scenarios from that fitted assessment; they are not a new population-model fit. The SEDAR assessment report describes an age-structured Stock Synthesis model with four commercial fleets (vertical line and longline, each divided into east and west Gulf areas), annual age compositions, aggregate CPUE and bottom-longline abundance indices, and age-based biology.

Commercial age compositions use one-year age bins with a 40+ group. The assessment pools female, male and unsexed samples because sex assignments and some sex-specific samples were unreliable. The public WP-04 document provides annual age sample counts and plotted age proportions, but not complete numeric age-composition arrays. The bottom-longline survey has east and west aggregate abundance indices for 2000–2021, with annual CVs and confidence limits tabulated in WP-09. It also contributes survey age compositions, but the exact age vectors and Stock Synthesis input files were not recovered.

The assessment uses regional von Bertalanffy growth curves, a fixed gutted-weight to total-length relationship, and the inherited SEDAR 22 maturity and protogynous sex-transition assumptions. Natural mortality follows a Lorenzen size-dependent schedule based on a reference estimate of 0.073. These assumptions provide a documented route to age-specific biology, but the run-matched model files were not located. The model is annual and assumes continuous, homogeneous fishing through the year. The bottom-longline survey is conducted in August in the eastern Gulf and September in the western Gulf; exact within-year timing values from the fitted model were not verified.

The final assessment reports annual SSB, recruitment and exploitation outputs with uncertainty information. Full numerical N-at-age and F-at-age output arrays were not confirmed in the reviewed public sources. Exact observed age-composition arrays and fitted model files remain the main source blockers, so eligibility remains unresolved pending further recovery. The stock is a plausible candidate for source curation if the numerical compositions can be recovered or defensibly reconstructed from the source figures without substituting fitted values.
