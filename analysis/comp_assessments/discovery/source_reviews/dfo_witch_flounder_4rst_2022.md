# DFO Gulf of St. Lawrence witch flounder, NAFO 4RST

## Current unit and assessment

The inventory entry is the DFO Gulf Region witch flounder unit in NAFO Divisions 4RST, *Glyptocephalus cynoglossus*. The 2024 DFO indicator update identifies the March 2022 assessment as the latest full assessment and says a new full assessment is planned for 2027. The 2022 run used data through 2021.

## Data and model

The 2022 Research Document reports annual landings by gear in tonnes for 1960–2021. The model uses landings aligned to September of the previous year through August of the current year, beginning in 1961. It describes commercial catch-at-length samples, but the assessment is a Bayesian Schaefer surplus-production model. It does not fit catch-at-age or length-composition likelihoods.

The model fits three aggregate biomass indices of fish at least 30 cm long: the 4T September research-vessel index for 1971–1992, combined 4RST August/September research-vessel biomass for 1987–2021, and combined July/August sentinel survey biomass for 2003–2019. A separate under-30-cm recruitment indicator is based on length, not age. The survey indices are standardized from multiple vessels/gears and are not age-specific abundance observations.

Age samples from the September survey in 1974–1981 (301 males and 445 females) were used for sex-specific growth estimation. Historical maturity-at-age and maturity-at-length were estimated from survey samples. These biological analyses are not used as age-specific inputs to the population model. The model estimates biomass, surplus production, process error, index catchability and exploitation rate; it does not estimate age-specific M, F, N or SSB.

The official Open Government dataset contains annual 30+ cm biomass posterior medians and 2.5th, 25th, 75th and 97.5th percentiles for 1961–2021, in tonnes. Its 2021 median (17,767.7 t) matches the research document's 17,768 t estimate and is rounded to 17,770 t in the Science Advisory Report. The 2024 update extends an interim survey-derived indicator to 2024; it does not report a new fitted model trajectory.

## Screening decision

This is a verified, distinct DFO assessment unit but is ineligible for the age-structured expansion batch: the current production assessment is biomass-based and has no age-disaggregated population or likelihood data. The unit remains in the discovery register as screened/ineligible; no stock, assessment, input or output records were added to the canonical database.
