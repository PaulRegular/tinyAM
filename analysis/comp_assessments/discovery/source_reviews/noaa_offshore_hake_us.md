# Offshore hake, U.S. Northwest Atlantic (NOAA:10524)

Reviewed 2026-11-01. The pinned NOAA Stock SMART snapshot identifies stock 10524 as Merluccius albidus, with a 2011 benchmark through 2009. Stock SMART lists the method as AIM and SEINE and the review result as “Reject - Data insufficient for assessment.” It reports no estimated biomass or F values. No canonical database record for this U.S. stock was found.

The 2011 SAW 51 report discusses offshore hake mostly as a species that is mixed with silver hake in landings and survey samples. It describes two approaches for estimating species composition in the southern silver hake fishery, based on length samples or survey depth, and gives estimated offshore-hake landings. The report explains that fishery age compositions were built for silver hake from silver-hake survey age-length keys; those age compositions are not offshore-hake catch-at-age. It also notes uncertainty in separating offshore and silver hake in the source records.

The more recent 2023 NEFSC management-track sources do not provide a separate offshore-hake population assessment. The table of management-track stocks groups “Silver/Offshore Hake (North and South),” but the accessible detailed report is for northern silver hake. It reports an empirical survey-index and catch-ratio approach for silver hake, says offshore-hake survey data are unreliable for population trends, and provides no offshore-hake N-at-age, F-at-age, SSB, or recruitment series. NOAA’s small-mesh multispecies specifications state that scientific information is insufficient to produce catch limits for offshore hake.

## Age-disaggregated screening

- **Catch:** Species-specific landings and discards are estimated from length- or depth-based species composition methods, but no offshore-hake catch-at-age series or offshore-specific age composition was recovered. Silver-hake catch-at-age values cannot be substituted for offshore hake.
- **Survey:** Survey data support species-composition estimates and biomass summaries for silver hake. No offshore-hake age-specific abundance index was found.
- **Biology and mortality:** Length-weight relationships are described, but no offshore-hake age-specific weight or maturity series, nor an age-structured mortality treatment, was recovered.
- **Outputs:** The current sources do not provide offshore-hake population-at-age, F-at-age, SSB, or recruitment outputs. Species-specific catch estimates and silver-hake survey/status quantities are not substitutes for those outputs.

Screening result: **new U.S. management stock; ineligible for the age-disaggregated source set on current public evidence**. This is a source-availability conclusion, not a claim that offshore hake are biologically unimportant. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added.
