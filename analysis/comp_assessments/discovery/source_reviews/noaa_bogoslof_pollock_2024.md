# NOAA Stock SMART 10512: Bogoslof pollock

Reviewed 2026-10-29. This is a distinct U.S.-managed pollock unit, separate from Eastern Bering Sea, Aleutian Islands, and Gulf of Alaska pollock. NOAA describes five separate pollock stocks; the Bogoslof unit is the Central Bering Sea-Bogoslof Island stock. It is not represented in the canonical stock table.

## Assessment and current status

The latest Council-approved assessment located is the 2024 SAFE assessment, based on the 2024 winter acoustic-trawl survey and used for 2025-2026 harvest specifications. The Council's December 2024 SSC report confirms its approval of the BSAI Plan Team recommendations. The 2026 SAFE chapter is newer and is posted among the 2026 final SAFE chapters, but its cover calls it a September 2026 draft and says it is for pre-dissemination peer review. It proposes 2027-2028 specifications; treat it as later draft evidence, not as a formally disseminated agency determination.

Stock SMART still lists assessment year 2022, which does not reflect the 2024 accepted production update. NOAA/NMFS reports that the winter acoustic-trawl survey is conducted in February-March and supplies abundance, biomass, sex, size and age information.

## Inputs, assumptions, and outputs

The production assessment is Tier 5. Its primary observation is aggregate acoustic-trawl biomass in tonnes, with a random-effects smoother. The accepted report gives the 2024 survey estimate as about 245,000 t and the smoothed biomass estimate as 247,137 t; it recommends 2025 and 2026 ABC of 58,015 t and OFL of 77,354 t. The 2026 draft leaves the 2024 survey estimate and Tier 5 calculation unchanged.

The report also provides estimated survey numbers-at-age for ages 4 through 14 and 15+ in 1988-2020. These are identified as survey age data used in a separate Tier-3-type age-structured research model; age data from the 2024 survey were unavailable. The 2024 SSC report says the older Tier 3 model was to be brought forward for future work. The age table is useful supporting evidence, but it is not a catch-at-age likelihood or a fitted age-specific population surface from the current production assessment.

Directed pollock fishing in the Bogoslof district has been closed since 1992; catches are low bycatch. The production calculation carries forward scalar M = 0.313 from the age-structured research work. The Tier 5 report does not provide a run-matched weight-at-age or maturity-at-age input or N-at-age/F-at-age fitted output for the production method. The 2026 draft likewise states that the 2024 age data were unavailable.

The candidate is screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated production-assessment pool. This decision is about the method and outputs used for current advice; it is not a claim that age observations are absent from all supporting research. No assessment or numeric series has been imported.
