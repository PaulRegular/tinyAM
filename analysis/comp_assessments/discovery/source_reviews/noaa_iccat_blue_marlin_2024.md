# Atlantic blue marlin (NOAA:11254; ICCAT 2024 assessment)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**Scientific name:** *Makaira nigricans*
**Assessment unit:** Atlantic-wide blue marlin, a single ICCAT stock.
**Latest NOAA Stock SMART assessment:** 2024; operational; full acceptance; Stock Synthesis metadata; data through 2022.

NOAA Stock SMART 11254 identifies the Atlantic blue marlin inventory row. No canonical tinyAM stock matches this unit. ICCAT's 2024 assessment combined equally the results from four Stock Synthesis scenarios and four JABBA scenarios for management advice. ICCAT identifies the 2024 run as covering data through 2022.

## Inputs and outputs

The assessment uses fleet removals, aggregate abundance indices, and catch/length-frequency information. The reports do not describe catch-at-age or population age-composition likelihoods. Otolith age-at-size observations inform growth, and the model also uses expected mean size-at-age; these are age observations for growth estimation, not age compositions of the assessed population. The available data therefore do not provide age-specific survey indices or a defensible direct conversion of the aggregate indices to abundance-at-age.

The reports provide sex-specific growth and length-weight relationships, a length at 50% maturity of 206 cm, and natural mortality of 0.148 as an initial value carried from the 2018 assessment; the assessment team attempted to estimate M in Stock Synthesis. These do not amount to a run-matched maturity-at-age or weight-at-age observation matrix. The 2024 advice is based on a joint grid of aggregate biomass and fishing-mortality status results. Numeric annual N-at-age and F-at-age surfaces were not identified.

The abundance indices are fishery CPUE series rather than a seasonal survey index, so a survey timing fraction is not applicable. The reports describe aggregate annual model inputs and do not specify a tinyAM-compatible within-year timing fraction for those CPUE indices.

## Screening decision

This is a **new, screened, ineligible candidate** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. Although the production assessment includes an age-structured Stock Synthesis platform and uses age-at-size growth data, its population observations are total removals, CPUE, and length compositions. It does not provide the age-composition/age-specific index information or annual N-at-age and F-at-age outputs needed for this inventory's tinyAM-readiness screen. The biological and mortality assumptions are not a substitute for those population observations and outputs.

No canonical numerical records were added and no tinyAM translation was attempted. The two official reports and their retrieval date, revisions, sizes and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in the ignored local cache at `source_cache/discovery_noaa_iccat_blue_marlin_2024/sources.csv`.
