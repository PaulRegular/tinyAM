# NOAA Gulf of Alaska Thornyhead Complex

## Stock identity and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART ID 10457 is listed as Shortspine Thornyhead - Gulf of Alaska (*Sebastolobus alascanus*). The official 2024 assessment is for the Gulf of Alaska thornyhead management complex, which includes shortspine (*S. alascanus*) and longspine (*S. altivelis*). The Tier 5 assessment uses shortspine as the representative species and does not fit a species-specific age-structured model. No matching canonical tinyAM record or other inventory identity was found. The candidate is provisionally new as a management unit, with the species-complex relationship noted explicitly.

The latest detailed assessment is the November 2024 GOA SAFE chapter, using bottom-trawl biomass data through 2023 and longline relative-population-weight data through 2023. Models 22.a and 24.2 are compared; Model 24.2 is recommended for the updated biomass estimates. The source is cached locally at `source_cache/discovery_noaa_goa_thornyhead_2024/`; its ignored manifest records the retrieval date and SHA-256.

## Assessment inputs and outputs

The current model is a random-effects model fit to regional AFSC bottom-trawl survey biomass estimates and regional AFSC longline relative population weights. The SAFE describes the stock as Tier 5: reliable biomass estimates are the information used for ABC calculations. Catch totals and commercial length compositions are reported. Few fishery age samples exist and none have been aged. The age-structured assessment was suspended in 2003 because of uncertainty in the age and growth information; the current 2024 model is biomass-based and does not use age-composition likelihoods.

Natural mortality is assumed to be 0.03 per year to define reference harvest rates; it is not estimated by the current model. The report summarizes maturity at length from prior studies, but no maturity-at-age, weights-at-age, or age-specific population dynamics are fitted. It cannot estimate recruitment statistically. The 2024 assessment reports 2025 GOA-wide biomass of 59,459 tonnes (95% CI 47,064–75,117 tonnes), as well as regional biomass trends, ABC and OFL. It does not report comparable age-specific N, F or M surfaces.

## Decision

Screening decision: provisionally new but ineligible for the current age-disaggregated source set. The 2024 assessment provides catch, survey biomass and relative-population-weight indices, length information, assumed M and biomass/harvest outputs. It does not provide catch-at-age, age-specific survey observations, age-specific biological schedules, or age-specific fitted outputs. Historical age studies and the old age-structured method are not substituted for the latest current assessment.
