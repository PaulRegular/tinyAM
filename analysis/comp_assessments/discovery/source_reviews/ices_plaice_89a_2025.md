# ICES plaice in Bay of Biscay and Atlantic Iberian waters, 2025

ICES unit `ple.27.89a` is plaice (*Pleuronectes platessa*) in Subarea 8 and
Division 9.a. The latest detailed source located is section 13 of the 2025
WGBIE report, version 4.

The report classifies this as a category 5.2 data-limited stock and states
that there is no analytical assessment. It presents official landings and
commercial numbers-at-length, but no catch-at-age or age-composition model
inputs. The report says no commercial abundance index is available and notes
that survey or commercial abundance indices and biological information remain
gaps. Its assessment output is a precautionary landings recommendation, not
an age-structured population estimate. ICES Standard Graphs key 19633 reports
aggregate landings only and leaves the F-at-age field blank.

The ICES 2025 Advice record (3923) reports a landings recommendation of 99 t
for each year 2026–2028. The WGBIE report says 124 t for those same years.
This source discrepancy is unresolved. It does not change the eligibility
screen: neither source provides age-structured inputs or population outputs.

This unit is **ineligible for the age-disaggregated inventory** on the
production-assessment evidence reviewed. The review does not assert that
biological samples are absent; it establishes that the current assessment
cannot support the requested age-structured comparison. No canonical
numerical records or tinyAM translation were added.
