# Fraser River Eulachon

## Identity and current management

The DFO Sustainability Survey lists “Eulachon - Fraser River” as “Uncertain” in
its 2024 results. This entry refers to the Fraser River Designatable Unit (DU),
not all Canadian Eulachon DUs. The 2026 Fraser River Integrated Fisheries
Management Plan (IFMP) is the latest management source reviewed. DFO says the
older 2003 multi-indicator framework is no longer used and identifies the Fraser
River Egg and Larval Survey as the main source used to determine food, social and
ceremonial harvest levels. The survey has operated annually since 1995 and
provides aggregate spawning-biomass indices.

The IFMP reports SSB-index values through 2025. It also describes offshore
small-mesh trawl observations, but fish ages and spawning-stock origins are
uncertain, and the observations are treated as aggregate ecological indicators,
not as age-specific inputs to a current production assessment. DFO states that
there is no validated ageing technique for Eulachon.

## Historical age-structured model and screening

The 2012 Canadian RPA includes an Appendix 3 Bayesian age-structured stock
assessment model for the Fraser DU. It used annual commercial catch biomass
(1900–2009), shrimp-trawl effort to represent bycatch, and aggregate relative
abundance indices. Its simplified life history assumed semelparity at age three,
used fixed mean masses for ages one to three, and fixed natural mortality at
0.51 per year. The 2015 Fraser-specific RPA refers back to this historical
model. These sources establish that an age-structured research model was once
fit, but do not show that it was the current production model for advice.

The historical model did not use catch numbers-at-age or an age-specific survey
index likelihood. Its reported quantities include biomass, depletion, recruitment
anomalies and aggregate fishing-mortality measures; the documents do not provide
a comparable annual fitted N-at-age or F/M-at-age output series. The current
IFMP instead presents the Egg and Larval Survey as an aggregate SSB index and
notes unresolved age and biological information. Therefore the unit is
**ineligible for the current age-disaggregated assessment set**, despite the
historical age-structured model. Do not confuse otolith-age research or model
age classes with observed age-specific assessment inputs.

No canonical stock, assessment, input or output records were added.
