# North Sea horse mackerel: current-run source screen

## Unit and latest assessment

ICES stock key `hom.27.4bc7d` is horse mackerel in divisions 4.b, 4.c and 7.d.
It is a distinct assessment unit from the existing Division 9.a horse mackerel
record. The current ICES stock inventory identifies the 2026 production
assessment as SAM. The 2026 advice and Stock Assessment Graphs data (assessment
key 25921) report historical age-1 recruitment, SSB, and F for ages 2-8 for
2002-2025, with lower and upper uncertainty values, plus a 2026 forecast point
without intervals.

## What the detailed prior benchmark establishes

The 2024 WKBHMB report labels the benchmark stock `hom.27.3a4bc7d`, while the
current 2026 stock key is `hom.27.4bc7d`. The older key included Division 3.a;
do not transfer those records to the current unit without confirming the
reallocation. The report describes the benchmark SAM assessment and its
age-structured data. It reports catch-at-age in tonnes and catch weight-at-age
in kg for ages 0-14 over 2002-2022. These values support a number conversion for
that benchmark run. The final assessment starts in 2002, uses age 1 as the
minimum age and 14+ as the plus group, and gives the same F for ages 11+.

The benchmark used natural mortality-at-age and maturity-at-age from the
Western horse mackerel assessment because North Sea-specific biological
estimates were unavailable. It describes a combined FR-CGFS Q4 and NS-IBTS Q3
survey biomass/SSB index and a commercial CPUE index for 2017-2023. The reviewed
sources do not provide survey age compositions or an age-specific abundance
index. The assessment report notes that the survey SSB index was formed from
survey biomass and maturity-at-length information.

## Current-run gaps and decision

The latest 2026 assessment has public aggregate output, but the run-matched
catch-at-age, biological and index inputs were not recovered during this
screen. The 2024 benchmark values are useful context only; they are not used as
substitutes for the 2026 production-run inputs. The exact fractional timing for
the current observations is also not established. The reviewed report does not
provide numeric N-at-age or a full F-at-age surface for the 2026 run.

The stock is therefore recorded as one distinct, screened candidate with
eligibility unresolved. Revisit it when the detailed 2026 working-group report
or model inputs become public. No numerical data were imported.
