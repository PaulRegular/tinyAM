# DFO inventory Yellowtail Flounder - NAFO 5Z

## Identity and current assessment

The DFO Maritimes inventory row for Yellowtail Flounder in NAFO 5Z maps to the existing combined U.S.-Canada Georges Bank yellowtail stock, `ck_dfo_nefsc_ferruginea_gb`. NOAA Stock SMART 10033 is already crosswalked to that same record. These are agency inventory entries for one stock unit, not separate U.S. and Canadian cases. They are distinct from the DFO 4T, NOAA Southern New England/Mid-Atlantic, and NOAA Cape Cod/Gulf of Maine yellowtail units.

The latest production assessment is the 2025 NEFSC management-track WHAM update, with observations through 2024 and projections through 2028. The final Framework 72 submission confirms approval for management, although the unit report retains a draft-for-peer-review label. The selected production model uses age-based estimated M; the numerical 2025 production inputs and full age-specific outputs remain unrecovered. The existing canonical assessment record is partial.

## Age-data screen

- **Catch-at-age:** Exact current-run catch-age observations and likelihood arrays are not available in the reviewed production report or recovered files. The 2024 `Constant_M` archive is not substituted because peer review selected the revised `Age_based_M` model.
- **Survey ages:** The production assessment uses NEFSC spring and fall and DFO spring surveys. Exact current-run age compositions and likelihood arrays are not recovered.
- **Biology and M:** The selected 2025 production assessment updated the maturity ogive and uses age-based M. Exact production maturity, weights and mortality surfaces are not available. Recovered 2024 constant-M arrays are from a rejected model candidate and cannot fill these gaps.
- **Timing:** Exact configured observation fractions for the three survey series were not recovered.
- **Outputs:** The 2025 assessment report and NOAA Stock SMART provide aggregate catch, SSB, fully selected F and age-1 recruitment summaries for 2015-2024, with terminal SSB and F intervals and separate projections through 2028. Numerical production N/F/M-at-age surfaces are not available.

## Screening decision

This is a verified identity match to the canonical Georges Bank record, not a new stock. It remains screened with **eligibility unresolved** because the current production-run age observations, key biological inputs and age-specific fitted surfaces are incomplete. The candidate register marks it imported only because partial aggregate official outputs are already stored in the canonical database; it does not imply a complete import. No canonical data were changed during this crosswalk review.
