# U.S. Pacific Coast Greenstriped Rockfish (NOAA Stock SMART 10566)

## Stock unit and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART identifies one Pacific Coast assessment unit for greenstriped rockfish (*Sebastes elongatus*), Stock ID 10566. PFMC Amendment 37, implemented in 2026, retains greenstriped rockfish as one coastwide stock unit in the Pacific Coast Groundfish FMP. The 2009 model treated the outer U.S. West Coast population as one homogeneous stock; its fisheries and survey analyses include regional strata, but these are not separate assessment units. No existing canonical tinyAM stock matches this U.S. unit.

Stock SMART records the 2009 assessment as fully accepted, using data through 2008 and Stock Synthesis 3.03a. The 2025 PFMC stock-structure review calls it the first and only benchmark assessment. Preliminary NMFS recommendations published in 2026 still list 2009 as the last assessment and identify greenstriped rockfish as an alternative for a possible 2027 benchmark; that is a future possibility, not a completed production run. The detailed 2009 assessment is therefore the latest official run located for the current unit.

## Inputs and outputs available

The 2009 assessment reports reconstructed removals and length compositions for five fishery groups: Washington/Oregon trawl, California trawl, foreign, other commercial gears, and coastwide recreational. It models a long catch history beginning in 1916 and estimates retention and discards for selected gears. The report says age observations were available only from the NWFSC survey. It explicitly notes that previously collected ages from the 1995 triennial survey and Oregon landings were not available to the assessment. Thus the accepted run has no fishery age composition or catch-at-age series, and the fishery catch totals plus length compositions are insufficient for a source-supported catch-at-age reconstruction.

Three aggregate survey biomass series are used: early triennial (1980-1992), late triennial (1995-2004), and NWFSC shelf-slope trawl survey (2003-2008). The NWFSC survey contributes sex- and year-specific conditional age-at-length observations, age bins 1-45 with a 45+ group, and about 3,472 aged fish. These are not a directly fitted age-specific abundance index. A marginal age composition can be calculated from the conditional age-at-length and length data for limited survey years, but it does not supply the missing fishery catch-at-age data.

The report provides weight-at-length relationships in kg and cm from Love et al. (1990), Shaw and Gunderson (2006), and NWFSC survey samples from 2003-2008. It estimates sex-specific growth from survey age and length samples; an age-weight surface would be derived from those growth and weight-length relationships, not copied from an observed weight-at-age series. The report gives size-based maturity and fecundity information, and the assessment model fixes a maturity ogive and fecundity relationship; the final model specification lists first mature age 2. Natural mortality is fixed at 0.08 per year for both sexes.

The model timing table lists fishery timing 0.5, early triennial timing 0.6, late triennial timing 0.5, and NWFSC survey timing 0.5. These are assessment settings; the triennial survey's operating dates varied among years. The assessment report includes an annual numbers-at-age matrix and aggregate spawning output, depletion, and exploitation results. The numbers-at-age are fitted population outputs and cannot be substituted for observed age inputs.

## Screening decision

This is a new current-unit candidate, marked ineligible for the age-disaggregated tinyAM source set. The detailed assessment has survey age-at-length observations and fitted numbers-at-age, but no fishery age compositions or accepted-run quantities that support fishery catch-at-age reconstruction. The official model's age structure and output tables do not cure that input gap. No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added, and no translation was attempted.
