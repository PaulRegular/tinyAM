# NOAA North Pacific blue shark assessment (2022)

NOAA Stock SMART ID 11686 links to the fully accepted 2022 ISC benchmark assessment for the North Pacific blue shark stock, through 2020. This is an ISC-managed, highly migratory stock unit, distinct from the North Atlantic blue shark row (NOAA:10689) and the South Pacific stock.

The assessment is a fully integrated, size-based, age- and sex-structured Stock Synthesis model. Its annual inputs include total catch, standardized CPUE abundance indices, and sex-specific length compositions. Catch covers 1971–2020 (mostly metric tons, with three fleets reported in thousands of fish); CPUE covers 1976–2020; length compositions cover 1979–2020. Length bins are not age compositions. The public input documentation does not describe catch-at-age or age-specific abundance-index observations.

The official assessment reports annual spawning-stock biomass, aggregate fishing mortality, age-0 recruitment, management quantities, projections, and ensemble uncertainty. Its published tables and Stock SMART time series do not provide numeric annual N-at-age or F-at-age arrays. The model's internal age structure therefore does not supply the age-disaggregated observations and comparison surfaces required for the current candidate set. This stock is screened ineligible for that set; no canonical database rows were added.
