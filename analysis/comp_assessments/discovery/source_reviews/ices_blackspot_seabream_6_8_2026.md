# Blackspot seabream in ICES subareas 6–8: 2026 screen

**Review date:** 2026-10-30  
**Official stock code:** `sbr.27.6-8`  
**Scientific name:** *Pagellus bogaraveo*

## Stock identity and assessment

The 2026 ICES advice and Standard Graphs identify blackspot seabream in
subareas 6–8 as one current management unit. The 2026 advice is category 6 and
recommends zero catch for 2027–2028. The linked 2026 Standard Graphs record is
the latest current-unit assessment entry reviewed. The 2024 Standard Graphs
record was checked as a prior-year cross-check, not as a replacement for the
2026 record.

## Inputs and assumptions

The 2026 Standard Graphs source table contains annual landings, catches, and
discards. Its age-and-length fields give recruitment age 0 and leave F age
blank; it does not provide catch-at-age, age compositions, age-specific survey
indices, weights-at-age, maturity-at-age, or mortality assumptions. The 2024
source table likewise exposes aggregate landings, catches, and discards, not an
age-disaggregated observation set.

## Official outputs

The 2026 advice recommends zero catch under category 6. The current Standard
Graphs record does not expose recruitment, SSB, F, or population-at-age
trajectories for this unit. The reviewed official material therefore does not
provide comparable age-structured population outputs.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new candidate, screened **ineligible** for the
age-disaggregated assessment set. The current advice and linked official source
tables provide aggregate catch history and a catch recommendation, but no
age-specific model inputs or population outputs. The decision is based on the
current official assessment record; the older 2024 table does not supply the
missing age-specific series.

No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output records were added. No tinyAM
translation was attempted.
