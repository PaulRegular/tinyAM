# ICES lesser spotted dogfish in Celtic Seas, 2025

ICES stock `syc.27.67a-ce-j` is lesser spotted dogfish in Subarea 6 and
divisions 7.a–c and 7.e–j. Its official code and assessment area do not match
a canonical stock record. It is distinct from the other ICES lesser spotted
dogfish units.

The latest assessment located is the 2025 ICES Working Group on Elasmobranch
Fishes (WGEF) assessment, section 25.9.3. It applies the category 3 `rfb`
rule. Assessment inputs listed in the report are landings, four normalized
trawl-survey biomass indices, and commercial length-frequency data for
landings and discards. The survey series are EVHOE-WIBTS-Q4,
IE-IGFS-WIBTS-Q4, SP-PORC-WIBTS-Q3, and UK(E&W)-BTS-Q3. Their combined
relative biomass-index series covers 2005–2024. The report says length data
from 2020–2024 were used, with the length reference point calculated from
pooled 2020–2022 observations.

The report and ICES Standard Graphs source data identify aggregate `B_index`
in kg/km² and `Fproxy` as a ratio. The 2025 report gives a 5194 t RFB
calculation and landings advice of 4781 t for 2026 and 2027. It uses a
length-based `LF = M` proxy with `Lc` 42 cm, 2024 `Lmean` 55.4 cm, `Linf`
87.4 cm, and `k` 0.118. The sources do not describe catch numbers-at-age,
age-composition likelihoods, age-specific survey observations,
weight-at-age, maturity-at-age, or an age-specific natural-mortality process.
They do not provide comparable N-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, SSB, or
recruitment estimates; the Standard Graphs age-specific F field is blank.

This unit is therefore **ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment
set** on the production-assessment evidence reviewed. This finding concerns
the assessment inputs and outputs, not whether age samples exist in national
biological monitoring. No canonical numerical data or tinyAM translation
were added.
