# NOAA Bering Sea/Aleutian Islands shortraker rockfish (2024)

## Identity and assessment

Stock SMART 10553 is the NOAA Bering Sea/Aleutian Islands unit of shortraker rockfish (*Sebastes borealis*). It is separate from the Gulf of Alaska stock unit, NOAA 10554. No canonical tinyAM stock, assessment, input, or output record matches either unit.

The November 2024 report describes an operational update using Model 22_2024, the 2022 REMA model updated with 2024 Aleutian Islands bottom-trawl survey biomass and 2023 eastern Bering Sea slope longline relative population weights. The 2024 BSAI Plan Team recommended the authors’ model. The final 2025 SAFE chapter treated 2025 as a catch-report year; the final 2025 SSC report says the next full assessment was scheduled for 2026. As of this review date, the 2024 update is the latest detailed production model available.

Sources are cached in source_cache/discovery_noaa_shortraker_2023_2025/; sources.csv records their URLs, revisions, retrieval date, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes.

## Assessment data and outputs

The assessment updates total catch through 22 October 2024 and lists fishery and survey length compositions. Its population model is REMA, fit to design-based survey biomass estimates and observation errors, with relative population weights from the EBS-slope longline survey. It does not fit catch-at-age, age compositions, or an age-specific abundance index. The report contains no numeric annual N-at-age or F-at-age surface.

Natural mortality is fixed at 0.03 per year under Tier 5. The model estimates exploitable biomass by region and survey scaling/process terms; harvest reference rates are set from M. The report provides aggregate biomass trajectories with uncertainty and catch limits, including 2024 biomass of 21,018 t. It does not provide the age-indexed inputs and outputs needed for the candidate set.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new current assessment unit, but it is ineligible for the age-disaggregated candidate set. The age-structured model-input requirement is not met: the operational model is a survey-biomass random-effects model, and the report lists no age-composition likelihood. No canonical data were imported.
