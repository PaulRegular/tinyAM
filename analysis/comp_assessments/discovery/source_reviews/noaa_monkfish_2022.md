# NOAA monkfish (2022)

## Units and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART IDs 10500 and 10501 identify distinct *Lophius americanus*
management units: Goosefish - Gulf of Maine / Northern Georges Bank, and
Goosefish - Southern Georges Bank / Mid-Atlantic. The 2022 operational
assessment is the latest row in the pinned Stock SMART snapshot for both units.
Stock SMART records model category 2 (index-based), catch input level 5,
abundance input level 3, biological input level 2, and composition input level
2. The composition score is summary metadata; it does not prove that age
compositions entered an age-structured population likelihood.

The 2022 NEFSC Fall Management Track report updates the 2019 Ismooth/Plan B
approach. It says an analytical assessment was not possible because a reliable
aging method was unavailable. Both units use aggregate catch and spring/fall
bottom-trawl survey biomass indices in Ismooth. The report rescales the two
index series by their means, combines the fall index in year y with the spring
index in year y+1, smooths the series, and uses its recent trend to derive
catch advice. For the 2020 gap, the preferred approach filled the missing
survey indices using the mean of the 2019 and 2021 observations.

## Inputs and outputs relevant to this audit

Catch is reported as aggregate discards, landings, and assessment catch by year
(2012-2021), in metric tonnes. The report does not provide catch numbers-at-age.
The spring and fall NEFSC indices are aggregate biomass indices, not
age-specific abundance series. The report provides aggregate index plots with
approximate 90% log-normal intervals, not N-at-age, recruitment, F-at-age, or
M-at-age surfaces. The Ismooth catch multipliers are 0.829 for the Northern
unit and 0.646 for the Southern unit. Reference points and stock status are
reported as unknown.

Both candidates are **ineligible** for this age-disaggregated comparison set.
This is a statement about the verified assessment's inputs and outputs; it is
not evidence that biological age samples are absent outside the assessment.
No canonical stock, assessment, input, or output rows were added.

## Provenance

The NOAA Stock SMART snapshot identifies the assessment rows as 2022
Operational, with model labels Plan Ismooth for ID 10500 and Ismooth for ID
10501. Both are labelled “Full acceptance” in Stock SMART. The Fall 2022
report's monkfish sections describe the methods and panel recommendations. The
official NOAA repository PDF could not be downloaded in this session; its
