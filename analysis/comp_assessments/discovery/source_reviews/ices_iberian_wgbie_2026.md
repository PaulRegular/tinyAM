# Iberian megrim and sole: 2026 working-group screening

The [2026 ICES WGBIE report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32790321.v1) contains a regional assessment listing as well as stock-specific sections. This review covers two separate stocks: megrim (*Lepidorhombus whiffiagonis*) and sole (*Solea solea*) in divisions 8.c and 9.a. The canonical stock table contains no *L. whiffiagonis* entry and its sole entries represent other geographic units. In particular, this sole is distinct from the northern Bay of Biscay `sol.27.8ab` candidate. Neither entry creates a canonical stock record.

## Megrim: `meg.27.8c9a`

Section 6 describes the current age-structured a4a assessment using catches, discards, biological inputs and the Spanish SpGFS-WIBTS-Q4 survey (G2784), used at ages 1-6 from 1983 onward. The report says catch weights-at-age for 1986-2025 also serve as stock weights; mortality and maturity are referred to the stock annex. It plots some age surfaces and refers to tables for discard numbers-at-age, but these citations do not establish that the full numerical input matrices are available in the downloaded section. Recover the actual 2026 model inputs, stock annex and survey timing rather than transcribing or treating plotted values as numerical source data.

Table 6.3.5 supplies historical population results, and the [ICES assessment graph source table, key 22577](https://standardgraphs.ices.dk/ViewSourceData.aspx?key=22577), supplies age-1 recruitment, SSB and mean F at ages 2-4, including low/high values. Confirm the run identity, point-estimate convention and uncertainty level. Eligibility remains unresolved until numerical inputs and biology are verified.

## Southern sole: `sol.27.8c9a`

Section 8 says the official advice uses length-based indicators and Spanish survey and Portuguese LPUE biomass indices. Its fishery length frequencies cover 2011-2025; the 2025 ICES advice uses key 19817 and applies for 2026-2027. The 2026 working group updated the length and biomass information rather than issuing a new age-structured assessment. The reviewed report does not establish recoverable catch numbers-at-age, age-specific survey indices or comparable age-structured outputs. This is a source limitation, not proof that no external age data exist; search the cited benchmark and public survey sources before deciding eligibility.
