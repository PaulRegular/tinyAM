# Iberian megrim and sole: 2026 working-group screening

The [2026 ICES WGBIE report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32790321.v1) contains a regional assessment listing as well as stock-specific sections. This review covers two separate stocks: megrim (*Lepidorhombus whiffiagonis*) and sole (*Solea solea*) in divisions 8.c and 9.a. The canonical stock table contains no *L. whiffiagonis* entry and its sole entries represent other geographic units. In particular, this sole is distinct from the northern Bay of Biscay `sol.27.8ab` candidate. Neither entry creates a canonical stock record.

## Megrim: `meg.27.8c9a`

Section 6 describes the current age-structured a4a assessment using catches, discards, biological inputs and the Spanish SpGFS-WIBTS-Q4 survey (G2784), used at ages 1-6 from 1983 onward. The report says catch weights-at-age for 1986-2025 also serve as stock weights. The [2023 Stock Annex, revision v1](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.23261081.v1) was recovered and cached. It specifies M=0.2 for all ages and years, female maturity-at-age of 0, 0.06, 0.55, 0.96, 1 and 1 for ages 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5+, and describes the age-specific biological inputs. These definitions help identify the model assumptions, but their match to the 2026 production run still needs confirmation.

The 2026 report provides discard numbers-at-age, not a complete numerical landings-at-age matrix. It describes the G2784 age-index series but the complete numerical age-index matrices and exact survey timing were not recovered from the reviewed public sources. Plotted surfaces are not treated as numerical observations. Table 6.3.5 supplies historical population results, and the [ICES assessment graph source table, key 22577](https://standardgraphs.ices.dk/ViewSourceData.aspx?key=22577), supplies age-1 recruitment, SSB and mean F at ages 2-4, including low/high values. Confirm the run identity, point-estimate convention and uncertainty level. Screening is complete; eligibility remains unresolved because complete, run-matched catch and survey age inputs are unavailable.

## Southern sole: `sol.27.8c9a`

Section 8 says the official advice uses length-based indicators and Spanish survey and Portuguese LPUE biomass indices. Its fishery length frequencies cover 2011-2025; the 2025 ICES advice uses key 19817 and applies for 2026-2027. The 2026 working group updated the length and biomass information rather than issuing a new age-structured assessment. The reviewed report does not establish recoverable catch numbers-at-age, age-specific survey indices or comparable age-structured outputs. This is a source limitation, not proof that no external age data exist; search the cited benchmark and public survey sources before deciding eligibility.

For southern sole, screening is complete and the current assessment is ineligible for the age-structured candidate set. The 2026 WGBIE report describes the latest update as length-based indicators and biomass trends; the latest advice series (key 19817) reports age-0 recruitment and age-aggregated biomass/F indicators, not age-specific model inputs or fitted population surfaces. The reviewed current sources therefore do not establish the catch-at-age, age-specific abundance or composition inputs, or age-structured official outputs required for this audit. This is a conclusion about the current production assessment and available source evidence, not a claim that age samples do not exist.

The WGBIE megrim and sole sections, working-document annex, Stock Annex and megrim SAG source page are cached under `source_cache/discovery_ices_wgbie_2026/`; `sources.csv` gives retrieval dates, source URLs, revisions and SHA-256 checksums. This is a partial regional review, not a complete audit of WGBIE stocks.
