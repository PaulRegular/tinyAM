# DFO Newfoundland and Labrador Atlantic salmon inventory entry

Reviewed 2026-10-29. The discovery row
`DFO:Newfoundland and Labrador:Atlantic Salmon - NL` is a regional grouping,
not a single stock unit. DFO describes river-level populations across SFAs
1–14B (407 known salmon-bearing rivers in the 2025 full-assessment report).
Only a subset is monitored in any given year, with some areas lacking current
abundance assessments. The candidate register leaves the canonical and
candidate unit identifiers blank and marks identity as ambiguous.

The latest public status update is for 2025, published as Science Response
2026/021. It says the last full assessment covered 2023 returns and was
published as Science Advisory Report 2025/025; the next full assessment was
anticipated for late 2026. The 2026/021 update explicitly describes the
current approach as index-based and the assessment unit as individual rivers.
Its data include adult and smolt counts, fishery removals, and biological age
and size information collected at some monitoring facilities. However, the
reported assessment inputs are not catch numbers-at-age or an age-specific
abundance likelihood. Outputs are river-level total returns, smolt abundance,
marine survival, and egg-deposition status, not population N/F/M-at-age.

The 2023 full-assessment report (2025/025) and detailed 2022 Research Document
2024/075 were also reviewed. The 2025/025 report describes count-based returns,
size-group fisheries and river-specific biological calculations; it does not
provide a catch-age series or age-structured population outputs. The 2024/075
report is an earlier assessment and is not used to fill gaps in the current
assessment.

The row is screened ineligible as one age-structured candidate because it
groups river-specific populations and the current production method is
index-based rather than a catch-at-age population model. This is not a finding
that every individual river lacks useful age data; any river-specific follow-up
must first establish the current official unit and run-matched data.

Sources:

- [DFO Science Response 2026/021](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.962539/publication.html), 2025 status update.
- [DFO Science Advisory Report 2025/025](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/41294713.pdf), full assessment of 2023 returns.
- [DFO Research Document 2024/075](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2024/mpo-dfo/fs70-5/Fs70-5-2024-075-eng.pdf), detailed assessment of 2022 returns.
