# DFO British Columbia Longspine Thornyhead

## Unit and current assessment status

The DFO inventory entry identifies Longspine Thornyhead (*Sebastolobus altivelis*) in the Pacific Region. No matching canonical tinyAM stock record was found. The 2024 DFO groundfish activity report says a stock assessment had been requested but was not scheduled; it also reports that an acceptable ageing protocol was not available. Earlier DFO research documents exist, but no recent age-structured production assessment was located.

## Age data and outputs

DFO's 2025 review of commercial groundfish biological samples covers 1996–2022. For Longspine Thornyhead it reports 34,488 collected age structures, none of which had been aged. The review describes length, sex, and specimen sampling, but this does not provide catch-at-age or age-composition observations. The reviewed sources do not provide age-specific assessment inputs or current official N-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, recruitment, or SSB outputs for the BC unit.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct provisional DFO Pacific stock unit, not a match to an existing canonical stock. It is ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set because current age observations and a current age-structured production assessment were not verified. This decision does not imply that the species has never been assessed: historical DFO research documents exist, and the 2025 biological-sampling review is not itself a stock assessment.
