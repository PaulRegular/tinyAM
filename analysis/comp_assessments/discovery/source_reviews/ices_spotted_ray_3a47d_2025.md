# ICES spotted ray in Subarea 4 and divisions 3.a and 7.d, 2025 review

ICES unit `rjm.27.3a47d` is spotted ray (*Raja montagui*) in the Greater North
Sea. The 2025 WGEF report gives a detailed current assessment and advice run.
It describes reconstructed landings and discards as total catch input, plus a
continuous-time surplus-production model (SPiCT). Catch data are not split
into ages.

Fishery-independent observations are combined into two seasonal biomass
indices using a Delta-lognormal generalized additive model. One index combines
NS-IBTS-Q1 with FR-CGFS-Q4; a second combines Q3 surveys. The assessment
reports relative biomass and relative fishing pressure, retrospective and
hindcast diagnostics, and catch advice for 2026–2027. It does not use annual
catch-at-age or age compositions, and it does not report population numbers,
fishing mortality, or natural mortality by age. These SPiCT outputs are not
SSB or age-specific F.

The assessment is documented and has usable aggregate inputs and outputs, but
it is **ineligible for the age-disaggregated inventory**: its latest official
model is a surplus-production assessment rather than an age-structured model.
The report also notes that the 2022 catch observation is an outlier and that
first-index OSA residual tests show dependence; those are model-quality caveats
to preserve if the aggregate assessment is curated later. No canonical
numerical records or tinyAM translation were added.
