# ICES lesser spotted dogfish in divisions 8.c and 9.a, 2025

ICES unit `syc.27.8c9a` is lesser spotted dogfish in divisions 8.c and 9.a
(Cantabrian Sea and Atlantic Iberian waters). The official code and area do
not match a canonical stock record. The unit is distinct from the other ICES
lesser spotted dogfish units.

The latest detailed assessment located is in the 2025 Working Group on
Elasmobranch Fishes (WGEF) report, section 25.9.5. The report states that a
new assessment was done in 2025, adding landings length data from 2023 and
2024. It uses a length-at-capture indicator (`Lc`) defined from pooled
2017–2022 lengths, alongside a rule based on normalized survey biomass
indices. ICES did not receive a request for fishing-opportunity advice for
2026–2027; the report presents assessment indicators and an RFB calculation,
not a new landings recommendation.

The assessment inputs described in the report are aggregate landings,
commercial length-frequency distributions, and a combined biomass index. The
index averages normalized biomass indices from the Portuguese Q4 groundfish
survey, Spanish Gulf of Cadiz Q1 and Q4 surveys, and Northern Spanish Q4
survey. Its combined series runs from 1993 to 2024. The report discusses
growth parameters used by the length-based indicator (`Linf` 70 cm and `k`
0.15), but does not describe catch-at-age, age-composition likelihoods,
age-specific survey observations, age-specific weights, maturity-at-age, or
an age-specific natural-mortality process. The report shows the length-based
fishing-pressure proxy, aggregate stock-size index and rule-based calculation;
it does not provide comparable N-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, SSB or
recruitment series.

This unit is therefore **ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment
set** on the production-assessment evidence reviewed. The finding concerns
the assessment's inputs and outputs; it does not establish that age samples
are absent from national biological sampling. No canonical numerical data or
tinyAM translation were added.
