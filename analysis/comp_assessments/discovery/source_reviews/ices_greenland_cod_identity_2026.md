# Greenland cod identity review

**Review date:** 2026-11-01  
**Inventory:** ICES Stock Information Database snapshot retrieved 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** Identity and assessment-component crosswalk only; age-input eligibility remains unresolved.

## Finding

ICES identifies three separate Greenland cod assessment components in its 2026 advice: West Greenland offshore spawning cod (`cod.21.1.osc`), West Greenland inshore spawning cod (`cod.21.1.isc`), and East Greenland–Iceland offshore spawning cod (`cod.21.27.1.14`). The 2026 advice describes the NAFO Subarea 1 fishery as a mixture of these stocks and warns that management should avoid exceeding advice for any one of them. That shared fishery does not make the spawning components one assessment unit.

All three are distinct from the cod units in the canonical database. Existing ICES cod units cover the North Sea/West of Scotland and Northeast Arctic; DFO and NOAA cod records cover different Northwestern Atlantic areas. None has a Greenland stock code or the spawning-area boundary listed by ICES. The three ICES identifiers are therefore linked to separate provisional candidate units; no canonical assessment or numerical record is created.

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database lists the 2026 assessments as Category 1 for the two West Greenland components and Category 6 for East Greenland–Iceland. These method categories help prioritize subsequent evidence searches but do not establish the age-specific likelihood inputs, biological surfaces, or reported age-specific outputs needed for eligibility. Screening remains not started and eligibility remains unresolved for all three records.

- `cod.21.1.osc`: [ICES advice 5088](https://asd.ices.dk/viewAdvice/5088)
- `cod.21.1.isc`: [ICES advice 5089](https://asd.ices.dk/viewAdvice/5089)
- `cod.21.27.1.14`: [ICES advice 5091](https://asd.ices.dk/viewAdvice/5091)

The [ICES Stock Information Database](https://sid.ices.dk/Default.aspx) and the pinned local 2026 SID snapshot provide the names and geographic definitions used for the initial crosswalk. Detailed 2026 assessment inputs and age-specific outputs have not yet been reviewed.
