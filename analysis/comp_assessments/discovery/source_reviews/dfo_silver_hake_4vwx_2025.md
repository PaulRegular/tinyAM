# DFO 4VWX Silver Hake

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO identifies a separate Scotian Shelf management unit in NAFO Divisions 4VWX, *Merluccius bilinearis*.

## Current assessment and data

The latest detailed current-unit assessment located is the 2025 stock status update, reviewed by CSAS on December 2-3, 2025 and published as Science Response 2026/011 in March 2026. The response says the 2025 biomass is above the upper stock reference and gives catch advice for 2026/27. It also describes the current assessment method: an analytical reconstruction developed in the 2012 framework from commercial landings and DFO Maritimes Summer RV Survey data for 1993-2011, using a logistic biomass-dynamics model.

The current update uses aggregate landings and survey biomass, not catch-at-age or age-specific survey likelihoods. It reports biomass status, recruitment trends, aggregate fishing mortality relative to FMSY, and catch advice. It does not report age-specific population abundance, fishing mortality, weight, or maturity. The survey season is identified as summer, but a within-year observation fraction is not given.

The 2012 framework and its assessment report discuss earlier age-based/VPA analyses. These historical methods are not the current 2025 assessment and are not used to fill gaps in the current-unit record. The latest current unit has recoverable run-specific aggregate inputs and status outputs, but not the age-disaggregated inputs/outputs required for the candidate age-structured assessment set. It is screened ineligible for that set.

The DFO 4VWX unit remains distinct from NOAA's northern and southern U.S. silver hake management units. Shared species identity and regional proximity are insufficient evidence to combine their assessment records.

No canonical database records or numerical series were imported.
