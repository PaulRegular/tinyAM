# Eastern Pacific striped marlin (NOAA:11073; IATTC assessment available in 2010)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**NOAA Stock SMART name:** Striped marlin - Eastern Pacific
**Scientific name:** *Kajikia audax*
**Assessment area:** IATTC northern EPO, north of 5°S and east of 145°W; described in the report as the northeast Pacific stock.
**Latest NOAA Stock SMART assessment:** 2010; assessment ID 5266; data through 2008; Stock Synthesis; full acceptance. The IATTC detailed status report uses data through 2009 and information available on 30 October 2010.

No canonical tinyAM stock matches this IATTC unit. NOAA's current species page identifies the Eastern Tropical Pacific as a separate striped marlin stock and lists the 2010 IATTC assessment as its latest assessment. The WCNPO striped marlin row is a separate western and central Pacific unit, so it is not merged with this candidate.

## Inputs and outputs

The IATTC assessment used annual fishery catch/removals, standardized longline CPUE indices and size compositions. The detailed report describes six fisheries across Japanese longline subareas, other longline fleets, Mexican recreational fishing and purse seine. It reports annual catch in tonnes, longline catch and effort, Japanese longline length compositions in 2-cm intervals for assessment (with 1-cm and 5-cm source measurements combined), and purse-seine length compositions in 2-cm intervals. The assessment data span 1975–2009; 1954–1974 were used only in sensitivities. The report explicitly states that no age-at-length data were available for the assessment.

Stock Synthesis is internally age-structured, with maximum age 15, but the observed population inputs are catch, CPUE and length compositions. The report fixes natural mortality at 0.5 per year and growth parameters K = 0.25 and Linf = 275 cm; maturity is assumed to be 50% at age 2 and complete thereafter. These biological assumptions and model age bins do not provide age-composition observations or age-specific abundance indices. Catch-at-age cannot be recovered from the published length compositions without additional age-composition or age-length keys; no such assessment input was verified.

The report publishes annual spawning biomass and recruitment results through 2009, aggregate stock-status quantities, estimated fishing mortality by fishery, and average F-at-age by time period. It does not publish annual N-at-age and F-at-age surfaces suitable for a matched comparison. The abundance indices are fishery CPUE, not seasonal surveys, so no survey timing fraction applies.

## Screening decision

This is a **new, screened, ineligible candidate** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. Although the production assessment is an age-structured Stock Synthesis model and provides some age-indexed fitted results, its population likelihood is based on catch, aggregate CPUE and length compositions. The assessment report explicitly says there were no age-at-length data; no catch-at-age, population age composition, age-specific abundance index, or defensible age reconstruction was found. The official report's biomass and recruitment tables do not replace the missing age-specific population inputs.

No canonical numerical records were added and no tinyAM translation was attempted. The IATTC assessment and status report, retrieval date, byte sizes, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in the ignored local cache at `source_cache/discovery_noaa_iattc_striped_marlin_2010/sources.csv`.
