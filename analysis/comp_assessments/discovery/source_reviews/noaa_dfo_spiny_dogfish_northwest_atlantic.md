# Northwest Atlantic spiny dogfish: cross-agency discovery review

The DFO Sustainability Survey lists Atlantic Canada spiny dogfish in NAFO 4VWX5; NOAA Stock SMART lists Atlantic spiny dogfish as stock 10701. These records concern the same transboundary population, but their current assessment units are not one-to-one. DFO's historical accepted model represented the Northwest Atlantic population with Canadian and U.S. regions, seasonal steps, sex and maturity stages. The NOAA 2023 assessment is the U.S. management-track model. Keep the DFO row's identity crosswalk unresolved and group both records under the provisional candidate unit `spiny_dogfish_northwest_atlantic` so they are not counted as two independent biological stocks.

The most recent accepted DFO population model located is the stage-based framework with a 2013 update, published as DFO Research Document 2015/065. The 2016 stock-status update continued to use that framework. DFO's 2018 assessment-model update was not accepted as a basis for advice; the 2019 proceedings say DFO would rely on the U.S. stock assessment until a Canadian or joint model could be developed. The 2024 DFO survey-trends report provides biomass and indices-at-length, not a new age-structured population assessment.

NOAA's latest identified production assessment is the 2023 Management Track model, using data through 2022 and Stock Synthesis 3. Its report explicitly identifies the lack of age and growth data as an important source of uncertainty. It uses catch totals, survey indices and length-based information; its reported outputs include spawning output in million pups, fully selected fishing mortality and recruits in million pups, not N-at-age or F-at-age. This evidence does not support the age-disaggregated inputs and outputs required for the current assessment candidate screen. Both agency rows are screened ineligible for an age-structured tinyAM candidate; this is distinct from saying the species has no age or growth observations in any research dataset.

The official DFO reports, 2024 survey-trends report and NOAA 2023 management-track report are cached under `source_cache/discovery_dfo_spiny_dogfish_4vwx5/` and `source_cache/discovery_noaa_spiny_dogfish_atlantic_2023/`. Each cache has a `sources.csv` manifest with source URL, retrieval date and SHA-256 checksum. No canonical stock or numerical assessment data are added by this review.
