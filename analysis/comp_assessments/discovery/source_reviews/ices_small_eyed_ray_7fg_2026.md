# ICES Celtic Seas small-eyed ray (rje.27.7fg), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

This is the ICES stock unit for small-eyed ray (*Raja microocellata*) in
divisions 7.f and 7.g (Bristol Channel and northern Celtic Sea). It is distinct
from the 7.d–7.e unit. The latest published advice located is the 2026 ICES
assessment, key 25855, for advice in 2027–2028. ICES classifies it as Category
3 and advises a landings limit of 124 tonnes per year; the advice says it
cannot quantify the corresponding catches.

## Assessment evidence

The 2026 Standard Graphs source reports an aggregate biomass index in
kilograms per hour. Its annual table reports aggregate SSB/index estimates,
landings, and uncertainty bounds; it does not provide age-specific catch,
survey abundance, or population surfaces. Recruitment age is listed as zero,
age-specific fishing mortality is blank, and the stock graphs contain no
N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age output. No weight-at-age, maturity-at-age, or
age-specific natural mortality inputs are reported.

The available evidence describes an aggregate index-based assessment, not an
age-structured assessment with recoverable age-disaggregated likelihood data.
It does not support the minimum age-specific input and output set for tinyAM.

## Screening decision

This stock unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment set.
No earlier stock unit was substituted and no canonical stock, assessment,
input, or output rows were added.
