# ICES blue ling in subareas 6, 7, 12 and Division 5.b (2026)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31
**Official stock code:** `bli.27.5b6712`
**Scientific name:** *Molva dypterygia*
**Assessment year:** 2026

## Stock identity and current assessment

The ICES 2026 Stock Information Database (SID), Advice and Scenarios Database,
Standard Graphs, and SAG data identify the current unit as
`bli.27.5b6712`. It is distinct from the separately listed Icelandic blue ling
unit `bli.27.5a14` and has no matching stock in the canonical database. SID
describes the current method as a trend assessment from a biomass index and a
length-based indicator. The 2026 advice lists category 3.2 and recommends
catches no greater than 6,536 tonnes in each of 2027 and 2028. The advice
applies to Division 5.b, subareas 6 and 7, and Division 12.b; it does not cover
blue ling in 12.a or 12.c.

recruitment-age, F-age, or F-length definition and no age-data table. The
reviewed current-run sources do not provide weights-at-age, maturity-at-age,
natural-mortality-at-age, catch-at-age, or age-specific survey observations.

samples or biological data are absent from national source systems.
