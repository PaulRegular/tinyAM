# NOAA Atlantic goliath grouper, SEDAR 47 (2016)

## Identity and assessment run

NOAA Stock SMART lists two 2016 records for *Epinephelus itajara*: ID 10315 is
labeled Gulf of Mexico (assessment ID 6915), while ID 11042 is labeled Southern
Atlantic Coast / Gulf of Mexico (assessment ID 12476). Both point to the same
SEDAR 47 assessment family. The report describes one Southeastern U.S.
population, so these inventory rows are linked as one audit unit rather than
counted as separate assessments. ID 11042 also has an older 2010 assessment
(ID 2929), partially accepted for status determinations only; it is not the
latest run.

SEDAR 47 is the latest detailed assessment located by this screen. NOAA Stock
SMART records both 2016 entries as rejected because the results were too
uncertain. The current Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission page
describes alternative management goals and population indicators adopted in
2018 instead of a full-scale traditional stock assessment. No later full
production assessment with age-disaggregated inputs and comparable outputs was
located by 2026-10-31.

## Age-disaggregated evidence

The independent SEDAR 47 review states that catch data had no age structure.
The report used an age composition from only 22 adult research-fishery fish;
this does not provide annual fishery catch-at-age or an adequate basis to
reconstruct it across the catch history. The assessment presented four
aggregate abundance indices: three recreational catch-per-trip series and one
SCUBA visual index. No age-specific survey observations were identified.

The report tabulates modeled mid-year weight-at-age in kg for ages 0–40. This is
a model-derived biological curve, not a complete annual observed weight-at-age
input series. The assessment used assumed reproductive functions; the report
notes limited information on age and size at maturity and fecundity. Its models
used constant natural mortality even though episodic cold-kill events likely
changed mortality. No fractional-year timing for the aggregate indices was
located.

SEDAR 47 reports age-structured model outputs, but its review panel concluded
the assessment was not adequate for status inferences or management advice.
NOAA Stock SMART records both 2016 entries as rejected. These model outputs are
therefore not treated as accepted comparable official outputs for the
age-disaggregated candidate set.

## Screening decision

The grouped Southeastern U.S. unit is screened ineligible for the expansion's
age-disaggregated source set. The decisive gaps are the absence of age-structured
catch observations, the very small research age sample, and the rejection of
the latest detailed assessment for management use. The 2010 partial-acceptance
record is older and does not define a separate current unit. No canonical stock,
assessment, input, or output values were imported.
