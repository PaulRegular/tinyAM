# Icelandic Atlantic wolffish (`caa.27.5a`), 2026 assessment

Reviewed 2026-10-29. ICES lists Atlantic wolffish (*Anarhichas lupus*) in Division 5.a (Iceland grounds) for assessment year 2026, Standard Graphs key 22633. The MFRI production assessment was published on 12 June 2026 and uses data through 2025. This is a distinct Icelandic unit; the NOAA Stock SMART entry 11562 is Atlantic wolffish in the Gulf of Maine/Georges Bank and is not the same unit. No canonical tinyAM stock record uses `caa.27.5a`.

The latest production method is a statistical catch-at-age SAM. The MFRI report describes commercial catch-at-age and landings from 1979 onward, with total landings used where age-disaggregated catches are unavailable in 1981-1997. The model tracks ages 4-16+, with age 16 as a plus group. The 2022 ICES Stock Annex describes an earlier catch-age span (1979 and 1996 onward, with landings for 1980-1995), so the difference in the covered years must be resolved against any run-matched input files before numerical import. Do not combine or silently substitute the two versions.

The assessment uses age-disaggregated numbers from the Icelandic spring groundfish survey (IS-SMB), beginning in 1985, and the autumn groundfish survey (IS-SMH), beginning in 2000. The spring survey is conducted in March; the autumn survey is sampled mostly in October. These correspond approximately to 0.17 and 0.83 of a calendar year. The stock annex says autumn age-composition information is based on spring-survey age data; whether this remains true in the 2026 production inputs needs confirmation. The annual age-specific series are presented in figures rather than downloadable numeric tables.

The 2026 report shows stock weights-at-age from spring-survey data and annual proportion mature-at-age. The 2022 Stock Annex describes stock weights estimated from spring-survey weight-length data, and catch weights from commercial catch data; both are expressed as weight per fish after division by numbers. It describes maturity proportions from length-based ogives fitted to autumn survey and commercial observations, held to a fixed annual effect and smoothed with a three-year moving average. The production report says SSB uses mature females; available maturity samples come from autumn survey and commercial catches in June-December. Current annual numeric weight and maturity series were not found outside report figures. The production assessment assumes M = 0.15 per year for all ages; M is not estimated.

ICES Standard Graphs key 22633 reports annual age-4 recruitment (thousands), SSB (tonnes), and total F for ages 10-15, each with 95% intervals. The fitted series ends in 2025; 2026-2028 values in the MFRI report are projections. Full fitted N-at-age and F-at-age matrices, along with run-specific numeric catch, survey and biology matrices, were not located in the reviewed public outputs.

The current production report, advice, graph page, structured output page and 2022 stock annex are cached under the gitignored `source_cache/discovery_ices_iceland_wolffish_2026/`; `sources.csv` records their URLs, retrieval date, run/DOI, size and SHA-256. The general 2022 WKICEMP benchmark report is reused from the adjacent plaice cache to avoid retaining a second 35 MB copy. A search of ICES TAF and stockassessment.org by stock code and species did not locate a current run repository or fitted object. The `stockassessment` R package is not installed in this execution environment, so `fitfromweb()` could not be queried; this does not prove that a fitted object is unavailable.

This unit is eligible for source curation: the latest report confirms catch-at-age, two age-disaggregated survey series, age-specific biological inputs, fixed M, survey timing and comparable official outputs. It is not yet a verified numerical input bundle. Recover plotted series only with documented digitization and precision limits, and resolve the catch-age year discrepancy before import.
