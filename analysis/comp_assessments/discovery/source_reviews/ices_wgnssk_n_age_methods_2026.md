# ICES WGNSSK current non-age methods (2026)

**Review date:** 2026-10-30  
**Source:** [ICES WGNSSK 2026 report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32676345.v2)

## Grey gurnard (`gug.27.3a47d`)

The current unit is distinct and not present in the canonical stocks table. The
2026 audit describes a category-3 trends method based on IBTS Q1 and Q3 survey
indices and limited catch data. The rb rule uses a survey stock-size indicator
for advice. The report describes length-weight and maturity inputs for a
mature-biomass index, not age-specific population inputs. Annual N-at-age and
F-at-age outputs are not reported. This unit is ineligible under the current
advised method.

## Brill (`bll.27.3a47de`)

The current 2026 assessment is a SPiCT update using commercial landings and
three modelled exploitable-biomass indices. Discards are not included in the
assessment model. The report does not describe age-specific observations,
age-specific biology, or annual N/F-at-age surfaces. This unit is ineligible
under the current advised method. The separate Baltic brill unit
`bll.27.22-32` remains distinct and has not been reconciled here.

## Flounder (`fle.27.420`)

The 2026 benchmark changed the stock boundary and excluded Kattegat. The
accepted method is SPiCT tuned to DYFS, NS-IBTS Q1 and BTS Q3 survey indices.
The current report does not describe an age-structured population likelihood
or annual N/F-at-age outputs. This unit is screened separately from
`fle.27.21-23`, which covers subdivisions 21–23.

## Screening decision

These are current assessment units, but their 2026 advised methods do not
provide the age-disaggregated inputs needed for a defensible tinyAM population
model. They are screened ineligible for that set. No canonical numerical
records or tinyAM translations were added.
