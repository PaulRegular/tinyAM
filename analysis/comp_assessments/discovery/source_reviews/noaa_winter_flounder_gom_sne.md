# NOAA winter flounder: Gulf of Maine and SNE/MA screens

The NOAA Stock SMART register contains three separate winter flounder units. Georges Bank (ID 10029) is already matched to the canonical `ck_nefsc_americanus_5z` record. This review screens the other two current U.S. units: Gulf of Maine (ID 10028) and Southern New England/Mid-Atlantic (ID 10030). These are not duplicates of Georges Bank.

## Gulf of Maine — NOAA Stock SMART 10028

The current detailed assessment is the 2025 management-track update, with data through 2024. Stock SMART identifies it as an Operational assessment using the area-swept biomass method (assessment ID 14073). The report describes the 2025 assessment as an update to the 2022 area-swept assessment. It updates commercial and recreational catches and survey indices, and estimates biomass for fish 30 cm and larger from three fall surveys (NEFSC, MDMF, and MENH), using updated catchability `q = 0.79`.

The report provides aggregate catches and a 30+ cm biomass/exploitation-rate series. It estimates 2024 biomass at 4,537 metric tonnes and exploitation rate at 0.044. It does not use catch-at-age, age-composition observations, age-specific survey abundance, age-specific weights or maturity, or annual age-specific N/F/M outputs. Survey timing is identified as fall; the report does not specify a fractional-year model timing.

This unit is ineligible for the current age-structured assessment candidate set because its latest assessment is an empirical area-swept biomass model, not an age-structured model.

## Southern New England/Mid-Atlantic — NOAA Stock SMART 10030

The current detailed assessment is the 2025 management-track ASAP update, with data through 2024 (Stock SMART assessment ID 14072). The update was used in 2026 management decisions. The public report is labelled a draft working paper for peer review only; it nonetheless supplies the current assessment results presented to the ASMFC Management Board. The report states that the data structure, model settings, and assumptions did not change from the prior management-track assessment.

The report provides commercial and recreational catches and survey biomass-index series, but not current-run catch-at-age arrays, age-composition samples, numeric survey-age inputs, the numerical weight-at-age/maturity-at-age arrays used in projection, or a survey timing table. It gives 2024 aggregate outputs of SSB 2,787 mt, fully selected F 0.048, and recruitment 6,211 thousand fish; the plots show approximate 90% lognormal intervals. It does not provide annual N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age output tables.

The assessment is age-structured: the current 2025 report names ASAP and refers to age-specific selectivity, maturity, and weights-at-age. The May 2025 Assessment Oversight Panel report describes the prior 2022 ASAP model as ages 1–7+, with M = 0.3 and 12 fishery-independent indices; because the 2025 report states that model settings and assumptions were unchanged, this is supporting context, not a substitute for the missing 2025 model inputs. The exact current model arrays and timing remain unverified. Keep age-input eligibility unresolved until those source files are recovered.
