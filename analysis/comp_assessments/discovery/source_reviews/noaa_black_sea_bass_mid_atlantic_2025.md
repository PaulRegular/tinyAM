# NOAA Mid-Atlantic black sea bass (Stock SMART 10311)

Reviewed 2026-10-29. NOAA identifies one Mid-Atlantic management stock north of
Cape Hatteras, North Carolina. Its 2025 assessment models two internal regions
divided near Hudson Canyon; they are not separate inventory stock units. No
matching canonical stock record was found, so the NOAA Stock SMART identifier
is retained and a provisional candidate unit is assigned.

The latest assessment is the 2025 management-track update, with data through
2024. NOAA's June 2025 peer-review panel concluded that it met the terms of
reference and represented best scientific information for the stock. The
assessment updates a spatially explicit multi-WHAM model and includes
commercial and recreational catch, state and federal survey indices, and
age-specific weight and maturity information. The peer-review report discusses
random-effects numbers-at-age. A public assessment summary reports SSB,
fully-selected F, and age-1 recruitment through 2024.

The public 2025 assessment summary located is explicitly marked as a draft for
peer review. The final assessment report is referenced in NOAA's SASINF
repository, but the final run-matched report, input matrices, age-length-key
files, and numerical N-at-age/F-at-age output series were not recovered during
this screen. The exact age range, full catch and age-composition coverage,
natural-mortality specification, and fractional timing for the survey indices
therefore remain unresolved. Eligibility remains unresolved rather than being
inferred from the age-structured model label alone.
