# Herring in ICES divisions 7.e and 7.f

## Identity and latest assessment

ICES stock `her.27.7ef` is the current herring unit for the western English
Channel and Bristol Channel. It is distinct from the Celtic Sea herring unit
`her.27.irls` and the eastern English Channel herring unit `her.27.3a47d`.
No canonical database stock currently uses this stock code.

The latest detailed source is the 2026 Herring Assessment Working Group report,
section 7. The report says this is the first year ICES provided advice for the
unit and that there is currently no stock assessment. It applies a Category 5
landings-based advice method because only a landings time series is available.
The 2026 report therefore supersedes the 2025 assessment year recorded by the
initial 2026 SID inventory snapshot.

## Evidence and screening decision

The report states that catch numbers and catch weights are unavailable, and
that mean weight-at-age, maturity-at-age, and natural mortality-at-age are not
available. It describes the Channel Groundfish Survey and the PELTIC acoustic
survey as sources of exploratory information, but does not use them as
age-specific abundance inputs to the current advice. The report says no
assessment exists for the stock, status is unknown, no projections or reference
points are provided, and the Category 5 advised landings use the 2015–2024
average with a 20% precautionary buffer.

**Screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set.** The current
advice does not use age-specific catch or abundance data and reports no
age-specific population or mortality outputs. The surveys described in the
report do not change that classification because they are not inputs to a
current age-structured assessment. No canonical assessment data or tinyAM
translation were added.
