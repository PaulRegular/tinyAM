# ICES lesser-spotted dogfish, `syc.27.3a47d`

## Unit and latest assessment

The 2026 ICES stock list identifies `syc.27.3a47d` as *Scyliorhinus canicula* in Subarea 4 and divisions 3.a and 7.d (North Sea, Skagerrak and Kattegat, and eastern English Channel). The latest detailed public assessment located is the 2025 WGEF assessment, which informed catch advice for 2026 and 2027. This is distinct from the Celtic Seas, Bay of Biscay, and Atlantic Iberian lesser-spotted dogfish units also discussed in that report.

## Age-disaggregated input and output evidence

WGEF 2025 section 25.6.1 (printed pages 697–698) describes the stock indicator as a combined total-biomass index standardized with a Tweedie GAM from five trawl surveys: NS-IBTS-Q1, NS-IBTS-Q3, FR-CGFS-Q4, BTS-BEL-Q3, and BTS-Eng-Q3. It is an aggregate biomass series, not an age-specific index or survey age composition.

Section 25.9.2 (printed pages 705–709) identifies the stock assessment as an ICES Category 3 rule for stocks without a full analytical population assessment. It reports landings and length-based catch indicators, a 2004–2024 relative biomass index, and a 2026–2027 catch recommendation of 2,765 tonnes. It does not report a catch-at-age likelihood, age composition, numbers-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, SSB, or recruitment. The life-history parameters cited from vertebral samples collected in the Irish Sea do not constitute age-structured inputs or outputs for this assessment.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new ICES stock unit, but it is ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set. Its latest assessment uses aggregate survey biomass and length-based indicators, with no age-structured population model or age-specific outputs. The report's life-history and survey information do not support reconstructing the missing age-disaggregated assessment observations without inventing model inputs.

No canonical numerical records or tinyAM translation were added. The cached report and provenance are recorded in `source_cache/discovery_ices_dogfish_2025/sources.csv`.
