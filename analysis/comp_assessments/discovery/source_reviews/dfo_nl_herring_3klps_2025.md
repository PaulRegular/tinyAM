# Newfoundland and Labrador Atlantic herring inventory screen

Reviewed 2026-10-29 against the latest official update, the detailed research
document for the latest full assessment, and the cited assessment framework.

## Identity and assessment boundary

The DFO sustainability-survey label is “Atlantic Herring - NAFO 2J3KLPs.”
This is a management/fishery area, not one assessed population unit. The latest
full assessment covers NAFO 3KLPs and treats five biological complexes
separately: White Bay–Notre Dame Bay (WBNDB), Bonavista Bay–Trinity Bay (BBTB),
Conception Bay–Southern Shore (CBSS), St. Mary’s Bay–Placentia Bay (SMBPB), and
Fortune Bay (FB). Each complex can contain spring- and fall-spawning
components. DFO states that the origin of herring along Labrador and parts of
the south coast is unknown; these fish are not assigned to adjacent assessed
complexes. The 2J3KLPs inventory row cannot be matched one-to-one to a single
canonical stock. No canonical stock is created or matched.

## Current evidence

The latest full assessment located was reviewed in October 2022 using data to
2021 and is documented in Research Document 2025/079, published in December
2025. Science Response 2025/023 updates indicators to 2024 and identifies the
approach as index-based, using fishery-dependent and fishery-independent
indices.

Research Document 2025/079 describes annual commercial catch numbers-at-age
vectors by complex and spawning type. It derives them from fishery landings,
mean whole weights and age proportions in processor samples; the report shows
sample-allocation tables and catch-age figures, not a complete numerical
annual age-vector series. These catch-age summaries are fishery indicators,
not inputs to a fitted age-structured population likelihood. Research gillnet
samples also provide age compositions and age-4 catch-rate recruitment
indices. The standardized spring gillnet program produces catch-rate indices
for mainly age-4-and-older fish, not age-specific abundance indices. Acoustic
surveys produce aggregate biomass indices and survey-level mean weights, not
age-specific abundance or weight-at-age surfaces.

The documents discuss length-at-age, maturity and age composition but do not
specify population-model maturity-at-age inputs. The latest official approach
does not estimate a population age structure, fishing-mortality surface, or
natural-mortality process. Reported outputs are stock-status and recruitment
indices, catch compositions, and episodic aggregate acoustic biomass indices.

The 2015 framework report explains the historical spring research-gillnet
status-index approach. It does not describe a current population-model
likelihood. The 2025 detailed report and update provide the latest evidence;
no numerical source records are imported from the older framework.

## Screening decision

**Identity:** ambiguous at the inventory-row level. The row describes the
2J3KLPs management area, while the latest assessment covers five separate
3KLPs biological complexes; 2J herring is not included in those five complexes.

**Age-structured eligibility:** ineligible for the current age-structured
population-assessment candidate set. Age data and derived catch numbers-at-age
exist, but the official method is index-based and does not estimate comparable
age-specific population or mortality outputs. This is not a claim that the
underlying fish lack age data.

No canonical stock, assessment event, input, or output record was added. The
three authoritative PDFs are cached locally under the gitignored
`source_cache/discovery_dfo_nl_herring_2025/` folder; `sources.csv` records
retrieval date, source URLs and checksums.
