# DFO Units 1 and 2 redfish (2024 assessment)

## Unit and latest official assessment

DFO treats *Sebastes mentella* and *S. fasciatus* as two distinct stocks distributed across management Units 1 and 2. The unit boundaries differ seasonally: Unit 1 covers NAFO Divisions 4RST and Subdivisions 3Pn4Vn from January to May; Unit 2 covers Subdivisions 3Ps4Vs, unit areas 4Wfgj and Subdivisions 3Pn4Vn from June to December. No matching canonical stock record for these DFO units was found.

The latest detailed assessment source located is the 2026 Research Document 2026/037, which reports the 2024 stock status. The related 2025 Science Advisory Report says the current full approach was last approved in 2022 and identifies the February–March 2025 meeting as the 2024 assessment review; a Science Response published in 2026 updates indicators for 2025. Thus the later update does not replace the detailed 2024 record. The 2025 update could not report Unit 2 status because no Unit 2 survey was conducted in 2025.

## Inputs, assumptions and outputs

The official assessment is explicitly index-based. It uses commercial landings by management unit, not by species, and empirical fishery-dependent and fishery-independent indices. Unit 1 survey coverage is 1984–2024; Unit 2 surveys are intermittent (2000, 2001, 2003, 2005, 2007, 2009, 2011, 2014, 2016, 2018 and 2024). Catch rates were standardized to the CCGS *John Cabot* modified Campelen trawl. Species attribution uses anal-fin-ray counts and is uncertain, particularly for the less abundant *S. fasciatus*.

The assessment reports survey biomass by size class, length-frequency information, SSB in kilotonnes with 95% intervals, and recruitment as biomass of fish smaller than 11 cm. It reports that species-level landings and species-level fishing mortality are unavailable. Potential-removal calculations consider alternative natural-mortality assumptions, but the assessment is not an age-structured population model and does not estimate an age-specific M process.

No run-specific catch-at-age, age-specific survey likelihood, weights-at-age, maturity-at-age, age-specific mortality, or fitted N-at-age/F-at-age population surfaces were verified. Biological samples and length-at-age growth analyses do not make those quantities inputs or outputs of the accepted index-based assessment. The current sources therefore support an index/size-based status record, but not eligibility for the age-disaggregated assessment set.

The two inventory rows are distinct species stocks, provisionally new relative to the canonical database. They share the same assessment and source history; they are not counted as separate assessment versions or management-area components. No numerical assessment values were imported.
