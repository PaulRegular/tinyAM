# ICES southern hake (`hke.27.8c9a`)

Reviewed 2026-10-30. The latest official unit is southern hake in ICES divisions 8.c and 9.a. The current 2026 Category 1 assessment (Standard Graphs key 23751) supports advice for 2027.

The 2026 WGBIE report lists southern hake among the length-based production assessments. Its inputs are catches and length compositions, with survey and CPUE abundance indices represented at aggregate scale; the current sources do not report catch-at-age or age-specific survey likelihood series. The fact that the graph metadata labels the output as “Age and Length” does not establish age-disaggregated observations.

Standard Graphs reports age-0 recruitment in thousands, SSB in tonnes, and fishing pressure averaged over ages 1–7. It defines 90% uncertainty intervals for recruitment, SSB, and F. It does not provide historical N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age surfaces. No run-matched weight-at-age or maturity-at-age observation series was recovered for this length-based production model. These outputs can support an aggregate comparison, but the assessment is ineligible for the age-disaggregated input set.

The report states that a review-level benchmark of model adjustments was expected in 2026 for application in the following year's assessment. That does not replace the 2026 production run used for current advice; the reviewed graph key and advice record both identify assessment year 2026.
