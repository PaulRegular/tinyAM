# ICES white anglerfish (mon.27.8c9a), 2026

## Stock and assessment identity

The 2026 ICES advice record 7177 identifies white anglerfish in divisions 8.c
and 9.a. It is a Category 1 assessment linked to Stock Assessment Graphs key
22608.

## Assessment evidence

WGBIE 2026 section 4.2 describes a sex-separated Stock Synthesis assessment
updated with data through 2025. The model uses quarterly landings from four
fleets, two aggregate commercial CPUE series, one aggregate fourth-quarter
Spanish SpGFS-WIBTS-Q4 survey index and length compositions. The report
explicitly states that no age information is available for this stock.

The reported biological assumptions include fixed sex- and age-specific M, a
length-based female maturity curve and sex-specific weight-at-length and growth
parameters. These do not provide observed age-specific inputs. The public graph
reports age-0 recruitment (thousands), SSB (the report defines this as
female-only, tonnes) and F ages 2–15. It does not provide N-at-age or F-at-age
annual surfaces.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated candidate set because the
production assessment explicitly has no age observations and uses aggregate
and length-based observations. Its available outputs are aggregate or age-group
summaries. No canonical assessment, input or output rows were added.
