# Gulf lane snapper, 2026 SEDAR 49 interim analysis

## Unit and current analysis

NOAA Stock SMART identifies stock 10239 as Gulf of Mexico lane snapper. No
matching canonical tinyAM stock record was found. Stock SMART's latest listed
assessment year is 2023, but the latest official analysis located is the 2026
SEDAR 49 interim analysis, which informed catch advice for 2026-2028. The
Gulf Council's Standing Scientific and Statistical Committee considered that
analysis consistent with best available scientific information.

The current method is an ITarget data-limited interim analysis based on the
G-FISHER composite video index of relative abundance. It uses natural-reef
sites. The earlier SRHS headboat CPUE index was dropped from the current
analysis because of representativeness concerns. This is not a current
age-structured population assessment. The SSC report explicitly says ages and
lengths do not directly inform interim analyses.

## Inputs and outputs

The sources reviewed do not document catch numbers at age, age-specific survey
information, weights at age, maturity at age, or an age-specific natural
mortality vector as inputs to the 2026 analysis. The latest analysis uses an
aggregate relative-abundance index to update catch advice; the reviewed source
does not provide age-specific population states or outputs. The SSC's 2026
recommendation is an overfishing limit of 1,336,445 lb whole weight and an
acceptable biological catch of 1,304,917 lb whole weight for 2026-2028.

The 2016 SEDAR 49 data-limited assessment and associated life-history materials
are historical context. They are not substituted for current-run inputs or
outputs. The SSC's 2026 report links back to SEDAR 49 but describes the current
interim method and finding directly.

## Screening decision

This current unit is ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set:
the current official method is index-based, ages and lengths do not directly
inform the interim analysis, and age-specific population inputs and outputs
were not found. This decision concerns age-structured tinyAM source
eligibility; the 2026 analysis remains an official source for aggregate catch
advice. No numerical records were imported and no tinyAM translation was
attempted.
