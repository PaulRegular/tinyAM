# White hake - NAFO 3NOPs

Reviewed 2026-10-29. DFO's survey entry `White Hake - NAFO 3NOPs` is a current transboundary biological stock. NAFO's 3NO advice concerns the portion of this stock distributed in 3NO and 3Ps; Canada also manages its 3Ps portion separately. This is distinct from the existing Northwest Atlantic white-hake record for the Gulf of Maine and Georges Bank.

## Current advice and latest detailed assessment

The latest NAFO report is the June 2026 Scientific Council report. It states that the Council could not complete a full assessment or interim monitoring report for 2027 and beyond because the designated-expert position was vacant. The latest detailed full assessment document located is NAFO SCR Doc. 23/036, for the 2023 advice cycle and data through 2022. Its cover says not to cite it without prior reference to the authors, so its detail is treated as screening evidence rather than a freely citable final assessment report. Its assessment is a qualitative review of catch and survey trends, not an age-structured population model.

## Age-disaggregated evidence

The 2023 report uses annual landings, fishery length frequencies, aggregate survey biomass and abundance indices, a recruitment index based on the number of fish under 27 cm, and relative F calculated as commercial landings divided by Canadian spring survey biomass. It reports no catch-at-age, age-specific survey likelihood, fitted N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age. The report recommends age determination of collected otoliths to enable future age-based analyses; this is a research recommendation, not evidence of age-based inputs to the current production assessment. The assessment does not require weight-at-age or maturity-at-age.

The NAFO 2024 biological-sampling inventory lists Canadian commercial sampling for 3NOPs white hake as catch only. Although other fleets report biological sampling, the 2023 assessment documents length compositions and does not use age compositions in its assessment. The latest 2026 advice remains unavailable because no monitoring report was completed. The unit is therefore ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set. No canonical assessment or numeric series was imported.
