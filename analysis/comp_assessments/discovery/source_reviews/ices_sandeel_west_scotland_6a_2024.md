# Sandeel in ICES Division 6.a: 2024 advice screen

**Review date:** 2026-10-31  
**Official stock code:** `san.27.6a`  
**Scientific name:** *Ammodytes* spp.  
**Assessment area:** Division 6.a (West of Scotland)

## Stock identity and current advice

The ICES Advice and Scenarios Database identifies `san.27.6a` as sandeel in
Division 6.a, assessment year 2024, category 6. Its advice is zero catch for
2024–2026. The 2025 Herring Assessment Working Group report says the same advice
was given under the category-6 method. This is one current unit and is distinct
from the North Sea `san.sa.*` units already in the database. The TAF search
result `2019_san.sa.6` is a different stock code and was not treated as a match.

## Inputs and outputs

The current advice record and HAWG report do not describe an age-structured
production model for this unit. No catch-at-age, age-composition, age-specific
survey index, weights-at-age, maturity-at-age, or quantitative age-specific
mortality inputs were identified. The public advice is a zero-catch
recommendation; the reviewed current sources do not provide fitted SSB,
recruitment, N-at-age, or F-at-age trajectories. The HAWG GitHub repository was
also checked. Its published stock folders do not contain a Division 6.a
sandeel production assessment; the `VIa` folder is the Malin herring work.

## Screening decision

This is a distinct new ICES unit, screened **ineligible** for the current
age-disaggregated source-curation set. The basis is the current category-6
advice and the absence of age-structured inputs and fitted population outputs
in the reviewed assessment sources. No canonical stock, assessment, input,
output, or tinyAM translation records were added.
