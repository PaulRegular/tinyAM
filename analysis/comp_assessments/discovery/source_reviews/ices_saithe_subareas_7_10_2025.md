# ICES saithe in subareas 7–10 (2025)

**Review date:** 2026-10-31  
**Official stock code:** `pok.27.7-10`  
**Scientific name:** *Pollachius virens*  
**Assessment year:** 2025  
**Stock category:** 5

## Stock identity and current assessment

The ICES 2026 Stock Information Database (SID) identifies `pok.27.7-10` as the
current advice unit and records 2025 as its most recent assessment year. The
2025 Advice and Scenarios Database record and Stock Assessment Graphs identify
assessment key 19486 and category 5. The unit covers subareas 7–10: the southern
Celtic Sea and English Channel, Bay of Biscay, Atlantic Iberian waters, and
Azores grounds. No matching stock or assessment is present in the canonical
database.

## Current-run inputs and outputs

The official SAG XML and source-data page contain 22 annual fish-data rows for
2003–2024. Only aggregate landings and discards are populated. The export has no
age-data table, catch-at-age, age-composition input, age-specific survey index,
or populated recruitment, stock-size, or fishing-pressure series. The XML also
has no recruitment-age, F-age, or F-length definition. Its generic stock-size
label says SSB, but the annual stock-size values are empty.

The 2025 advice record recommends catches no greater than 146 tonnes in each of
2026–2028. This is an advice limit, not a fitted age-structured population
output. The reviewed current-run products do not provide weights-at-age,
maturity-at-age, natural-mortality-at-age, survey timing, N-at-age, F-at-age,
or M-at-age. These findings describe the public products checked for this
assessment; they do not establish that age or biological samples are absent
from national data systems.

## Screening decision

This is a **new, screened, ineligible candidate** for the age-disaggregated
assessment set. The latest current-unit assessment is category 5, and its
published products provide aggregate catches only, without age-disaggregated
observations or age-specific population and mortality outputs. It remains in
the discovery register; no older or neighbouring saithe unit is substituted.
No canonical data were added, and no tinyAM translation was attempted.
