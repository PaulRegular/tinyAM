# Main Hawaiian Islands crimson jobfish / opakapaka (`NOAA:12442`), 2024 review

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-02
**Inventory record:** NOAA Stock SMART 12442
**Assessment unit:** Main Hawaiian Islands opakapaka (*Pristipomoides filamentosus*)
**Screen:** Screened ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set.

## Assessment identity and latest run

Stock SMART identifies assessment 8020 (2018; data through 2016) as a fully accepted Bayesian surplus-production assessment. NOAA's 2018 benchmark and 2021 update also describe a single-species opakapaka production model. The latest assessment relevant to this species is the 2024 fully accepted Main Hawaiian Islands Deep 7 benchmark. It includes a supplementary opakapaka Stock Synthesis model, but that model is a species-level comparison within the Deep 7 assessment rather than a separately accepted age-based assessment for the NOAA:12442 candidate.

## Age-disaggregated input and output screen

- **Catch:** the 2018 assessment is aggregate surplus production, and the 2024 supplemental Stock Synthesis model uses total catch rather than catch-at-age.
- **Abundance:** the 2024 model uses aggregate CPUE and BFISH relative-biomass indices. No age-specific abundance index or observed age-composition likelihood is documented.
- **Compositions and biology:** the supplemental model uses length compositions and commercial weight compositions. Life-history inputs, including natural mortality, growth and reproduction, are fixed. Age-related biology does not substitute for age-disaggregated observations.
- **Timing:** the 2024 complex assessment uses July–June fishing years; BFISH observations are aggregate and are not age-specific.
- **Official outputs:** the accepted complex model and supplemental opakapaka model report aggregate biomass, harvest rate and status. The reviewed report does not provide a stock-specific N-at-age or F-at-age trajectory for this candidate.

The candidate is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated inventory because the available current assessment does not document age-specific observed inputs. No canonical stock, assessment, input or output records were added.
