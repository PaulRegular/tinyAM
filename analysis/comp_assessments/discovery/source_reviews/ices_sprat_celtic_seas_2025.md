# ICES Celtic Seas and West of Scotland sprat (spr.27.67a-cf-k), 2025

## Stock and assessment identity

This is the ICES unit for Sprat (*Sprattus sprattus*) in Subarea 6 and
divisions 7.a–c and 7.f–k. It is separate from Baltic sprat and English Channel
sprat. The latest advice located is the 2025 Herring Assessment Working Group
(HAWG) advice, record 3838, with Stock Assessment Graphs key 19352. The advice
applies to calendar years 2026 and 2027. The 2026 HAWG report states that
biennial advice for this unit was provided in 2025.

## Assessment evidence

The latest assessment is ICES Category 5.2. HAWG describes the method as using
only catch/landings data. The published ICES graph source for assessment key
19352 contains annual catches in tonnes through 2024, with no age-specific
input series. The 2025 advice recommends catches no greater than 1,792 tonnes
in each of 2026 and 2027.

The current assessment method does not use survey abundance, catch-at-age,
age-specific biology or age-specific mortality. The published graph provides
catch history and the advice provides a catch recommendation; neither source
reports SSB, recruitment, N-at-age, F-at-age or M-at-age population outputs.

## Screening decision

This unit is **ineligible** for the age-disaggregated assessment set. It has a
catch-only advice method and no age-specific population inputs or outputs. No
earlier assessment was substituted and no canonical stock, assessment, input
or output rows were added.
