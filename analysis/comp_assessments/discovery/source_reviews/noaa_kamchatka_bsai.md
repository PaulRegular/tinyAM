# Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands Kamchatka flounder

## Identity and assessment

NOAA Stock SMART ID 10002 is *Reinhardtius evermanni* in the Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands. It is a different species from the adjacent BSAI arrowtooth flounder unit. The latest full assessment located is the 2024 Model 16.0b SAFE chapter. The final October 2026 SSC report identifies the 2026 update as a harvest projection based on the 2024 assessment, not a new full fit.

## Age-input screen

The [2024 SAFE chapter](https://files.npfmc.org/SAFE/2024/BSAIkamchatka.pdf) documents fishery catch biomass and fishery length compositions, but no fishery catch-age series or fishery age composition. Survey age compositions occur only in selected EBS slope and Aleutian Islands years; they do not create the missing catch-age input. The report's age-structured model and fitted age outputs are not substitutes for observed catch-at-age. This candidate is therefore ineligible for the current age-input screen.

The report fixes natural mortality at 0.11 per year and describes growth, length-weight and maturity biology. Age-specific biological vectors and timing would still need to be extracted or stated as approximations if the source search is reopened. The assessment output is available, but inputs do not meet the catch-age requirement.

The 2024 SAFE, survey introduction and 2024 SSC report are cached with retrieval details and checksums in `source_cache/noaa_flatfish_screening_20261029/sources.csv`.
