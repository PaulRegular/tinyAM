# ICES pelagic beaked-redfish screening

Review date: 2026-10-31

## Unit identity and coverage

The 2026 ICES SID lists two separate Irminger Sea beaked-redfish units with
latest assessments from 2024: deep pelagic (reb.2127.dp, >500 m) and shallow
pelagic (reb.2127.sp, <500 m). These are separate official assessment units.
They are distinct from the Northeast Arctic beaked-redfish stock already
represented in the database. No numerical data were imported.

## Screening

| Unit | Current method and evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| reb.2127.dp — deep pelagic | The 2024 MFRI assessment describes an age-length Gadget model using commercial catches, catch age and length data, acoustic/trawl survey index and length data, with natural mortality fixed at 0.05. Survey coverage is June–August, with the 2024 survey in June–July. It reports recruitment, SSB and F relative to the time-series mean. The public assessment summary does not specify stock weight and maturity inputs, and the underlying catch-at-age files have not yet been recovered. Age observations have not been available since 2013; relative recruitment from 2009 onward is assumed from the earlier geometric mean. | Eligibility unresolved pending verification/recovery of missing biological and native input details. |
| reb.2127.sp — shallow pelagic | The 2024 assessment is a trend-based method using aggregate acoustic biomass indicators and catch information. The source reports no age-disaggregated inputs or fitted age-specific population/mortality outputs. | Ineligible for the age-structured candidate set. |

The two decisions distinguish assessment-unit identity and current method. They do
not imply that age samples are unavailable for unrelated monitoring or research.
