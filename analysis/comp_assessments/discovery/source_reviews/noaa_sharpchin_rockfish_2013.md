# U.S. Pacific Coast Sharpchin Rockfish (NOAA Stock SMART 10435)

## Stock unit and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART identifies one Pacific Coast unit of Sebastes zacentrus. No existing canonical tinyAM stock record matches this U.S. unit. Its latest detailed assessment is the 2013 data-moderate exSSS assessment, using data through 2012 and published in 2015. Stock SMART lists the run as fully accepted. The 2025 Pacific Coast Groundfish SAFE confirms that the 2013 assessment was used to set harvest specifications; the 2026 Amendment 37 analysis also identifies the coastwide data-moderate assessment as the most recent. The assessment report assumes a single coastwide stock for its assessment model; its north/south allocation to management complexes is not a second assessment unit.

## Age-specific inputs and outputs

The 2013 report's Appendix A.1 includes the model data file. It specifies a 58-age population but sets age-composition observations to zero and mean-size-at-age observations to zero. The run uses commercial catch totals in biomass units, one fishery index, and three aggregate trawl-survey indices, also in biomass units. Its internally age-structured population therefore does not provide observed catch-at-age, age compositions, or age-specific survey abundance observations.

The assessment includes sex-specific growth, weight-length, maturity, and natural-mortality model settings, plus fitted stock-recruitment and catchability parameters. These do not constitute observed age-specific input series. The model file sets all fishery and survey timing to 0.5 within the season; that is a model timing setting rather than verified survey-date timing.

The assessment reports aggregate outputs including unfished and 2013 spawning biomass, depletion, F/FMSY, MSY and OFL values. It was accepted for harvest specification and status use. The reviewed report does not provide annual numeric N-at-age, F-at-age, or M-at-age surfaces; its data file also disables detailed age-structured report output.

## Screening decision

Treat NOAA Stock SMART 10435 as one new coastwide U.S. Pacific candidate and mark it ineligible for the age-disaggregated assessment set. The accepted fit has internal age structure and aggregate status and harvest outputs, but its run file includes no age-composition or mean-size-at-age observations and no age-specific abundance index. Do not infer age observations from the model's age structure or create canonical age-series records.
