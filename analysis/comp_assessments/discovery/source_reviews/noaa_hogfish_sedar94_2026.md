# NOAA Florida hogfish: SEDAR 94 (2026)

## Stock units and current assessment

SEDAR 94 is the latest benchmark assessment located for Florida hogfish, with data through 2024 and a completed assessment report published in 2026. It covers two separate units: West Florida Shelf (WFS, the Gulf unit) and East Florida/Florida Keys (EFL/KEYS). NOAA Stock SMART candidates 12808 (“Eastern Gulf of Mexico”) and 12809 (“Florida Keys / East Florida”) map to those units, respectively. Candidate 12810 (“Carolinas”) is a separate Georgia–North Carolina (GA-NC) management unit and is not part of SEDAR 94.

The assessment uses Stock Synthesis 3.30.24 as a single-area, annual, age-structured integrated analysis for each unit, spanning 1950–2024. The Keys/EFL assessment has spawning on January 1. The model is sex structured and includes protogynous sex change, so its biological and biomass definitions are not equivalent to a simple one-sex age model.

## Inputs and age information

The assessment uses commercial and recreational landings (and recreational discards), survey abundance indices, size compositions, conditional age-at-length (CAAL) data, and biological information. The review presentation describes aggregate video and Reef Visual Census indices; these survey inputs include length/size information, not an age-specific abundance index. The CAAL likelihood includes commercial, recreational, and fishery-independent research sources. These age samples inform the integrated length-based model but are not themselves survey abundance indices.

The supporting SEDAR 94 DW-11 paper summarizes 847 fishery-dependent aged fish, including 657 from WFS and 190 from EFL/KEYS, with sample summaries by year and source. The review presentation also summarizes 1,576 otoliths overall, age range 0–16, and distinguishes commercial, recreational, and fishery-independent sources. The reviewed publications do not provide the complete run-matched numeric CAAL arrays needed to reconstruct the assessment likelihood or a validated catch-at-age series.

The assessment uses external growth and length–weight relationships, a fixed age-specific Lorenzen M vector, and a female maturity relationship. The presentation reports female age-at-50%-maturity of 2.08 years. Exact run-specific numeric biological vectors and survey observation timing have not been extracted here.

## Outputs and screening decision

The final assessment report and review presentation report population estimates and stock-recruitment, spawning-biomass, and fishing-mortality results. Numeric run-matched N-at-age and F-at-age files or machine-readable tables have not been located in the materials reviewed. The SEDAR project page lists the final report, working papers, and review presentations; no public native model archive was identified in that listing. This is an unresolved source search, not evidence that such files do not exist elsewhere.

Candidates 12808 (WFS) and 12809 (EFL/KEYS) are marked **new / screened / eligibility unresolved**. The assessment is age structured and has CAAL observations, but a fully verified age-disaggregated input/output dataset cannot yet be built from the reviewed public files. The CAAL and biological observations should not be mislabeled as catch-at-age or age-specific survey abundance.

Candidate 12810 is marked **new / screened / ineligible** for this age-disaggregated audit. The current GA-NC unit is distinct from the SEDAR 94 units. The South Atlantic Council SAFE report says an assessment attempt lacked sufficient information to run the models and describes landings-based advice; no age-structured production inputs or population outputs were located.

No canonical stock, assessment, input, assumption, or output rows were added by this screen.
