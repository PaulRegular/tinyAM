# DFO 2J+3KL Capelin

## Current unit and assessment

DFO treats capelin in NAFO Subarea 2 and Divisions 3K and 3L as one stock
complex. Division 3Ps is a separate stock area, although DFO notes that advice
for 2J+3KL is also applied to 3Ps. Do not combine 3Ps into this assessment unit.

The latest advice is the 2026 assessment to 2025. Its report describes the
approach as index-based, using a forecast model to predict the spring acoustic
biomass index. It reports 2025 acoustic biomass of 766 kt (90% CI 560–1,070 kt),
an age-disaggregated biomass figure, a larval recruitment indicator, and a
maximum-F index. It states that additional publications from the 2026 review
will follow. A local download attempt for this PDF returned the publications
portal's archived-notice page rather than the document.

The most recent detailed research document located is DFO Research Document
2025/061, which reports data through 2024. It is cached in
`source_cache/discovery_dfo_capelin_2j3kl/` with its source URL and checksum.
Keep its detailed 2024 material separate from the newer 2025 observations in
the 2026 advice; do not treat the 2024 report as a detailed report for the
2025 assessment.

## Age-specific information and limits

- Commercial fishery biological samples are processed with year-, division-,
  and sex-specific age-length keys. Catch-at-age is calculated by gear-sector
  and division catch cell and expanded using landings. These data describe the
  spawning population's age and size structure; they are not an age-based
  likelihood in the assessment's population model.
- The spring acoustic survey produces aggregate biomass and abundance indices.
  Research Document 2025/061 plots age-disaggregated abundance and age
  composition from 1985–2024. The 2026 advice plots age-disaggregated biomass
  through 2025. The report's 2024 aggregate abundance estimate is 87.0 billion
  fish, and aggregate biomass is 647 kt.
- The detailed report plots acoustic mean weights at ages 1–6 for 1985–2024.
  It also reports the proportion of female age-2 fish maturing; the 2026 report
  gives 34% for 2025. Neither source supplies a complete annual maturity
  matrix, and numeric weight-at-age values are not tabulated.
- The assessment reports no age-specific M assumption or fitted M-at-age
  surface. Its maximum-F index combines the mature fraction of acoustic biomass
  and fishery landings; it is not F-at-age from a population model.
- Neither report provides fitted population N-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, or
  SSB trajectories. The acoustic age series are survey indices, not fitted
  population states.

This is a distinct DFO unit, but it is ineligible for the age-structured
tinyAM comparison set: the current official assessment is index-based and does
not provide comparable age-structured population outputs or age-specific
mortality assumptions. This screening decision does not imply that the
underlying acoustic or commercial biological data are unavailable for other
uses.
