# NOAA South Atlantic red grouper

Review date: 2026-10-31

## Unit and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART 10332 is the U.S. South Atlantic red grouper unit
(*Epinephelus morio*), separate from Gulf of Mexico Stock SMART 10331. NOAA's
current species page identifies the South Atlantic stock's 2017 assessment as
the most recent assessment used for stock status. SEDAR 86 was planned as an
operational update, but it was not completed; the project was returned to the
benchmark schedule. The latest completed detailed production assessment found
for this unit is therefore SEDAR 53, published in 2017, with data through 2015.

## Source screen

SEDAR 53 used the Beaufort Assessment Model in a statistical catch-age
formulation. The model covers ages 1–16+, with age 16 as the accumulator group.
It fits annual landings and discards by four fleets, age compositions for
commercial lines, headboat, general recreational, and the SERFS survey, three
aggregate abundance indices, and length compositions. The final report and
supporting data papers document age compositions and annual catches. The
commercial age-composition paper provides numeric weighted handline/longline
age proportions for 1996–2015. These source quantities support a documented
age-specific reconstruction for fleet-years with matched catch totals and
composition data; they do not establish complete catch-at-age coverage for
every fleet and year. Missing combinations must remain missing.

The report summarizes whole-weight inputs and growth, a female maturity ogive
(50% mature at age 2.8; age-1 females immature), all males mature, and a fixed
sex-ratio ogive. The assessment's age-varying natural mortality is fixed over
time using a Lorenzen relationship rescaled to match cumulative survival under
constant M = 0.14 through age 26. Spawning biomass is total mature biomass of
both sexes at peak spawning in mid-April.

SEDAR 53 Table 6 reports start-of-year abundance at age in thousands of fish
for ages 1–16+, including assessment years 1976–2015 and a 2016 forecast.
Table 8 reports annual apical F, total biomass and peak-spawning SSB through
2015. These are age-specific population and comparable aggregate outputs. The
reviewed report does not tabulate uncertainty by age. It does report uncertainty
analyses for assessment quantities.

The assessment inputs are annual indices derived from sources with different
sampling seasons. The report does not give one common within-year survey
fraction. Any later tinyAM translation must preserve source timing where
available or label the chosen single-fraction timing as an approximation.

## Decision

This is a **new, eligible source-curation candidate**, not a canonical database
record yet. Eligibility reflects the recoverable assessment inputs and outputs
and documented age-specific reconstructions; it does not mean every fleet-year
has complete age observations or that a tinyAM translation will be exact. No
assessment event, input series, output series, or fit was added in this screen.
