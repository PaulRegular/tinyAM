# DFO Quebec North Shore Atlantic herring (NAFO 4S)

Reviewed 2026-10-29 against the latest status update, the most recent full
assessment report and the supporting survey-method documents. The official PDFs
are cached under `source_cache/discovery_dfo_quebec_herring_4s_2025/`; `sources.csv`
records each source URL, retrieval date, file size and SHA-256 checksum.

## Identity and current assessment

The DFO Sustainability Survey entry “Atlantic Herring - Herring Fishing Area
15” refers to Quebec North Shore herring in NAFO Division 4S. DFO describes two
genetically distinct spawning stocks, spring and fall, and says they are assessed
separately. The single inventory row therefore does not identify one assessment
unit and is cross-referenced as ambiguous. Division 4S is not the same unit as
the canonical Southern Gulf of St. Lawrence Division 4TVn spring- and
fall-spawning stocks.

The latest source reviewed is Science Response 2025/015, an update through 2024.
It says a requested full assessment was cancelled while the framework was being
revised. The update classifies the approach as index-based, using fishery and
survey indicators. The last full assessment report is the 2020 assessment
(Science Advisory Report 2021/037); it also evaluates empirical stock
indicators rather than a fitted population model.

## Age data, biology and outputs

The 2025 update describes commercial catch-age composition for 1985-2024 and a
recruitment index based on the proportion of ages 3-5 in commercial catches,
lagged three years, through 2021. It lists total biomass indices and age-specific
abundances from the summer acoustic survey in unit area 4Sw for 2019-2024 and
the fall acoustic survey for 2009-2022. These reports show cohort composition
and aggregate indices, but the reviewed sources do not provide complete
machine-readable age-specific catch and survey arrays for a run-matched model.

The 2020 assessment report includes historical weight-at-age and length-at-age
indicators. A complete current run-matched weight-at-age surface and an
assessment-specific maturity-at-age surface were not recovered. The latest
update explicitly marks mortality data unavailable; it does not estimate F-at-age
or M-at-age. Reported outputs are commercial landings, aggregate acoustic biomass
indices by spawning group, age-composition indicators and the recruitment index.
They are not fitted N-at-age, F-at-age or model-derived SSB trajectories.

The 2024 fall and summer acoustic survey research documents revise and describe
survey methods for the adjacent 4RSw area, including 4Sw. They do not turn the
4S update into an age-structured population assessment or supply missing
population-state outputs. The latest source also notes extensive movement and
mixing between 4Sw and 4R and suggests a future unit-boundary review; this is
not a formally adopted merger and does not justify merging the 4S record with
the 4R or 4TVn units.

## Screening decision

The row remains identity-ambiguous because its management-area label covers two
separately assessed spawning stocks. It is ineligible for the current
age-structured model candidate set: the latest assessment is index-based, key
age-specific inputs are not available as complete numerical arrays, mortality
data are unavailable, and the official outputs are indices rather than fitted
age-specific population states. Preserve the reported indicators if a later
source-curation goal targets data-limited index assessments; do not manufacture
M, F or abundance surfaces for this screen.

No canonical stock or assessment record is created by this discovery review.
