# Baltic plaice: historical Subdivisions 24–32 catalogue entry

## Assessment relationship

The Charbonneau–Keith row describes the former plaice unit in subdivisions
24–32. Following the WKBPLAICE benchmark, ICES now assesses one combined
21–32 management unit as `ple.27.21-32`. Its 2026 production assessment is
represented by `ices_plaice_baltic_21_32_2026`; the current model and report do
not provide a separate fitted assessment for subdivisions 24–32.

## Database decision

Keep the historical component metadata-only. The combined 21–32 input and
output series cannot be copied into this row as though they described only
24–32. Any tinyAM translation or comparison belongs to the combined stock
record.
