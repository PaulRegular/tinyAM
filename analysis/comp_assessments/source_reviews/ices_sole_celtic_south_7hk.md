# Southern Celtic Sea and southwest Ireland sole

## Stock identity and source selection

The current ICES advice unit `sol.27.7h-k` covers divisions 7.h–k. The
Charbonneau–Keith catalogue entry `ICES-WGSCE_solea_IS7jk` names a narrower
area. Their exact historical relationship remains unresolved, so the
catalogue entry and the current broader unit are kept separate; data for the
broader unit are not relabelled as 7.j–k-only values.

The 2026 WGCSE report and ICES advice describe a category-5 stock with no
population assessment. The advice for each of 2027–2029 is 136 tonnes,
calculated by applying a 0.8 precautionary buffer to the preceding 170-tonne
advice. Advice record 6146 is marked Valid. The report repeats an incorrect
2026–2028 range in one heading; its rule table and the published advice both
state 2027–2029.

## Data and assumptions

The 2026 SAG export retains 75 annual landings values for 1951–2025 and
22 annual discard values for 2004–2025. WGCSE Table 23.2 shows that the
landings series uses official reported totals for 1951–1995 and ICES
estimated landings for 1996–2025; the categories are not interchangeable.
The 2024 and 2025 official country totals in that table are preliminary,
and some individual country entries are incomplete for confidentiality.
Misreporting of areas is a concern and has not been fully quantified.
Landings and discards are context for advice, not a fitted age-specific
likelihood; the advice is calculated from the previous advice and buffer.
Discarding is considered negligible in the current advice.

No population N/F-at-age, recruitment or SSB estimates are produced by the
current rule. The source report notes uncertainty about biological stock
structure and possible connectivity across neighbouring areas. Stock
boundaries have not been revised on that basis. The record's terminal year
is the latest year of reported landings, not a model fitting year.

## Earlier age-based assessment and translation

The 2019 advice used XSA on landings-at-age and a commercial LPUE series
from 7.j to provide relative trends for 7.h–k. The 2020 WKFlatNSCS benchmark
found that the 7.h component was insufficiently represented and moved the
stock to category 5; WGCSE 2026 also notes poor model fits and strong
retrospective revisions. The historical 2019 SAG XML and benchmark report
are cached for further review. Their age-specific inputs and output scales
have not yet been verified or imported; the older model must not be presented
as the 2026 assessment. A translation of the current advice rule into an
age-structured tinyAM fit is not supported, and there is no current official
population trajectory for a dashboard comparison.
