# BSAI arrowtooth flounder: male catalogue entry

## Assessment relationship

The Charbonneau–Keith male entry is a sex-specific label for the Bering
Sea/Aleutian Islands arrowtooth flounder management stock, not an independent
assessment. The selected detailed production model is AFSC Model 18.9 (2022),
recorded as `afsc_arrowtooth_flounder_bsai_2022`; its reported population and
mortality outputs distinguish females and males.

## Database decision

The available official inputs and outputs remain under the canonical stock
`afsc_arrowtooth_flounder_bsai`. Do not duplicate a sex-specific subset as a
separate assessment or tinyAM fit. Associate any tinyAM comparison with the
canonical stock; its source review records the remaining production-input
gaps.
