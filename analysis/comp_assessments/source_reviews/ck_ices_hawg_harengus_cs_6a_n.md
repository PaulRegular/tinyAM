# North of 56°N herring (6.a.N): survey-based harvest-rule assessment

## Current assessment

The Charbonneau–Keith entry `ICES-HAWG_harengus_CS 6a (N)` corresponds to
North Sea autumn-spawning herring in ICES Division 6.a North, stock code
`her.27.6aN`. The 2026 HAWG report assesses it separately from the 6.a South,
7.b–c component using the genetically split Malin Shelf Herring Acoustic
Survey (MSHAS). The method is a constant-harvest-rate (CHR) index assessment,
not an age-structured population model. HAWG notes that terminal recruitment
information is limited and that there is no recruitment index from the
surveys.

The 2026 ICES advice record (assessment key 21308) is marked invalid in the
Advice and Scenarios Database. The 2025 advice record (assessment key 19342;
advice record 3843) is active. The invalidity does not make the 2026 series an
age-structured production run: the published source data provide aggregate
SSB, catch, and relative fishing-pressure indicators rather than fitted
abundance and mortality surfaces by age.

## Database and translation decision

This stock has no recoverable current age-structured assessment to translate
into tinyAM. The aggregate survey and advice indicators are not entered as
age-specific abundance inputs or population outputs. No tinyAM recipe or
dashboard is appropriate. The full HAWG report and associated ICES pages are
cached with checksums under
`source_cache/ck_ices_hawg_harengus_cs6_a_s_7b_c/`; that report covers both
6.a.N and 6.a.S/7.b–c.
