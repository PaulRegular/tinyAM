# Iberian anchovy: historical combined Division 9.a catalogue entry

## Assessment relationship

The Charbonneau–Keith row `ICES-WGHANSA_mordax_SP9a` is a historical combined
Division 9.a entry. The species label *Engraulis mordax* is inconsistent with
the official Iberian stock, *Engraulis encrasicolus*. The 2024 benchmark split
Division 9.a into western (`ane.27.9aW`) and southern (`ane.27.9aS`) stocks.
The current database record `ices_anchovy_gulf_cadiz_2025` represents the
southern stock only; it is not the old combined unit.

## Database decision

Keep the historical combined entry metadata-only. Do not copy southern-stock
values into it or combine them with western-stock data. The current southern
stock has its own documented tinyAM translation; the western successor needs
separate source curation before it can be compared.
