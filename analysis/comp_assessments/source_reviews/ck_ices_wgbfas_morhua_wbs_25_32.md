# Baltic cod: ambiguous WBS 25–32 catalogue entry

## Catalogue identity and assessment boundary

The Charbonneau–Keith Master row is `ICES-WGBFAS_morhua_WBS,25-32` and cites
“BS gadus morhua.pdf”. The label does not establish a separate current stock
or model: ICES identifies the eastern Baltic cod unit in subdivisions 25–32
(with part of that stock also occurring in subdivision 24), while western
Baltic cod is assessed in subdivisions 22–24. The `WBS` prefix conflicts with
the catalogue's 25–32 area text.

The related eastern Baltic catalogue record is
`ck_ices_wgbfas_morhua_bs_25_32`; its source review records the selected
assessment and why its inputs remain unavailable. No distinct official
assessment was verified for this additional WBS-labelled row.

## Database decision

Keep this row metadata-only. Do not copy eastern Baltic data or assign the row
to western Baltic cod based on the inconsistent label. No independent tinyAM
translation or dashboard is supported until an authoritative source resolves
the stock identity.
