# Witch flounder: historical 5Y catalogue label

The Charbonneau–Keith entry `NEFSC-GARMIII_cynoglossus_5Y` names 5Y.
The current NOAA assessment covers a broader Gulf of Maine–Georges Bank and
southern US unit. These labels do not establish identical stock boundaries, so
unit-wide observations and results are not assigned to this 5Y catalogue row.

The separately identified current unit is `nefsc_witch_flounder_unit`. See
[its source review](nefsc_witch_flounder_unit.md) for the 2024 assessment,
the separate 2026 data update, and the relationship and limitations.
