# Witch flounder (5Y): assessment does not support an age-structured translation

## Current official method

The Charbonneau–Keith entry is `NEFSC-GARMIII_cynoglossus_5Y`, for witch
flounder (*Glyptocephalus cynoglossus*) in the Maine–New Hampshire area. The
latest official assessment located for this stock is the 2024 Management Track
update. It uses an empirical approach based on spring and fall survey-biomass
trends. The 2016 analytical VPA and ASAP models were not accepted because of a
strong retrospective pattern. The 2024 work also shows an informational ASAP
sensitivity, but that model was not the method used for management advice.

The 2024 peer-review material reports exploitable biomass of 30,093 tonnes for
2023, calculated from the 2022 fall survey biomass multiplied by 0.9, and an
exploitation rate of 0.034. These are aggregate empirical indicators, not
estimated abundance-at-age, fishing mortality-at-age, recruitment, or SSB
surfaces from an accepted age-structured model. Age observations available for
research do not become likelihood inputs to the empirical assessment solely
because they are age-resolved.

## Database and translation decision

The assessment database is scoped to official age-structured assessments. This
stock's current accepted method does not provide an age-structured population
model or matching age-specific fitted outputs. No assessment inputs or outputs
are added, and no tinyAM recipe or dashboard is appropriate. The rejected or
informational ASAP results must not be presented as the official assessment.
