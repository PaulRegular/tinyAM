# Northeast Arctic haddock, 2026 JRN-AFWG assessment

The existing Charbonneau–Keith record `ck_ices_afwg_aeglefinus_nea_1_2` is Northeast Arctic haddock (`had.27.1-2`). The current detailed production run is the 2026 JRN-AFWG SAM assessment. Survey observations extend through 2026, while catch ends in 2025; the model estimates the population through 2026. The translation therefore uses 1950–2026 and leaves 2026 catch missing.

The ignored cache is `source_cache/ck_ices_afwg_aeglefinus_nea_1_2/assessment_2026/`. `source_manifest.csv` records source URLs, local retrieval date, the stockassessment code revision recorded by the fit, and SHA-256 checksums. It contains the JRN-AFWG report, native SAM run and data objects, configuration, input files and model source. The report is [JRN-AFWG Report 2026, section 4](https://www.hi.no/hi/nettrapporter/imr-vniro-2026-5); the native run and files are hosted in the [NEAHaddock2026 assessment directory](https://www.stockassessment.org/datadisk/stockassessment/userdirs/user3/NEAHaddock2026/). The fitted object identifies stockassessment 0.12.0, `RemoteSha` `1cc464b80f6f`. The report contains detailed model settings; no separate framework year is identified for this annual run.

## Inputs and assumptions

The importer `scripts/database/090_import_jrn_afwg_haddock_2026.R` uses the final SAM object and its original input objects. It also checks transcribed report outputs against `stockassessment::ntable()`, `faytable()`, fixed M, and aggregates recalculated from official N, stock weight and maturity.

- The fit uses the residual catch series `cnWO` as its sole catch fleet. It is stored as 836 annual catch-at-age observations for 1950–2025, ages 3–13+, in thousand fish. The separate `cnW` total catch series is stored as `numbers_at_age_for_M_reconstruction`, not as another catch observation. The supplied source code uses `cnW - cnWO` as removals in its cod-predation M reconstruction.
- Four survey series are recovered as `exp(fit$data$logobs)` on the native SAM scale: RU-BTr-Q4, BS-NoRU-Q1 acoustic, BS-NoRu-Q1 bottom trawl, and FLT007:Eco-NoRu-Q3 bottom trawl. Physical index multipliers are not stated, so the values retain an unresolved native index scale. The native auxiliary table has 69 missing survey cells; these are not observations and are omitted. The 790 non-missing survey records are retained: 678 age-specific rows and 112 grouped terminal-age rows. Thirteen age-specific and two grouped terminal-age observations occur in 2026. The grouped rows are stored as `index_by_age_group`, with their source plus group in notes, and are not sent to tinyAM as single-age observations.
- Survey timing is 0.95 for RU-BTr-Q4, 0.133 for both Q1 surveys, and 0.7 for the Q3 ecosystem survey. The saved SAM observation-weight vector is missing throughout. The report lists prior weight 1, but its connection to the likelihood is unresolved; no weight is invented. All four survey likelihoods are lognormal and AR(1)-correlated across observed ages.
- Catch, discard and landings weights, landings fractions, beginning-of-year stock weight, maturity, and pre-spawning F/M fractions come from the final SAM data object. Stock weights and maturity cover 1950–2026, ages 3–13+; pre-spawning fractions are zero. Source units are kg per fish, proportions, and per year as appropriate.
- M is fixed in the final fit. Ages 3–6 contain 0.2 plus the reconstructed cod-predation component; ages 7–13+ are fixed at 0.2. The source script reconstructs predation mortality for 1984–2025, uses its age-specific mean for 1950–1983, and copies 2025 values to 2026. The final `fit$data$natMor` surface is retained as the authoritative fixed M input, and the total-catch series used by the reconstruction is separately retained.
- The model uses ages 3–13+, age-3 recruitment with a random walk, a log-N state process, and annual F states with AR(1) cross-age correlation. F states are separate through age 7, with ages 8–13+ sharing the age-8 state. F process SD has a separate age-3 value and a shared older value. Catch observation variance separates age 3 from ages 4+; each survey has its own observation-variance group. Survey q includes age-shared groups and density-dependent q-power terms. TinyAM can preserve the documented q level-sharing approximately, but it does not reproduce q-power density dependence or the survey residual AR(1) structure. Its N initialization and latent-state treatment also differ from SAM.

## Outputs and limits

Report Tables 4.12–4.14 give F-at-age through 2025, N-at-age through 2026, and fixed M-at-age through 2025. Table 4.11 gives age-3 recruitment, SSB, Fbar ages 4–7 and total stock biomass. N and recruitment are in thousand fish, biomass in tonnes, and F/M in per year. The 2026 row is retained for reported N, recruitment and biomass; Fbar is not reported for 2026. Age-specific uncertainty is unavailable. Table 4.11 Low/High bounds are retained without a confidence-level label because the report does not specify their coverage.

The importer verifies that the report's N/F/M tables match the final native fit. It also verifies that summing official N times the source stock weights and maturity reproduces reported SSB and that the corresponding total biomass and Fbar ages 4–7 reproduce Table 4.11 within report rounding. This confirms the tabulation and definitions; it does not make fixed M an estimated output or establish that tinyAM will match the SAM fit.

Canonical completeness is `inputs_status = complete`, `assumptions_status = partial`, and `outputs_status = partial`. The numerical native inputs and primary outputs are recovered, while index physical units, the meaning of the printed prior observation weights, some uncertainty definitions, and age-specific uncertainty remain unresolved or unavailable.
