# Eastern Bering Sea northern rock sole: male catalogue entry

## Assessment relationship

The Charbonneau–Keith male entry is a sex-specific label for the Eastern
Bering Sea northern rock sole management stock, not a separate assessment.
The current detailed run is AFSC Model 24.2 (2024), recorded as
`afsc_northern_rock_sole_ebs_2024`. It estimates both sexes jointly and reports
sex-specific population, mortality, and biomass quantities.

## Database decision

The production inputs and outputs remain under the canonical stock
`afsc_northern_rock_sole_ebs`, where sex is retained as a data dimension. Do
not copy the male rows into this catalogue-only stock or describe them as an
independent fit. Associate any tinyAM comparison with the canonical stock.
