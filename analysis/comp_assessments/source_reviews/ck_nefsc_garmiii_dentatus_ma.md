# NEFSC summer flounder catalogue entry (area MA)

## Identity and current assessment

The metadata-only record `ck_nefsc_garmiii_dentatus_ma` has Charbonneau–Keith
identifier `NEFSC-GARMIII_dentatus_MA`. Its catalogue source label is
“North Car Paralichthys dentatus.pdf”, while the identifier ends in `MA`.
Those labels do not establish a separate Massachusetts or Mid-Atlantic stock
boundary.

NOAA's current summer flounder assessment is a coastwide stock extending from
Maine to North Carolina. The canonical database already represents this stock
as `nefsc_summer_flounder`, with the 2018 detailed assessment. The 2025
management-track assessment is newer, but its public materials do not provide
the detailed age inputs and fitted age surfaces needed to replace the 2018
record. See [the existing summer flounder source review](nefsc_summer_flounder.md)
for the assessment selection and its source details.

## Database decision

No independent official assessment was found for the catalogue's `MA` area.
The 2018 coastwide inputs and outputs are not copied to this row, and the
2025 summary is not used to fill the age-specific gaps. Keep this catalogue
entry metadata-only until an authoritative source establishes its boundary
and a distinct assessment run. No tinyAM translation or dashboard is
appropriate for this entry at present.
