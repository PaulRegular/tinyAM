# Bothnian Sea herring: historical Subdivision 30 catalogue entry

## Assessment relationship

The Charbonneau–Keith entry describes the former Subdivision 30 unit. The
current WGBFAS production assessment is a single combined-stock model for
subdivisions 30 and 31 (`her.27.3031`), represented by
`ices_herring_gulf_bothnia_2026`. The current report, source data and database
record do not provide a separate fitted assessment for Subdivision 30.

## Database decision

Keep this historical component metadata-only. The combined 30–31 inputs and
outputs cannot be copied into a Subdivision 30 assessment without inventing a
spatial split. Any tinyAM translation or comparison belongs to the combined
stock record.
