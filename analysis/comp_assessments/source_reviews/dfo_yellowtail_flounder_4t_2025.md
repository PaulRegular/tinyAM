# NAFO 4T yellowtail flounder: source screen

## Identity and current assessment

DFO's 2025 production assessment treats NAFO Division 4T yellowtail flounder
(*Myzopsetta ferruginea*, also reported under the synonym *Limanda ferruginea*)
as a single biological unit in the southern Gulf of St. Lawrence. The accepted
assessment is age-structured and state-space. Its detailed Research Document
2026/054 supports the Science Advisory Report 2026/022. The current unit is
distinct from the existing Georges Bank and Southern New England/Mid-Atlantic
yellowtail records; there is no canonical DFO 4T record to match.

## Inputs and outputs identified

The 2025 model uses total fishery catch and numbers-at-age proportions by seine
and trawl fleets for 1985-2022. Its RV survey inputs for 1985-2025 include
numbers-at-age proportions, an age-aggregated biomass index, maturity-at-age
and weight-at-age. The official assessment notes updated historical age-length
keys and a 2021 survey vessel/gear change, with conversion factors applied to
maintain the time series.

The official report gives 2025 age-5+ biomass as 8,269 t, and reports age-1
recruitment, mean fishing mortality for ages 4-10+, and annual mean natural
mortality with age-specific time variation. The reported M groups include ages
4-7, 8-9 and 10+. Historical trajectories are shown, but exact age grids,
biological vectors, model timing, complete numeric output series and their
uncertainty are not yet extracted.

Catch-at-age counts and age-specific survey abundance are not direct observation
series in the summary description. The model's catch totals, number-at-age
proportions and weight-at-age support a documented downstream catch
reconstruction. The aggregate survey biomass, survey age proportions and
weights-at-age similarly support reconstruction of age-specific abundance,
after the detailed report's age coverage and scaling are confirmed. Preserve
the native totals, compositions and aggregate index in the canonical database;
do not replace them with these translated series.

## Source access and screening

Official catalogue records confirm that Research Document 2026/054 and Science
Advisory Report 2026/022 are public. Their PDF download URLs redirected to the
Government Publications archive notice on 2026-10-29, so the reports could not
be cached or inspected in full during this screen. The official catalogue HTML
records are cached in the gitignored source cache; the failed PDF downloads are
listed there separately. The 2025/010 indicator update was used only to confirm
that it is an interim update, not as a substitute for the 2025 assessment.

This candidate is screened eligible for source curation because the official
assessment documents age compositions, age-specific biology and comparable
population/mortality outputs, with source-supported reconstruction available
for the required observation scales. Import remains not started until the
detailed report is accessible and numeric source tables, transformations,
timing and uncertainty are verified.
