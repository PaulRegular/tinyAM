# Caribbean redtail parrotfish: NOAA Stock SMART 10144

**Review date:** 2026-10-30  
**Scientific name:** *Sparisoma chrysopterum*  
**NOAA Stock SMART ID:** `10144`  
**Assessment:** SEDAR 26

## Unit and latest assessment

NOAA Stock SMART lists one Caribbean redtail parrotfish management unit and
shows an assessment year of 2012. The latest completed redtail-specific detailed
assessment located is SEDAR 26, whose final report is dated 2011. It discusses
regional components in Puerto Rico, St. Thomas/St. John, and St. Croix; these
components are not counted as three additional candidate units.

SEDAR 103 is developing and applying alternative methods for Caribbean
island-based stocks. As of this review, its development workshop had taken place
in August 2026, with application webinars and review still scheduled. It is not
a completed redtail stock-status assessment.

## Inputs and methods

The SEDAR 26 report says catch-at-age/length was not developed for Puerto Rico
redtail parrotfish. It also reports no commercial catch-at-age in the U.S.
Virgin Islands and no recreational catch-at-age data there. The assessment's
Trip Interview Program data are length-frequency samples, not age compositions
or catch numbers-at-age.

The report identifies no species-specific standardized abundance index. Its
quantitative methods are data-limited: fishery CPUE where available, a
length-based Beverton-Holt total-mortality estimator, and the length-based
SEINE method. Puerto Rico CPUE was unavailable because reported parrotfish
landings were not identified to species. U.S. Virgin Islands CPUE likewise
could not be separated by species. The report does not use an age-specific
survey index or a complete weight-at-age or maturity-at-age surface. It notes
uncertainty in life-history inputs, including growth, and does not estimate
species-specific natural mortality-at-age.

## Outputs and screening

The report concludes there was no evidence of overfishing in the U.S.
Caribbean, while overfished status was unknown. It reports qualitative and
length-based findings, not annual numeric N-, F-, M-, or SSB-at-age series. The
review panel noted convergence problems for the Puerto Rico SEINE model and
considered its absolute total-mortality estimate unreliable for management.

This unit is screened **ineligible** for the current age-disaggregated set:
the production assessment lacks catch-at-age and age-specific abundance
inputs. The assessment outputs are not a like-for-like age-structured
comparison target. No canonical database records or tinyAM translation were
added.
