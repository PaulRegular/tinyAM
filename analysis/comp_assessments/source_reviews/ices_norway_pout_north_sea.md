# Norway pout source review

## Assessment identified

ICES assessed Norway pout (*Trisopterus esmarkii*) in Subarea 4 and Division 3.a
under stock code `nop.27.3a4`. The latest advice currently listed is the 2025
assessment, key 21230, with fitted data through 2025. The [2025 advice and
source table](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.advice.27202770) report recruitment
at age 0, F for ages 1–2, SSB, total biomass, and catches.

The [2026 WKBWNG benchmark report](https://doi.org/10.17895/ices.pub.32019630)
selected a revised February 2026 quarterly SESAM assessment. The 2026 WGNSSK
report says no spring advice was issued for Norway pout, so the benchmark model
and the 2025 advice run are distinct assessment products. The benchmark report
states that catch-at-age and catch weights are the same as in the September
2025 update, covering 1984–2025. It also describes constant maturity at age
(0%, 20%, and 100% for ages 0, 1, and 2+), revised quarterly survey indices,
and time-varying quarterly natural mortality based on the North Sea SMS model.

## Remaining gap

The benchmark is not ready for a canonical numerical record or tinyAM fit. The
report's age-by-quarter input tables are not available as text in the retrieved
document, and the supporting [Norway pout working documents](https://ices-library.figshare.com/articles/report/Benchmark_Workshop_on_Whiting_Norway_Pout_and_Golden_Redfish_WKBWNG_/32019630)
are needed to recover the processed survey indices and time-varying mortality.
The working documents and report could not be downloaded into the local
gitignored source cache in this environment because the file endpoint rejects
the available download connection. Only the report metadata and searchable
text were accessible.

The 2025 Stock Assessment Graphs values cannot substitute for the 2026
benchmark's fitted outputs, and the older 2025 model inputs should not be
combined with benchmark settings. No partial numerical record or translation
has been created. This is a source retrieval and extraction blocker, not a
claim that the detailed information is unavailable publicly.
