# Caribbean red hind: NOAA Stock SMART 10327

**Review date:** 2026-10-31  
**Scientific name:** *Epinephelus guttatus*  
**NOAA Stock SMART ID:** `10327`  
**Latest detailed red-hind assessment located:** SEDAR 35 (2014)

## Unit and assessment history

Stock SMART lists Caribbean red hind as one management unit. SEDAR 35 is the
latest completed red-hind-specific stock assessment located. The report covers
the U.S. Caribbean, including Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands.

The later SEDAR 46 data-limited assessment considered Caribbean candidate
species-island units and assessed six other units; red hind was not among the
assessed stocks. SEDAR 91's 2025 final products concern U.S. Caribbean spiny
lobster in Puerto Rico, St. Croix, and St. Thomas/St. John. They do not update
the red hind assessment. SEDAR 103 is a Caribbean alternate-methods development
and application process; its application webinars and review are scheduled for
2026–2027, so it is not a completed red-hind assessment used for advice.

## Inputs and methods

SEDAR 35 is data-limited and length-based. It uses fishery landings, effort,
length-frequency observations, and fishery CPUE. A relative abundance index is
also reported from the SEAMAP-C survey in southwest Puerto Rico, along with
spawning-aggregation density information. These are aggregate indices, not
age-specific abundance observations. The report does not provide catch
numbers-at-age or a catch-at-age likelihood.

The assessment estimates total mortality from mean length and derives fishing
mortality as total mortality minus natural mortality. Natural mortality is
based on maximum-age information. Age 3 knife-edge maturity and life-history
inputs are used for per-recruit reference-point calculations; these do not
constitute annual age-specific biology surfaces for an age-structured
population time series. The reviewed sources do not supply the required
annual weight-at-age and maturity-at-age inputs for such a model.

## Outputs and screening

The report contains aggregate CPUE and survey indices, length-based mortality
estimates, and per-recruit reference points. It does not report annual
abundance-at-age, fishing mortality-at-age, estimated natural mortality-at-age,
recruitment, or spawning-biomass series that could be compared with a
tinyAM age-structured fit.

This candidate is screened **ineligible** for the current age-disaggregated
comparison set. The source assessment is not age structured and lacks the
required age-specific observations and biological surfaces. No canonical stock,
assessment, input, or output records and no tinyAM translation were added.
