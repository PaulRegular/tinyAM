## Expanded-library review

### tinyAM translation

The source TEP index cannot be passed faithfully to tinyAM's age-specific index likelihood: tinyAM does not calculate the source's aggregate fecundity-weighted prediction. The translation excludes TEP rather than assigning it invented ages or treating eggs as fish biomass. The translator explicitly selects no survey and uses an empty index table, with no unused q or index-SD parameters. It fits the unchanged catch-at-age values with a direct observation likelihood, which is a substantial likelihood approximation, and retains the source fixed M and annual stock weight and maturity surfaces. It uses Beverton-Holt recruitment with IID recruitment deviations, IID survival residuals, and an age-correlated random walk for F; the recruitment and survival process forms are close to the source, while the F correlation approximates the source's shared annual F change without imposing its exact selectivity pattern. The source's separate F- and M-before-spawning fractions are not applied because tinyAM's single spawning-time adjustment uses combined Z. The common-definition SSB comparison instead derives beginning-of-year spawning biomass from source N-at-age with the same weight and maturity surfaces used in tinyAM; this derived comparison has no uncertainty. The resulting catch-only model omits the assessment's independent abundance information and may have weakly identified population scale and F, so it is a structural approximation rather than a close likelihood replication.

The translation recipe and focused preparation test are included. The catch-only model converged with package-default initialization and no `start_par`: optimizer code 0 (`relative convergence (4)`), objective 823.392, maximum absolute gradient 0.000584, successful standard-error calculation, and a positive-definite Hessian. It has 8 fixed effects and 1,130 random effects. The fit warns that the stock-recruit curve is weakly estimated and the F-increment age correlation is near its boundary.

The dashboard is cached locally at `results/cache/dfo_northern_mackerel_2025/dashboard.html`. Agreement is poor for this substantial structural approximation: mean absolute percent differences are 57.5% for F, 52.1% for N-at-age, 45.6% for total abundance, 48.0% for recruitment, 46.2% for biomass and 46.7% for SSB. Terminal differences range from -91.2% for N-at-age to +111.4% for F; the total-abundance trend correlation is 0.875. The separate TEP index—the assessment's independent egg-production information—is omitted because tinyAM cannot represent its aggregate fecundity-weighted prediction. Fixed M agreement is by construction. Poor agreement does not justify changing the source observations or adding unsupported likelihood structure; retain the result as a clearly limited catch-only approximation.
