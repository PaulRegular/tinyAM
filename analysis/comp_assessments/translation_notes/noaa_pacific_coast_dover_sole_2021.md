# West Coast Dover sole: translation blocked at source readiness

The preliminary recipe is registered but does not create observations or fit a model. The 2021 assessment's removals include landings and discards, while the available catch-age samples describe retained fish. Discard age composition is not available. The recorded mean-body-weight observations are discard-sample summaries, not age-specific retained-catch weights. The SS3-generated weight-at-age surface is not used to convert catch biomass into observations.

The WCGBTS age summary can be derived from conditional age-at-length and length data, but this does not supply the missing catch reconstruction. Reopen the translation if compatible source catch weights and discard age information are recovered.
