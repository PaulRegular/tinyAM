# Irish Sea herring: annual-reference check needed

No model has been fitted. The preliminary recipe stops before fitting because
the source annual origin needs verification. Catch-year labels described as
April–March and a January-to-September spawning-biomass definition do not
establish a common population-state origin. Relabelling these inputs as
calendar years would conceal that uncertainty. September spawning is known;
its fraction within the appropriate model year remains to be established.

The source-only finding is documented in SAIO's Irish Sea herring review.
The age-specific ISAS index is potentially usable once dates are resolved;
the aggregate ISSS spawning index has no matching age compositions. No
observations, timing fractions or source-based starts have been manufactured.
