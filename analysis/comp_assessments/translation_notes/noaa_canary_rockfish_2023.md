# Pacific coast Canary Rockfish: translation blocked at source readiness

The preliminary recipe is registered but does not create observations or fit a model. Native fishery age samples are conditional on length, but the recorded length-bin keys have not been verified well enough to reconstruct marginal age observations and their sampling weights. An active recreational catch fleet also lacks age samples, and compatible catch weights at age are unavailable. SS3-generated biology is not used as input data.

Native spawning output is in eggs rather than SSB. Reopen the translation if the length-bin mapping, catch-age coverage and catch-weight information can be verified from source inputs.
