# Oregon Black Rockfish: translation blocked at source readiness

The preliminary recipe is registered but does not create observations or fit a model. The Oregon shore fleet has positive removals but no age or length samples. Other catch-age inputs are conditional on length, compatible catch weights at age are unavailable, and age-specific survey observations are not reported.

The assessment's maturity-times-fecundity surface is reproductive output, not a maturity proportion. SS3-generated biological surfaces are not substituted for missing observations.
