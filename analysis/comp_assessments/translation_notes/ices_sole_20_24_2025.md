## Expanded-library review

### tinyAM translation and fit

The translation fits 1984–2024 at ages 1–9+, preserves the source survey names,
timings, q-sharing groups, weights, maturity and fixed M, and omits only the 18
blank Q4 index cells from the likelihood. It uses IID cohort-survival
deviations, random-walk recruitment, fixed M, and separate observation SDs by
index. The closest correlated random-walk F process and an independent F
random-walk check both failed from package-default starts with an NA/NaN
gradient. No source starts were supplied. The final, documented approximation
uses a stationary AR1 F process over years and ages with one mean level; this is
less like the source's accumulated random walk, but it converges without custom
initialization.

The final fit converged with optimizer code 0, objective 485.4934, maximum
absolute gradient 0.000805, a positive-definite Hessian, successful SD
estimation, 23 fixed effects and 729 random effects. Residual checks flag some
age/series residual spreads outside 0.5–1.5, residuals above 3 SD, and strong
adjacent-year residual correlations in some groups.

Over 1984–2024, the common-definition comparisons are: SSB 7.5% mean absolute
percent difference, +11.2% terminal difference and 0.978 trend correlation;
recruitment 17.0%, +17.1% and 0.919; total abundance 9.8%, +11.3% and 0.967;
N-at-age 15.0%, +10.1% and 0.966; F-at-age 16.7%, −13.3% and 0.902; Fbar for
ages 4–8 8.5%, −11.8% and 0.948; and total biomass 8.4%, +10.7% and 0.970.
SSB and total biomass are approximate because tinyAM treats the reported stock
weights as fixed, while SAM estimates a weight process. Fbar is approximate
because the source report does not clarify whether its averaging weights
match tinyAM's abundance-weighted calculation. M and Mbar are fixed-input
consistency checks, not estimated-M comparisons. Total abundance is derived
from official N-at-age and has no source uncertainty interval.

The local fit and exported CSV diagnostics are under the gitignored
`results/cache/ices_sole_20_24_2025/` directory.
