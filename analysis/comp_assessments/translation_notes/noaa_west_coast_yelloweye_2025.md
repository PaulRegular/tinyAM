# West Coast yelloweye rockfish: translation blocked at source readiness

The preliminary recipe is registered but does not create observations or fit a model. The active California trawl removals have no age samples, and one numbers-based recreational catch stream has an unresolved multiplier. Conditional age-at-length samples do not supply the missing catch ages. Compatible catch weights at age are not available, and SS3-generated weight surfaces are not used as observations.

The assessment contains California and Oregon/Washington components, while native spawning output is reported in eggs rather than SSB. These would require explicit comparison decisions after the input gaps are addressed.
